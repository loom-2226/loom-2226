# ARP v1.0 Future Qualification Sequence

This is a plan only. No campaign below is executed by ARP v1.0 build work.

1. **ARP-QUAL-01 — CERES:** independently reconstruct the empirical envelope and compare against the committed HCQ-01 v0.3-R1 gold fixture.
2. **ARP-QUAL-02 — EUROPA:** remote sensing, radiation environment, and competing interior interpretations.
3. **ARP-QUAL-03 — MARS:** heterogeneous multi-mission coverage and historical evidence progression.
4. **ARP-QUAL-04 — 67P/CHURYUMOV-GERASIMENKO:** irregular-body geometry and time-dependent activity.
5. **ARP-QUAL-05 — JUPITER:** gas-giant evidence ontology and atmospheric/interior model separation.
6. **ARP-QUAL-06 — POORLY CHARACTERIZED ASTEROID:** sparse honest authority rather than fabricated completeness.

Each qualification must be a separately governed campaign. The sequence is
intended to qualify the research machine across evidence regimes before any
full-Atlas autonomy. A future campaign must reuse existing coverage first and
must not treat a gold fixture as permission to add facts.
