## 7. Amendment protocol

A post-freeze change must state: (1) affected canonical claim(s), (2) evidence/reason for change, (3) whether it is a correction, refinement or deliberate retcon, (4) downstream documents/data touched, (5) migration/supersession notes, and (6) new version number/date. OPEN and PROVISIONAL fields may be refined without pretending the previous uncertainty never existed. Locked structural changes require an explicit canon amendment.
