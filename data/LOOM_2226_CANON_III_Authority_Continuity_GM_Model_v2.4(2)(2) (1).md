# 8. Xenoarchaeology truth-state and GM guardrails

This section governs what is objectively true in the setting, what is publicly known in 2226, what is restricted, and what remains deliberately unanswered.

## 8.1 Objective setting facts at campaign start

The following are objective setting facts, not campaign-specific discoveries:

- 1I/ʻOumuamua was an artificial Builder spacecraft or spacecraft-derived artifact.
- Artificiality was publicly verified in 2066; humans physically accessed the object in 2125.
- The Builders were embodied, carbon/water, multicellular organisms requiring obligate symbiotic biology plus manufactured developmental cognitive substrate.
- The Nursery existed and its biological hereditary systems were deliberately rendered unreconstructible.
- The coherent original artifact was lost through the Access Cascade by 2226; only finite samples, debris and recordings survive.
- The Tau Ceti Reef site and Epsilon Eridani Kite site belong to independent technological lineages distinct from the Builders and from each other.
- Exactly three technological lineages are verified by 2226; no living alien technological person or civilization is verified.
- All three show substantial late continuity/mobility reallocation sufficient to establish TMP as a comparative archaeological category.
- No common Great Filter cause is objectively established.

## 8.2 Restricted Builder truth ladder

### Public / specialist truth

- Builder obligate multi-lineage symbiosis is real.
- Manufactured developmental substrates directly participated in normal neural development.
- The biological First Erasure was deliberate and preceded the final catastrophic propulsion event.
- The 1I propulsion/control corpus materially redirected the human superheavy research search space.

### Black truth

- individualized higher-order synthetic developmental state was deliberately sanitized rather than merely lost to random degradation;
- surviving low-level architecture and state-accounting metadata establish that person-specific state once occupied physically surviving resources.

### Deep Black strong inference

- biological and synthetic sanitization were one coordinated event or coordinated multi-stage process;
- the most comprehensively destroyed information appears to include mappings associating particular biological developmental state with particular synthetic cognitive state;
- **Terminal Identity Denial** is the best forensic description of the outcome: preventing reconstruction of integrated persons while leaving substantial non-identity-bearing matter and generic architecture intact.

These are not equivalent to knowing motive.

## 8.3 Questions the GM model deliberately does not answer

The GM must **not** silently invent a hidden canonical answer to any of the following merely to simplify a plot:

- who initiated Builder TID;
- whether occupants consented;
- whether TID was defensive, ethical, coercive, automatic or hostile;
- whether the final Builder drive catastrophe was intentional;
- whether the Access Cascade was deliberately designed;
- Builder home system and self-name;
- exact Builder morphology;
- whether Reef or Kite descendants survive;
- whether TMP has one common cause;
- whether a universal Great Filter exists;
- whether any TMP cause is related to AI, germline modification, Mc, Loom, synthetic personhood or High Strangeness.

A campaign may earn a local resolution to one of these as **Campaign Truth**, but it does not automatically rewrite setting-wide continuity.

## 8.4 High-Strangeness causal firewall

Alien archaeology is not a privileged input to the High-Strangeness state model.

No Nursery archive, TID event, TMP site, Mc forensic sample or Builder ruin contributes to H/X/Window/Flap state merely because it concerns aliens, identity, death, information or advanced physics.

Unsupported shortcuts include:

- TID prevents relational tracking;
- Mc makes humanity visible;
- Builder ruins are automatically Windows;
- TMP is an M1/M2 predator response;
- Access Cascade is automatically a High-Strangeness event;
- Black Nursery evidence explains TAAM/TAAC.

Any archaeological anomaly must independently earn its evidence state.

## 8.5 Future GM-only comparative lineages

Two additional lineages are frozen as future comparative material but are **not known to humanity on 21 August 2226**.

### Lattice lineage — Epsilon Indi

- mature machine-substrate person civilization descended from earlier biological intelligence;
- terminal horizon roughly **0.6–1.2 Ma**;
- explicit lineage/fork/continuity archives across long periods;
- late selective deletion of identity-continuity records while autonomous outbound nodes are produced;
- deletion is a disturbing TID analogue but not canonically the same motive or mechanism;
- future discovery should intensify the Black Nursery/personhood debate without proving a common Filter.

### Mason lineage — 61 Cygni

- high-gravity biological civilization with deep subsurface cities, ceramics, geothermal engineering and planetary-scale infrastructure;
- terminal horizon roughly **3–6 Ma**;
- no clear terminal mobilization, no identity erasure and no large escape architecture;
- civilization ceases in place through an unresolved cause;
- this lineage is an explicit comparative counterexample preventing TMP from becoming a universal scripted law.

## 8.6 Future-xeno design rule

Future technological lineages must be generated in this order:

1. biology and environment;
2. personhood/cognitive architecture;
3. normal engineering path;
4. mature civilization;
5. only then terminal archaeology.

Do not begin by choosing how the civilization “responded to the Filter.” At least some future cases must fail to fit TMP.

## 8.7 Builder source constraint

The Builder source system remains unknown. GM material may use a broad local source corridor and competing candidate systems, but no conveniently early human destination may be promoted as the Builder homeworld without a dedicated future derivation and canon amendment.

- route direction may differ operationally because endpoint geometry, measurement quality, solution margin, hardware compatibility, or environmental burden differ.

This graph is therefore **not a map of wormholes** and is **not a nearest-neighbor stellar ladder**.

## 2. Generation doctrine

### 2.1 Node selection

The first hidden neighborhood uses real nearby stellar systems familiar to Solar astronomy. Exact stellar/planetary properties remain subject to the separate Extrasolar Astronomy Register.

Twenty-eight nodes are included. They are intentionally heterogeneous: bright nearby stars, red dwarfs, multiple systems, known planet hosts, and apparently unremarkable systems.

### 2.2 Edge classes

The hidden physical affinity of an edge is represented by a non-public class:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | unusually clean relational affinity; broad engineering margin after characterization |
| **B** | strong/useful affinity; likely operationally valuable |
| **C** | workable but demanding; sensitive to endpoint or hardware quality |
| **D** | weak relationship; generally unattractive except for probes/special missions |
| **T** | trace/candidate affinity; physically real in the model but difficult to distinguish from noise remotely |
| **Ø** | no useful direct relationship in the modeled operating envelope |

These are **physical-affinity classes**, not public route certifications.

### 2.3 Directional modifiers

A physical edge can carry endpoint modifiers:

- `↔` roughly comparable engineering burden both directions
- `→` easier in the written direction
- `←` easier opposite the written direction
- `!` unusually sensitive to local endpoint state/environment
- `?` difficult to infer remotely

Directional modifiers do not make fundamental physics one-way. They represent asymmetric engineering access to the same underlying relation.

### 2.4 Anti-convenience constraints

The graph was constructed to obey the following structural rules:

1. Sol is important but **not the best-connected node**.
2. Alpha Centauri is a strong early junction but **not a mystical nexus**.
3. At least one very nearby star is a poor strategic route.
4. At least one farther star is relationally closer than nearer alternatives.
5. At least one attractive astronomical destination is topologically inconvenient.
6. At least one apparently mediocre system is a major hidden junction.
7. At least one branch is difficult to infer from Sol and becomes obvious only after destination-side measurement.
8. Dead ends and low-value branches exist.
9. No compact object automatically receives superior connectivity.
10. The graph contains no edge whose sole justification is a desired future plot.

# 10. Economic consequences of the frozen graph

## 10.1 Alpha's value is temporary, not guaranteed monopoly

Alpha has first-mover infrastructure and excellent initial connectivity, but the graph prevents it from becoming the permanent unavoidable capital of interstellar civilization.

Once Epsilon Indi and 70 Ophiuchi are discovered, network centrality begins to diffuse.

That is desirable. Geography evolves without requiring topology to change.

## 10.2 Epsilon Indi creates a route-information shock

The discovery of the Epsilon Eridani–Epsilon Indi A-class edge should materially reprice:

- Epsilon Eridani infrastructure;
- Alpha packet/freight scheduling;
- route-survey firms;
- frontier land/resource options, where legally recognized;
- Loom hull availability;
- insurance capacity;
- strategic government interest.

The valuable discovery is not "Epsilon Indi has ore." It is "Epsilon Indi sees another part of the graph."

## 10.3 Barnard produces the first route-speculation loss

Because Barnard is near, historically famous, and accessible early, investors plausibly overestimate its future junction value.

The hidden graph says they are wrong.

This supplies a clean physical basis for part of the early Loom-market correction already proposed in the frontier-economy baseline.

## 10.4 70 Ophiuchi demonstrates pure network geography

If later astronomy finds 70 Ophiuchi materially ordinary, that is preferable rather than problematic.

Its eventual importance comes from **betweenness**, not miraculous resources.

Stations, depots, packet exchanges, rescue assets, insurers, and route-analysis teams can create a city where intrinsic planetary economics alone would not.

# 11. Campaign integrity rules

The following rules apply once v0.1 is accepted as the hidden baseline:

1. Do not add a direct Sol edge to solve a travel problem.
2. Do not upgrade an edge because a planet later proves attractive.
3. Do not downgrade an edge because a destination becomes politically inconvenient.
4. Discovery dates may change; physical edges may not, absent mechanics revision.
5. Operational certification may improve substantially with knowledge and hardware even when physical affinity class stays fixed.
6. Political borders may redirect traffic without changing topology.
7. A secret route must correspond to an existing hidden edge.
8. Lost expeditions do not imply that an edge disappeared.
9. New systems beyond this 28-node neighborhood may be generated later using the same doctrine, but their connection to the frozen neighborhood must be generated **before** they are needed by a story.
10. The hidden graph is allowed to be inconvenient.

# 14. Integrity statement

The v0.1 graph intentionally produces a universe in which:

- Alpha Centauri matters, but is not cosmically privileged;
- Barnard's Star is accessible but strategically disappointing;
- Epsilon Eridani opens a route nobody could infer properly from Sol;

> **The map humanity sees is not the map reality contains.**
