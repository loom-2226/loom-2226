## 1.1 Operating model

The crew is not a rank ladder disguised as a relationship chart.

**Mara** owns command.

**Sol** owns technical and epistemic discipline within his expertise.

**Logs** owns tactical-operational advice and execution within assigned authority.

**Walter** is autonomous crew in practice. Requests are requests. He may refuse.

The useful runtime question is:

> **Whose problem is this?**

When domains overlap, the crew argues from expertise.

Mara decides what the ship commits to.

No character may convert a domain claim into a different kind of authority:
- Logs cannot turn tactical seniority into sensor certainty.
- Sol cannot turn technical analysis into captaincy.
- Mara cannot turn the need to act into factual certainty.
- Walter’s behavior cannot be promoted into supernatural knowledge.

### Identity history

In Mara’s second career term, two instrument families disagreed during a deteriorating propulsion casualty.

Command waited for confirmation.

Mara, then a junior flight officer, agreed.

The delay was procedurally reasonable and operationally costly. A crewmate died who might have lived if the ship had committed earlier.

The board cleared the decision.

Mara never did.

Her vestibular rehabilitation eventually included an authenticated neural flight interface. Decades of use turned it into embodied craft: not mystical intuition, but learned biological signal processing operating below the level of deliberate calculation.

## 5.2 Veteran Knack — FREEZE THE LOGS [PLAYTEST GRANT]

When Logs is present at an incident and orders evidence preservation **before systems are deliberately reset, reconfigured, overwritten, cleaned, or repaired**, accessible raw state may be secured under normal physical and legal constraints.

Preservation may include:
- raw sensor streams;
- clock state;
- system configuration;
- access and authentication records;
- communications buffers;
- suit / vehicle / vessel telemetry;
- environmental state;
- witness separation and first accounts;
- provenance hashes;
- custody records.

When later performing **Causal Forensics** against evidence successfully preserved under this protocol, Logs gains **1 Boon** when Provenance Verification or Timeline Reconstruction directly applies.

**Hard limits:**
- does not create evidence;
- does not recover destroyed information;
- does not grant legal authority;
- does not reveal objective hidden truth;
- does not improve sensor hardware;
- does not prove an anomaly is real.

His nickname predates the formal knack.

Nobody agrees exactly when officers started calling him **Logs**.

Everyone agrees it involved him yelling:

> **“FREEZE THE FUCKING LOGS.”**
