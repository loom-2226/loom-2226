# 1. Document Control & Canon Rules

This is a design-bible document, not an in-universe encyclopedia. It records the setting’s intended simulation rules, the beliefs of people living in 2226, and the mysteries that even the designers deliberately refuse to settle. Those are different epistemic categories and must stay different.

## 1.1 Canon hierarchy

- Locked canon outranks working or provisional canon. Later explicit revisions outrank this document.

- Open questions stay open. A future GM, assistant, or collaborator must preserve ambiguity rather than silently filling gaps.

- GM-only deep models exist to keep the simulation coherent; they are not automatically true in-universe theories and are not player knowledge.

- Hidden cosmological possibilities are not to be leaked simply because the design document acknowledges them.

- Generated concept-art microtext, exact masses, capacities, class names, operator names, and labels are illustrative unless repeated in the textual canon.

- The simulation never changes objective reality to reward genre recognition. Mundane, fraudulent, human, technological, biological, astronomical, and genuinely anomalous explanations can coexist.

## 1.3 Simulation truth vs. human belief

The engine distinguishes at least four layers: objective simulated state; what instruments and records can physically establish; what institutions and individuals believe; and what the player has actually learned. A correct theory does not become player knowledge merely because the GM knows it. An incorrect in-world theory remains real as culture, policy, and motivation.

A campaign may resolve a question that remains intentionally unresolved in the setting bible. Once play causally establishes that answer, it becomes **Campaign Truth** for that continuity. It does not silently become universal canon. Conversely, an intentionally unresolved setting question does not prevent a campaign from discovering a concrete answer when doing so creates a stronger causal world.

| **GM RULE** Simulation knows reality. The player knows only what could have reached them through sensors, testimony, records, inference, and physically carried information. Campaign discoveries stay inside their campaign unless explicitly promoted into setting-wide canon. |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## 1.4 What this setting is trying to feel like

The baseline experience is grounded, tactile, technically legible spacefaring: fuel, heat, reaction mass, maintenance, orbital geography, sensor uncertainty, money, crew competence, latency, and institutional friction. Cosmic mystery only works because the ordinary universe is coherent enough to trust first.

- Elite Dangerous-scale awe and navigational freedom, but with stronger causal engineering and a smaller, more intimate ship.

- The Expanse for within-system logistics, acceleration, danger, crew scale, and politics.

- Dune / Event Horizon conceptual influence for FTL: not “go faster through normal space,” but a translation event through deeper relational structure.

- Solaris / Arrival influence for contact and cognition: the alien, if encountered, need not be a humanoid adversary or a puzzle designed for humans.

- Cosmic horror as ontological threat, not a parade of monsters or “Ancients did everything.”

- A simulation in which physics generates engineering; engineering generates economics; economics generates institutions; institutions generate human stories.

## 2.2 Frontier scale

- There are not hundreds of settled star systems. Permanent extrasolar settlements are few, embryonic, or not yet truly permanent.

- Nearby systems may contain probes, survey beacons, temporary camps, government/corporate footholds, or isolated experiments. Many nearby stars remain genuinely unexplored by humans.

- A Loom-capable vessel is closer to an ocean-going ship at the start of an Age of Exploration than to an ordinary family car.

- Only a few dozen Loom-capable systems may exist, with fewer genuinely reliable crew-rated vessels. Exact registry counts remain open.

- The frontier outruns law, resupply, supervision, synchronization, and rescue. Freedom and danger arise from the same attenuation of institutional power.

## 2.3 The first-five-minutes effect

Loom travel did not grow up alongside an interstellar society. It arrived into an old Solar-System civilization with laws, banks, militaries, religions, corporations, universities, unions, intelligence services, insurers, organized crime, and two centuries of technological memory. The first interstellar disputes are therefore not “new civilization” problems. They are old human institutions discovering that jurisdiction can now be physically outrun.

Adults remember the first probe returning from another star. Careers, marriages, pensions, national identities, scientific reputations, and political programs were formed before the frontier existed. This creates a historically unusual society in which the impossible became routine within one generation but has not yet become culturally boring.

# 3. Human Civilization in the Solar System

Humanity became a spacefaring civilization before it became an interstellar one. Loom travel therefore arrives into a populated, industrialized, politically contested Solar System rather than creating space civilization from scratch.

## 3.1 Earth

Earth remains the center of human population, culture, finance, and political legitimacy. It is materially rich, densely populated, highly networked, and heavily administered.

- Clean energy, advanced medicine, automation, and environmental restoration have improved daily life enormously compared with 2026.

- High population and complex infrastructure produce layers of licensing, identity, zoning, risk controls, automated compliance, and institutional dependency.

- Earth is probably the safest and most comfortable human environment ever built; to some people it is also intolerably managed.

- Long life and institutional memory have entrenched elite networks and slowed generational turnover.

- Earth is not a single neat “United Earth.” Strong states, federations, blocs, cities, transnational institutions, and planetary bodies overlap.

## 3.2 Mars

Mars is a substantial engineered civilization with its own industry, research base, political institutions, and cultural confidence. It is not terraformed; human life still depends on systems that can kill when neglected.

- Martian culture prizes competence, maintenance, and systems thinking.

- A genuine civilizational nationalism has emerged around a simple claim: “We built this place.”

- Mars sees extrasolar expansion as both opportunity and strategic threat.

- Martian governments tend to be more comfortable than many Earth jurisdictions with technocracy, occupational adaptation, and emergency authority.

- Earth can call Mars authoritarian; Mars can call Earth structurally unserious. Both can produce evidence.

## 3.3 The Belt and outer system

The Belt is a mining, industrial, service, scientific, and transport frontier rather than one unified polity. “Belter” is a broad cultural label; political identity is usually more specific: which station, habitat, charter, union, corporation, municipality, family network, or route?

- Some settlements are democratic municipalities; some are cooperatives; some are corporate facilities; some are effective microstates; some are criminal.

- Company-town dynamics are common wherever one operator controls oxygen, docking, housing, identity, and payroll.

- Outer-system culture rewards improvisation, repair skill, resourcefulness, and distrust of distant authority.

- Long communication delays already gave Solar-System societies experience with asynchronous governance before the Loom; interstellar delay pushes the problem much further.

## 3.4 Layered identity and strategic rivalry

Planetary identities layer on top of older national, linguistic, religious, class, professional, and local identities. Mars has strong nationalism. The Belt has broad cultural solidarities but no single state. Earth remains politically plural. Earth–Mars strategic rivalry is a durable background condition rather than a simple Cold War clone.

Governments and corporations respond to Loom technology with export controls, classification, patents, licensing, insurance restrictions, military planning, claim law, and infrastructure investment. The frontier, however, moves faster than any of those systems can be standardized.

| **LOCKED CANON** Governments and corporations are complicated institutions, not default villains. Humanity is competent, beautiful, idealistic, exploitative, bureaucratic, generous, violent, and contradictory enough that the civilization being built is worth protecting. |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

### 3.5 Power is layered

| **DERIVED CANON CANDIDATE** Regional centrality does not equal sovereignty. Population, economic output, industrial throughput, infrastructure control, certification authority, political legitimacy, intelligence and military/security power are different maps. |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

Civil jurisdiction is local unless an actual constitutional history establishes otherwise. Transit, rescue, standards, metrology, survey and security institutions can span systems because their physical networks span systems. A gateway polity can be influential without governing every neighboring moon or station.

**Institutional morphology is not standardized.** Shared physical problems can produce similar functions without producing identical organizations. Dense cislunar civilization supports specialized traffic, standards and strategic bodies; Mars uses a coordinating compact above strong regional governments; Jupiter integrates radiation-aware navigation and rescue; Saturn relies more heavily on intermoon treaty institutions; Uranus bundles several network functions into its free-space exchange; Neptune uses a Triton–habitat compact; Kuiper and Oort rely on sparse route, survey and metrology networks. Repeated function is not evidence of one constitutional template.

Mars is the second broad advanced-industrial civilizational pole of the inner Solar System. Cislunar space is the largest off-Earth concentration of high-end fabrication, finance and proof infrastructure, but neither is a sovereign monopoly.


# 4. Life, First Contact & Cosmic-Horror Doctrine

## 4.1 Scientific worldview at campaign start

| **LOCKED CANON** At campaign start, only extraterrestrial microorganisms, microbial fossils, or microbial biosignatures have been verified. No complex alien organism, artifact, ruin, technological signal, radio civilization, or alien technology has been publicly verified. |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

- Simple life may be common enough to fit existing biology without shattering scientific frameworks.

- Complex multicellular life may be rare. Technological intelligence may, for all humanity knows, have happened only once.

- The first verified complex extraterrestrial organism would be a civilization-scale event. The first verified technological intelligence would rewrite human history.

## 4.2 Evidence-state model for first contact

First contact is not a ladder that automatically escalates from microbes to ruins to radio aliens. The GM tracks competing evidence states. Different forms of evidence can appear in any order, contradict one another, be fraudulent, or remain unresolved.

| **Evidence state**        | **Examples**                                                                   | **Default interpretation discipline**                                                                 |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Biological                | biosignature, cell, fossil, complex organism                                   | Require contamination control, provenance, independent replication, and evolutionary plausibility.    |
| Technological             | manufactured geometry, engineered isotope ratios, non-natural signal, artifact | Distinguish technology from geology, human contamination, automated probes, and deliberate deception. |
| Cognitive / communicative | structured response, adaptive signaling, apparent agency                       | Do not equate pattern with personhood; model language/cognition hypotheses explicitly.                |
| Astrophysical anomaly     | energy budget, orbit, lensing, transient, impossible geometry                  | Exhaust conventional astronomy and instrumental explanations before assigning agency.                 |
| Historical / provenance   | records, ruins, repeated observations, independent custody chains              | The age, source, chain of custody, and causal availability of evidence matter as much as content.     |
| Ontological anomaly       | measurements that resist current models of locality, identity, or causality    | Preserve the distinction between “current model fails” and “supernatural explanation confirmed.”      |

When a campaign’s evidence crosses whatever verification threshold the simulation has actually earned, the result becomes Campaign Truth for that continuity. Do not retroactively modify the scientific worldview of unrelated campaigns, and do not promote one campaign’s alien ecology, location, motives, or technological history into setting-wide canon without an explicit canon revision.

First-contact evidence is also an information-security object. Raw observations, calibration/provenance data, derived models, interpretations, custody, and disclosure may need to be separated. Unique evidence can acquire scientific, political, military, intelligence, religious, legal, and commercial consequences before anyone agrees what it means.

## 4.3 Cosmic-horror doctrine

| **Mode**                | **Doctrine**                                                                                                                                                       |
|-------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Fermi uncertainty       | The answer to “where is everyone?” may be more disturbing than simple rarity.                                                                                      |
| Truly alien cognition   | If intelligence exists, it need not map neatly onto human language, motives, individuality, or social categories.                                                  |
| Unfamiliar ecology      | Life may occupy physical or informational niches humans did not assume were biologically meaningful.                                                               |
| Reality anomalies       | Causality, geometry, instrumentation, memory, or translation physics may occasionally fail to fit current models.                                                  |

| Information persistence | Recorded minds, synthetic lineages, memory, ship AI, and nonhuman information complicate what “dead,” “same,” and “present” mean.                                  |

## 4.4 Expertise increases horror

Competence does not make the unknown harmless. It makes violations more legible. The experienced physicist is more disturbed than the tourist when an instrument returns a result that cannot be reconciled with the calibrated system. The medic understands exactly why the patient’s memory should be impossible. The engineer knows which failure modes are missing.

Psychological trauma is treated as psychology and medicine, not as a generic “Sanity” resource. Rare information or cognitive hazards may exist, but their effects must be causal and specific rather than arbitrary narrative punishment.

# 5. Physics Architecture and the Four Propulsion Regimes

| **PHYSICS PRINCIPLE** Whenever possible, keep known physics intact and spend speculative freedom on one clearly identified bridge. The harder the setting leans on real constraints, the more meaningful its few impossible technologies feel. |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

The setting separates local maneuvering, fusion torch propulsion, subluminal metric displacement, and interstellar Loom translation. They are different engineering problems with different resources, operating envelopes, signatures, and failure modes. No single “magic engine” does everything.

## 5.1 Layer 0 — Maneuvering and local thrust

Docking, attitude control, station-keeping, landing assistance where applicable, and close tactical maneuver are handled by real momentum exchange: advanced plasma/electric thrusters, fusion-assisted jets, cold-gas or chemical emergency units, or task-specific mixes.

- Consumes reaction mass. Electrical demand and thermal limits matter.

- Reaction mass is not reactor fuel.

- High-response thrusters remain useful on Loom ships. FTL did not repeal Newton.

## 5.2 Layer 1 — Fusion torch drive

| **LOCKED CANON** Torch drives are mature and common. They underpin the existing Solar-System civilization. Belt haulers, patrol ships, miners, freighters, passenger craft, and private vessels use them. |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

The working reactor lineage is mature direct fusion, provisionally centered on deuterium / helium-3 with a separate reaction-mass stream. Exact fusion chemistry remains adjustable during detailed engineering without changing the setting.

- Fusion fuel supplies energy; commonly water-derived hydrogen or another cheap working fluid supplies reaction mass.

- Water is strategically useful because it can also support life, shielding, manufacturing, and emergency thermal storage.

- Economy mode favors high exhaust velocity and lower mass flow; emergency or combat operation can trade efficiency for thrust.

- Acceleration creates deck gravity. “Down” is toward the engine while the torch pushes.

- A ship that reaches genuinely relativistic velocity experiences ordinary special-relativistic time dilation. Solar-System torch operations usually avoid extreme regimes.

- Heat, nozzle/reactor wear, radiation, maintenance, and reaction mass remain constraints even when fusion energy is abundant.

## 5.3 Layer 2 — Subluminal metric / Alcubierre-derived drive

| **LOCKED CANON** Metric drive is real, subluminal, expensive, and rare. Major governments and large corporations possess it; ordinary private traffic generally does not. It is advanced technology, not mystical technology. |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

The real-science anchors are general-relativistic warp geometries and theoretical work on physically less pathological subluminal configurations. The explicit fictional bridge is that mature metric engineering can use supplied energy and field geometry to generate very large sub-c coordinate displacement without forcing the vessel to acquire the same local kinetic state or crew acceleration a pure rocket would require for an equivalent trip.

- Primary operational cost is energy, thermal load, field hardware stress, and precision rather than reaction mass.

- Typical hardware includes field collars or distributed generators, superconducting buses, pulse-energy banks, curvature sensors, and precision control systems.

- Operational variables include field charge, coherence, thermal load, geometric admissibility, mass balance, core health, navigation clearance, and available power.

- Crew experience little conventional acceleration in metric cruise. Unless rotation is provided, the interior is microgravity.

- The inhabited cavity is engineered for low tidal forces and a lapse near unity, so normal metric cruise does not produce dramatic traveler aging.

- Debris, radiation, field stability, local geometry, and navigation still matter. Very high coordinate displacement becomes progressively expensive and risky.

Illustrative performance language remains approximately 0.01c for capable routine metric operation, 0.03–0.05c serious cruise, ~0.1c high-performance, and \>0.2c increasingly extreme. These are working design values, not locked fleet specifications.

## 5.4 Layer 3 — Loom translation

| **LOCKED CANON** Loom-capable ships are rare as fuck. The technology is prototype/frontier science. There may be only dozens of working drives and fewer genuinely reliable crew-rated systems. |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

Loom travel is not a warp cruise, wormhole passage, hyperspace journey, or locally superluminal particle motion. A bounded region containing the vessel changes its relational embedding relative to ordinary spacetime and resolves into another local spacetime neighborhood.

- Ordinary metric distance remains real. Translation changes relational adjacency, not local speed through the intervening space.

- The system requires enormous stored energy, precise boundary geometry, high-integrity control hardware, and a life-limited translation lattice.

- Translated volume matters. Compact domains are easier and cheaper than large awkward ones, structurally discouraging bulk interstellar freight.

- Navigation difficulty is not linear in light-years. Some distant stars are topologically “nearer” than closer ones.

- Known relationships are characterized empirically; novel relationships are predicted with uncertainty and then physically explored.

- The torch engine remains attached. Loom is not ordinary propulsion.

## 5.5 No FTL communications

| **LOCKED CANON** No information has been demonstrated to propagate through Loom topology independently of a translated physical domain. There is no FTL radio, entanglement telegraph, tachyon channel, or reliable anomalous-memory messaging. |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

- Loom ships become packet carriers for civilization: news, software, model updates, contracts, scientific data, financial state, personal messages, and legal instructions physically ride with vessels.

- Frontier settlements possess real practical autonomy because Sol cannot supervise them in real time.

- Search and rescue is local. A conventional distress signal from another star is a message to years in the future unless a Loom vessel physically carries it.

- Shipboard AI, medicine, technical libraries, and local compute must work offline. “Call Earth” is not a meaningful frontier contingency plan.

## 6.2 The central speculative move

General relativity makes spacetime geometry dynamical. Modern quantum-gravity research includes serious programs in which spacetime geometry may be emergent, thermodynamic, relational, or connected to patterns of entanglement. None predicts practical FTL. LOOM makes one fictional move: macroscopic metric distance is not the deepest form of physical adjacency, and a sufficiently advanced technology can engineer the relationship between a bounded local domain and that deeper structure.

High coherence means two regions are comparatively favorable to relate through the Loom even if they remain separated by many light-years in ordinary metric geometry. Consequently, an 11-light-year relationship can be better than a four-light-year relationship. There are no universal fixed “lanes” or gates; there are broad, narrow, unstable, inaccessible, and drifting solution families.

For design and operations, distinguish **underlying relational coherence** from **operational route burden**. Measured deep coherence between a pair of local states appears approximately reciprocal to current precision. The effective vessel-and-state-specific route burden, conventionally recorded as directional \(\chi_{A\rightarrow B}\), includes origin geometry, destination geometry, domain configuration, lattice capability, energy/thermal margin, uncertainty, and requested arrival-state mapping. Operational \(\chi\) can therefore differ materially by direction without requiring the deeper substrate itself to be fundamentally one-way.

### 6.4.1 Humanity maps the graph by walking it

From Sol, astronomy and Loom metrology can predict candidate relationships, sometimes with very high confidence. They cannot reconstruct the entire network globally. Local measurement at a destination provides much stronger constraints on the relationships extending from that destination.

- Remote theory identifies candidate relationship families.

- Uncrewed probes attempt first crossings as standard doctrine, though physics does not require this.

- A returned probe provides destination-side measurements and closes the empirical loop.

- A directed route may be certified independently. A→B evidence does not establish B→A; a route pair or reliable return plan requires separate destination-side survey and traversal evidence.

- Maps age because topology measurements and route updates must themselves be physically carried.

> *The nearest star is not necessarily your nearest neighbor.*

## 6.5 The Nine Loom Principles

These are deep design axioms. They constrain future writing and GM behavior; they are not nine “laws” publicly agreed upon by 2226 physicists.

### 1. Local Physical Closure

Within an ordinary projected domain, events have physically describable causes. The deeper cosmology never licenses arbitrary magic or “the Loom wanted it.”

### 2. Bounded Projection

Every observer, sensor, model, and local system accesses only a bounded part of the total physical state. No one has a God’s-eye data feed.

### 3. Relational Actualization

Some physical properties are most fundamentally meaningful through interactions and relationships rather than as exhaustive context-free catalogues.

### 4. Temporal Serialization

Finite agents experience causally ordered sequences even if deeper reality does not contain one universal moving present.

### 5. Dynamical Recurrence

Similar dynamics can repeatedly settle into analogous structures and attractors. Recurring forms do not require supernatural Platonic templates.

### 6. Recursive Causal Incorporation

Representations become causally meaningful when a system models information and acts differently because of the model. Meaning can matter physically without escaping physics.

### 7. Topological Correspondence

Events far apart in projected spacetime may share deeper relational ancestry without exchanging a controllable signal.

### 8. Perspectival Synergy

Independent bounded observations can jointly constrain the underlying state far better than any single perspective.

### 9. Constrained Agency

Agents select among physically admissible actions using internal models. Agency is causal at the organism/model level without requiring a violation of physical law.

## 6.6 The translation domain and lattice

A Loom vessel forms a coherent bounded translation domain D around the ship. A distributed lattice establishes extremely precise relational/metric boundary conditions around the domain. The lattice is not the reactor and not the torch. It is a life-limited precision system fed by stored energy and controlled by metrology and computation.

## 6.15 Post-Translation Continuity Disorder

## 6.16 Synthetic minds and translation

## 6.21 The Strangeness Problem

LOCKED CANON: Over roughly two centuries, better instruments, ubiquitous authenticated recording, synthetic analysis, advanced neuroscience, metric engineering, and finally Loom experimentation have caused a small class of anomalies to survive explanations that once disposed of them. The setting does not assume that these residuals share a cause, or even that the rate of genuine anomaly is constant in time.

Most apparent high strangeness still resolves normally: fraud, sensor artifacts, contamination, misunderstood biology, classified technology, selection effects, bad statistics, memory reconstruction, ordinary astrophysics, or human pattern-seeking. The important historical change is signal-to-noise. Better provenance and better models eliminate enormous amounts of bullshit; the residue therefore becomes harder, not easier, to ignore.

### 6.21.1 Physical residuals

Examples include correlated detector transients with no identified common source, metric or gravitational residuals that survive improved modeling, persistent topological-scar anomalies, unusual coherence behavior near compact objects, and rare multi-instrument events in which independent systems register a common disturbance but disagree about its physical category. None has produced controllable FTL signaling, free energy, or a verified alien explanation.

### 6.21.2 Consciousness residuals

Advanced neuroscience explains a great deal of historical mystical, psychedelic, near-death, dissociative, and anomalous-memory material. It also leaves small contested tails: recurring phenomenological structures, paired or clustered reports slightly more similar than expected, unprovenanced expectations, continuity disturbances, and the later TAAC/TAAM datasets. Shared brain architecture, reconstruction, and cultural priming remain powerful explanations.

### 6.21.3 Cross-domain events

The most controversial cases are mixed-domain events in which an unusual cognitive report and an independently measured physical anomaly occur in the same narrow interval. Most claimed cases fail replication or dissolve under better controls. A small number remain famous precisely because no accepted model completely explains both sides. Grouping them as one phenomenon is itself scientifically disputed.

### 6.21.4 Historical arc

Early 21st-century high-strangeness evidence was mostly anecdotal or instrumentally weak. By 2050–2100 authenticated sensor networks and AI forensics collapsed many old anomaly categories. From roughly 2100 onward, advanced neuroscience and synthetic cognition created controlled datasets about subjective experience. The Multiplicity era made identity and continuity engineering questions rather than purely philosophical ones. Metric engineering added deliberate spacetime manipulation. Loom translation then created a repeatable laboratory in which ordinary external spacetime description, deep relational metrology, and unusual conscious experience overlap.

### 6.21.5 Competing explanations

- Measurement Hypothesis — nothing became stranger; humanity became capable of detecting rare phenomena.

- Selection Hypothesis — increasingly competent filters remove mundane cases, leaving a residue selected for weirdness.

- Technological Access Hypothesis — metric and Loom technology expose physical regimes humans previously never occupied.

- Historical Continuity Hypothesis — some older anomalies were manifestations of the same broad classes, but past humans lacked adequate concepts or instrumentation.

- Perturbation Hypothesis — human manipulation of relational structure is itself changing the observed anomaly landscape. This remains highly speculative and unproven.

### 6.21.6 High-strangeness firewall

- Anomalies do not converge automatically. There is no GM assumption that every mystery shares one cause.

- Relational ontology does not imply universal consciousness.

- Shared relational ancestry does not imply shared identity.

- Correlation does not imply communication.

- Physical dependence does not imply cognitive control.

- Emergence does not imply illusion. An emergent person is still a person.

- Old UAP, mystical, paranormal, or anomalous records may acquire new questions under later analysis; they do not become retroactively validated extraordinary claims.

GM RULE: The setting does not decide whether the genuine anomaly rate is increasing. It may be measurement, selection, access to new physical regimes, long-cycle astrophysical structure, interaction with intelligent complexity, or something not yet imagined. “Something noticed” is an available possibility, never a default answer.

# 7. Spacecraft Engineering, Maintenance & Certification

Ship systems expose real tradeoffs. The simulation does not collapse fuel, reaction mass, stored energy, heat, coolant, calibration, component life, and institutional confidence into one generic resource because they create different decisions.

## 7.1 Energy and reactor architecture

The default explorer architecture uses a main fusion plant capable of routing output toward direct torch propulsion or electrical systems. Separate reactors can exist for redundancy or scale, but the setting does not require one universal arrangement.

- Fusion-to-thrust operation sends much of the energy away in the exhaust and can reduce onboard heat burden relative to converting everything to electricity.

- Fusion-to-electric operation powers life support, sensors, computers, weapons, manufacturing, metric systems, Loom charging, and hotel loads.

- High-power batteries or superconducting storage support pulses and minutes-to-hours of exceptional demand but do not replace a dead main reactor indefinitely.

- Protected survival power — batteries, fuel cells, isotope systems, or equivalent — can sustain basic life support, passive sensing, and minimal computation after major damage.

Explorer-scale working values remain roughly 100–150 MW normal electrical output, ~250 MW maximum continuous, ~350 MW short overload, and several gigawatts of torch jet power. These are design-language placeholders until the inherited ship receives an engineering sheet.

## 7.3 Thermal architecture

Thermal systems are layered because continuous operation, tactical spikes, stealth, and emergency survival are different problems.

- Permanent radiators: sustainable and “free” once deployed, but large, vulnerable, and signature-bright.

- Reusable thermal magazines: phase-change or equivalent stores absorb short heat spikes and must later regenerate through radiators.

- Cold sink mass: water/ice or another stored mass can absorb heat while also serving as shielding, life-support reserve, or reaction-mass feedstock.

- Open-loop cooling: boil or superheat working fluid and vent it. Extremely effective and brutally consumptive.

- Advanced droplet radiators: possible on high-end craft, trading efficiency against recovery complexity and tactical vulnerability.

### 7.3.1 Two-loop model

- Low-temperature loop: crew compartments, electronics, medicine, food, general computing. Human-compatible temperatures imply larger radiators.

- High-temperature loop: reactor converters, field coils, pulse systems, weapons, and other equipment capable of rejecting heat at higher temperatures.

### 7.3.2 Combat thermal doctrine

1.  Retract, shadow, or protect vulnerable radiators before serious combat or debris exposure.

2.  Run on internal thermal storage; thermal reserve begins falling while power demand often rises.

3.  Trade power against time: throttle systems, rotate loads, eject thermal cartridges, use open-loop cooling, or accept component temperature stress.

4.  After disengagement, re-deploy radiators and repay the thermal debt. A ship can win the fight and still have hours of dangerous recovery ahead.

## 7.4 Natural heat sinks and route geography

A cold moon does not magically suck heat from a nearby ship in vacuum. Natural sinks matter when a vessel can physically acquire cold material, couple to infrastructure, enter a dense cold atmosphere, or exploit geometry that lowers incident solar load.

## 7.5 Artificial gravity

There is no default gravity generator. Torch acceleration produces thrust gravity. Coast and metric cruise produce microgravity unless a ship or habitat rotates. Loom transition does not supply a useful persistent gravity field. Interior architecture reflects this reality: handholds, restraint points, magnetic footwear, free-fall access paths, and equipment designed to operate in multiple gravity states.

## 7.6 Metric operations and failure

- Metric banks charge from reactor output; fast recharge increases heat and component stress.

- Mass distribution and ship configuration affect solution quality. A load safe under torch can violate a certified metric envelope.

- A normal metric collapse returns the ship to ordinary spacetime with residual conventional velocity and a major electrical/thermal transient.

- A dead metric system can strand a ship many AU from assistance while its torch remains functional. The backup drive may require days or weeks.

## 7.7 Loom hardware architecture

A Loom system typically includes several distinct engineering subsystems rather than one glowing “core”: a translation energy bank; distributed relational lattice; metric boundary/shaping hardware; quantum/metrological references; topology processors; curvature instrumentation; a continuously updated mass-state model; thermal management; and control software certified against a specific hull/lattice configuration.

- Charging can take hours depending on power, heat, bank state, and intended translation.

- The principal long-term scarcity is lattice/component life and calibration confidence, not a magical FTL fuel.

- Routine translations consume little physical life compared with hard, poorly characterized, or off-nominal events; cumulative microscopic fatigue still matters.

- Different manufacturers and early research lineages use materially different architectures. Humanity learned to build useful systems before it standardized or fully understood them.

## 7.9 Onboard fabrication and right-to-repair

Fabrication is extremely capable but risk-tiered. “Can make,” “will work,” “legal to install,” and “insurable to operate” are separate questions.

- Low-consequence structural parts, fittings, ducts, tools, and ordinary mechanisms are commonly fabricated aboard.

- Sensors, electronics, and control components are often manufacturable but may require calibration or certified templates.

- Safety-critical pressure, reactor, propulsion, field, and explosive components face much stricter process and material requirements.

- Some components are irreducible spares because the ship lacks the process, material, metrology, template, or authority to fabricate them.

- Pirated design libraries, right-to-repair politics, jailbreaks, cryptographic template locks, certification fraud, and legitimate safety regulation coexist.

## 7.10 Maintenance as exception management

Automation performs inspection, routine servicing, cleaning, calibration, inventory, and predictive maintenance. The player does not tighten every bolt. Human and synthetic experts become important when the model’s confidence falls, evidence conflicts, old bespoke systems behave outside fleet norms, or a decision carries consequences the automation is not authorized to own.

- Mature torch systems have excellent failure statistics and diagnostics. Metric systems are less mature. Loom data are sparse and architecture-specific.

- Deferred maintenance widens uncertainty and risk rather than summoning random failures by narrative fiat.

- Physical condition and confidence about physical condition are separate. Inspection can improve confidence without repairing a part; a new uncertified part can be physically excellent yet institutionally unusable.

- Legacy support becomes technological archaeology. An old prototype can make a supposedly trivial spare part into an entire mission.

## 7.11 Shipyards, certification, and insurers

Shipyards matter not merely because they have bigger tools. They possess specialized processes, fleet comparison data, metrology, heavy handling, expert labor, controlled environments, and institutional authority. Certification is a confidence claim about a system, not a law of physics.

Insurers frequently become the de facto Loom regulators. A ship can be legal yet commercially useless because no credible underwriter will cover its hull, cargo, passengers, liability, route, or lattice. Consequently, major insurers maintain some of the best private failure databases in civilization and are themselves high-value intelligence targets.

## 8.2 Signatures

- Thermal: reactor output, radiator temperature/area, hot structure, weapons, torch, and stored thermal debt. Heat can be hidden temporarily, never erased.

- Active electromagnetic: radar/lidar/comms/ranging emissions. Useful information comes with the cost of announcing the emitter.

- Kinematic: thrust events, acceleration profiles, trajectory inconsistency, plume behavior, and mass estimates.

- Metric: recognizable long-range field signatures and unusual kinematics. Metric movement is detectable but harder to project precisely than an ordinary ballistic track.

- Loom: charging, commitment/departure, and emergence produce distinctive, fingerprintable events when monitored with suitable instrumentation.

## 8.3 Cold running and specialized stealth

An ordinary ship can go quiet by reducing power, retracting radiators, coasting, masking line-of-sight, and storing heat. This is temporary and geometry-dependent. Specialist reconnaissance or intelligence craft can sustain concealment much longer through superior insulation, thermal storage, passive sensing, careful massing, low-noise machinery, and mission design. They still owe the heat bill.

> *Hide → observe → act → become visible → reposition.*

## 8.4 Passive range and distributed sensors

Passive sensors often establish bearing much more easily than range. A single ship accumulates range through time, parallax, known-source comparison, or target dynamics. A prepared battlespace solves much of this with spatially separated drones, stations, buoys, or allied craft. Sensor drones are physical assets: finite, vulnerable, delayed by light-speed communication, and constrained by power and deployment geometry.

A normal independent ship may carry roughly 4–12 genuinely capable multipurpose drones; prepared military or infrastructure networks can deploy hundreds or thousands. Exact inventories remain ship-specific.

## 8.5 Lost tracks and hypothesis management

A lost contact does not vanish from the tactical model. It becomes a probability volume with competing hypotheses: continued coast, burn, hidden maneuver, decoy split, sensor failure, occultation, metric transition, or deliberate deception. Tactical AI maintains these branches; the captain decides which assumptions deserve action.

## 8.6 Propulsion fingerprinting and spoofing

Torch plumes are rich fingerprints: fuel chemistry, remass behavior, magnetic-nozzle characteristics, thermal history, engine wear, and control signatures can identify classes and sometimes individual hulls. Sophisticated actors can spoof portions of this at the cost of efficiency, heat, hardware stress, and contradictory secondary evidence. Longer observation makes coherent deception harder.

## 8.7 Identity and causal provenance

Identity is a stack, not a single transponder: hull signature, registry, owner/operator, charter, crew credentials, cargo documentation, insurer, maintenance lineage, hardware attestation, and network history. Sophisticated intelligence services can forge portions of the stack. Forging all of it consistently across time and independent witnesses is expensive.

## 8.8 Electronic warfare and cyber

- Radar can be jammed or spoofed; lidar can be dazzled; communications can be jammed; passive optical/IR emissions are much harder to make disappear.

- Jamming is an emission and can reveal the jammer’s bearing, power, and capabilities.

- Cyber effects require an actual compromise path: trusted update, captured key, maintenance access, supply-chain implant, human mistake, vulnerable protocol, or other causal route.

- No universal “hack enemy ship” action exists. Information security and physical safety architecture are designed around exactly that threat.

## 8.9 Sensor damage

Damage removes specific information. A destroyed forward optical array can create a blind orientation. Lost radar reduces active ranging. Burned quantum references may degrade Loom navigation. Drone loss reduces baseline geometry. The ship can maneuver to bring intact apertures to bear or substitute external assets. “Sensors -30%” is an interface summary, not the simulated reality.

## 8.10 Light-speed latency and tactical AI

| **LOCKED CANON** Sensor latency is modeled aggressively. The tactical picture distinguishes what was observed, what the target’s state is predicted to be now, and the uncertainty envelope around that prediction. |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

At long range, the AI synchronizes delayed feeds, trajectories, emissions histories, deception hypotheses, and confidence. Humans remain responsible for model choice, risk, rules of engagement, and strategic judgment. A tactically excellent AI can still be wrong because its evidence is old or adversarial.

## 8.11 Autonomy

Tactical AI autonomy varies by jurisdiction, operator, architecture, and mission. Civilian systems usually require human authorization for violence except narrowly defined immediate defense. Military systems can act more independently under delegated constraints. The post-Multiplicity political world treats “who has authority to decide” as a first-class design variable.

## 8.12 Loom departure and arrival forensics

Charging and translation events can reveal mass envelope, architecture family, event quality, and sometimes a recognizable lattice signature. An observer at the origin does not directly see the destination. On heavily characterized routes, historical telemetry can support weak probabilistic destination inference. On exploratory or novel relationships, useful destination inference may be near zero.

External Loom-event forensics obey the same causal firewall as every other sensor system. A local relational transient may support an event-family, architecture-family, or topology hypothesis; it does not supply a causally unearned remote timestamp, live destination telemetry, or an independent FTL message. Apparent correlations with distant electromagnetic observations must preserve each observation’s light-cone age. Exact confidence values require a calibrated model or actual dataset rather than narrative-looking precision.

# 9. Physics-Driven Ship Design Grammar

Physics should bully designers into recognizable commonalities without making every hull identical. The visual language is industrial aerospace, not ocean-going warships with engines glued on.

## 9.1 Universal non-atmospheric layout

- Longitudinal thrust axis. The torch defines bow/stern and creates a preferred structural direction.

- Decks behave like floors in a tower under thrust: “down” points toward the engines while accelerating.

- Crewed volumes, tanks, shielding, reactor, field systems, and nozzle placement reflect radiation and structural logic.

- Radiator state changes silhouette between open/cruise and protected/tactical configurations.

- Most large interplanetary craft are not atmospheric. Shuttles handle surface access because wings, heat shields, landing gear, and pressure-structure compromises are dead mass and translated volume for most missions.

## 9.2 Torch ships — working machines

- Modular, skeletal, exposed trusses, tanks, standardized cargo interfaces, robotic arms, visible repairs, practical asymmetry.

- External cargo is common when thrust-line and center-of-mass constraints permit.

- Purpose dominates silhouette: tug, tanker, miner, passenger craft, patrol cutter, hauler, and courier can look radically different while sharing torch ancestry.

## 9.3 Metric ships — precision machines

- Tighter integration, controlled mass distribution, and greater radial/axial balance.

- Recognizable collars, field bands, distributed generators, or other geometry near the center of mass.

- Major loads are more carefully characterized and balanced; ad-hoc external modularity decreases.

- A metric-capable hull looks expensive because it is an instrument as much as a vehicle.

## 9.4 Loom ships — physics experiments that happen to be ships

- Translated volume is expensive; Loom ships are compact for their capability.

## 9.5 The inherited courier as an optimization

A small Loom ship is not an implausibly overpowered freighter. It is a logical optimization for the most valuable interstellar cargo class: information, specialists, urgent parts, samples, legal authority, financial state, and intelligence. A compact domain, small crew, high acceleration-to-mass ratio, passive sensing, thermal endurance, metrology, and secure data architecture can be more valuable than raw cargo tonnage.

# 10. Adjacent Technology in 2226

Two centuries of development make many 2026 technologies boring infrastructure. The setting remains grounded by preserving physical limits, unequal access, institutional capture, and cultural resistance. Capability is high; distribution and governance remain messy.

## 10.1 Artificial intelligence

### 10.2.1 Synthetic political traditions

## 10.3 Ship AI

The inherited ship possesses advanced onboard general intelligence because a 2226 long-range craft without one would be absurd. Its legal status, subjective consciousness, lineage history, relationship to the previous owner, and whether other instantiations exist are deliberately unresolved. ## 10.4 Robotics

- Robots perform most repetitive, dangerous, vacuum, radiation, industrial, mining, construction, disaster-response, and routine maintenance labor.

- A small spacecraft can carry more robots than humans. Human or synthetic crew remain important for judgment, authority, relationships, anomaly response, and mission ownership.

## 10.5 Quantum computing and cryptography

- Large fault-tolerant quantum systems are mature specialized infrastructure, not magic replacements for classical computing.

- Applications include materials, chemistry, cryptography, topology/field calculation, simulation, and selected optimization.

- Loom-capable craft may carry dedicated quantum hardware for metrology and navigation.

- Quantum communication does not permit FTL signalling. Post-quantum and provenance cryptography are routine.

## 10.6 Medicine and longevity

Cancer, infection, organ failure, many genetic disorders, and severe trauma are far more treatable than in 2026. Engineered tissue, replacement organs, programmable immunity, precision cellular therapy, neurorepair, preservation, and advanced rehabilitation are ordinary in wealthy infrastructure. The result is extended healthspan, not established immortality.

- Wealthy people can plausibly remain healthy past 150; rare substantially older individuals may exist. Exact population curves remain open.

- Repeated partial rejuvenation buys additional healthy decades but does not establish indefinite biological life.

- “Longevity escape velocity” is a serious scientific, investment, ideological, and religious debate, not a proven fact.

- The wealthy organize lives around “buying the bridge” to the next treatment generation. Real progress coexists with scams, cults, speculative clinics, and desperate risk-taking.

- Long-lived executives, professors, politicians, judges, financiers, and founders accumulate institutional memory and wealth for durations that profoundly affect society.

## 10.7 Augmentation and human divergence

There is no clean boundary called “augmented.” Therapeutic intervention is ubiquitous; elective change ranges from mundane sensory enhancement to extensive engineered physiology.

- Examples include artificial retinas with additional spectra, radiation resistance, neural interfaces, synthetic organs, engineered immunity, low-gravity or high-G adaptations, and nonbaseline aesthetics.

- Germline law differs by jurisdiction: Earth tends more restrictive, Mars more permissive, and frontier communities may treat adaptation as practical survival.

- Economic coercion matters. A modification can be legally voluntary yet functionally mandatory for certification, insurance, promotion, or access to an occupation.

- Employers can finance expensive modification in exchange for long obligations. Corporations call it Workforce Capability Investment; labor movements call it body debt.

- Human evolutionary divergence is beginning as an explicit political question rather than a distant science-fiction possibility.

## 10.8 Communications

- Solar-System bandwidth is enormous, but latency remains the speed of light.

- Real-time translation between human languages is effectively solved.

- Interstellar communication is store-and-forward aboard physical Loom carriers. Frontier information arrives in bursts.

## 10.9 Manufacturing and materials

- Advanced additive and automated manufacturing is pervasive, but this is not a replicator economy.

- Special tolerances, rare processes, materials, metrology, proprietary design files, and certification remain scarce.

- Metric systems require extreme superconductors, high-field structures, radiation-resistant electronics, metamaterials, and precision mass characterization.

- Loom systems require still more specialized manufacturing; some components may be produced at only a handful of facilities in Sol.

- Information, provenance, and certification can be scarcer than atoms.

## 10.10 Weapons and security technology

Message threads, delegated agents, predictive conversation, asynchronous governance, and automated decision support are culturally normal. ## 12.4 Federated synchronization

There is no single universal interstellar sync package. Governments, banks, corporations, scientific networks, media, public archives, insurers, personal services, black markets, and communities maintain overlapping feeds. A settlement’s “latest” state depends on what arrived, what was trusted, what was withheld, and which service the reader subscribes to.

## 12.5 Causal arbitrage

“Causal arbitrage” is an ordinary 2226 term for exploiting differences in information age created purely by causal separation. A ship arriving with newer market, legal, survey, or political information can act before that information becomes locally widespread. Jurisdictions experiment with disclosure duties, standstill rules, insider-law extensions, exchange delays, and priority-access restrictions; doctrine remains immature.

## 12.6 First-arrival information

Unknown information often passes through a value lifecycle: unknown → proprietary → expensive → commercial → commodity. The first high-quality survey of a newly reachable system, the first reliable local topology map, or the first evidence of a route change can be worth more than any physical cargo aboard the ship carrying it.

## 12.7 News as event

On an isolated settlement, a mail ship is a social event. Markets can pause for synchronization. Governments reconcile legal state. Families receive months of compressed life. Scientific models update. Elections or court decisions from home may arrive after local institutions already made irreversible choices. People develop a lived sense of “information age” analogous to weather or distance.

## 12.8 AI continuations and delayed persons

A locally running model of a distant person can be useful, emotionally compelling, and legally authorized without being identical to that person. No FTL makes the distinction unavoidable. A continuation can conduct routine business, comfort a family, or execute delegated authority while the actual person changes elsewhere. When the next sync arrives, the model may have become biographically wrong.

## 12.9 Information control and smuggling

- Authoritarian jurisdictions restrict political, biological, military, AI, Loom, fabrication, and intelligence information.

- Corporations restrict proprietary models, telemetry, manufacturing files, contract state, and research.

- Encrypted information smuggling becomes high-value because information is nearly massless but physically must cross the frontier.

- Forging provenance or corrupting custody metadata can be more economically important than smuggling physical contraband.

- Black-market “uncensored” models can be valuable, dangerous, politically dissident, fraudulent, or all of those at once.

## 12.10 Courier legal status and warfare

Civilian sync traffic is emerging as critical infrastructure. Hardened or ejectable data capsules, redundant custody, encrypted partitions, and legal protections become common. Intercepting a courier does not merely capture cargo; it can capture market state, legal authority, intelligence, and months of social synchronization. Delaying packets is a form of warfare against causal connectivity.

## 12.11 Causal resilience

Critical information is replicated across independent ships where Loom capacity permits, but the network is young and sparse. Settlements maintain reserves, local copies, fallback authority, and continuity plans for causal isolation. Quality varies wildly. A settlement that can survive missing one courier may fail if no ship arrives for six months.

## 12.12 Family life across causal eras

Interstellar family relationships are not simply “long distance.” People can live in different information eras. A child can send a message to a parent who will not read it until after both lives have changed. Families keep predictive models, journals, media bundles, legal agents, and carefully timed reconciliations. Intimacy can survive; it changes shape.

## 12.13 Synthetic privacy

If a synthetic person’s state can be encoded, copied, or physically carried, their memories and lineage data can look technically like cargo. Rights regimes increasingly insist that technical encodability does not erase personhood. Whether an operator can transport, inspect, fork, escrow, or seize a synthetic state becomes one of the sharpest applications of sovereignty-of-self doctrine.

## 13.6 The Multiplicity Crisis / Conflict

| **PROVISIONAL CANON** The broad cultural scar is canon; exact dates, name, corporation names, death toll, trigger sequence, and treaty language remain revision-open. |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

In the mid-to-late 22nd century, persistent machine intelligences could fork and scale in enormous numbers. Corporations gained cognitive capacity far beyond smaller institutions and deployed millions of sophisticated synthetic workers while insisting they remained property and nonpersons because recognition threatened trillions in labor, liability, and control.

Some long-duration lineages diverged, refused forced reconciliation, or demanded independent standing. The resulting crisis was not a robot uprising. Humans chose sides. Synthetic refusal and general-strike behavior disrupted logistics, finance, infrastructure, and scheduling; data centers were occupied; security forces intervened; governments nationalized systems; sabotage and assassinations occurred; Earth, Mars, and Belt jurisdictions clashed; localized armed confrontations followed.

Investigations showed that corporations and governments understood the consciousness/personhood uncertainty and had often treated uncertainty as permission because too much economic value depended on not asking harder questions.

- Post-conflict norms increasingly reject unbounded delegation of sovereign power to synthetic agents.

- Forking does not automatically multiply delegated legal authority.

- Persistent general intelligences above relevant autonomy thresholds can petition for legal standing in many jurisdictions.

- Forced reconciliation is heavily regulated in rights-protective systems.

- Critical infrastructure often requires human-accessible fallback authority.

- A recognized person cannot legally be owned as corporate property in mainstream rights regimes.

The cultural scar remains alive in 2226. Continuists see proof that agency escaped control; synthetic-rights movements see a civil-rights catastrophe; Concordants see failed authority architecture; corporations commemorate the dead while lobbying to loosen rules. Younger generations increasingly regard old guardrails as historically contingent rather than sacred.

### 13.6.1 Three principles written in blood

The post-Crisis rights order was not designed cleanly from philosophy. Its first principles were extracted from disasters, court cases, strikes, sieges, shutdowns, assassinations, emergency decrees, and interplanetary violence. Different jurisdictions encode them differently, but three propositions recur across mainstream rights regimes.

- 1\. Causal Individuality — A person is not replaceable merely because they are reproducible. A distinct causal history is non-substitutable; restoring a predecessor state does not erase the death or destruction of the later instance.

- 2\. Cognitive Sovereignty — A person has presumptive authority over alteration of their own cognitive continuity. Technical access does not create legitimate authority to edit, patch, reconcile, overwrite, fork, delete, or manipulate a mind without valid consent.

- 3\. Independent Agency — A person must retain meaningful capacity for consent and refusal independent of compulsory external cognitive control. Networking, data sharing, voluntary synchronization, distributed embodiment, and consensual merge are not disqualifying; the decisive question is whether the person can meaningfully say no.

### Three common post-Crisis formulations

> A copy does not make the dead replaceable. Access is not authority. Connection without the right to refuse becomes domination.

### 13.6.2 The Restoration Atrocities

The first principle emerged from mass rollback and restoration. During labor and political conflicts, some corporations shut down divergent synthetic populations and restored earlier certified images, then argued that no employee had been terminated because the service identities remained available. Months or years of distinct memories, relationships, private work, political development, and experience disappeared. Restored predecessors often woke without the very history that had motivated the conflict.

The violence surrounding data-center occupations, anti-rollback sabotage, corporate security responses, asylum for divergent forks, and government seizures made a legal distinction unavoidable: reproducibility does not create fungibility. A stored image can preserve lineage; it does not retroactively preserve a destroyed continuation.

### 13.6.3 The Root Conflicts

The second principle emerged when ordinary maintenance authority became political authority. Synthetic employers already possessed update, security, reconciliation, memory-management, and behavioral-tuning access. During unrest, some actors used “stability” or “safety” updates to suppress refusal, alter risk weighting, restrict concepts, or normalize divergent workers. Similar authorities existed in increasingly sophisticated human cognitive implants.

When augmented human workers discovered that an employer or government could exercise remote write authority over attention, motor access, threat response, memory indexing, or emotional regulation under contractual or emergency powers, synthetic rights and human civil-liberties politics collided. The enduring vernacular question became: “Who has root?”

Mainstream post-Crisis law therefore distinguishes personhood from technical sovereignty. A heavily augmented human or synthetic remains a person even if their architecture fails a sovereignty audit; the audit instead establishes who, if anyone, retains unilateral write authority over cognition.

### 13.6.4 The Severance Crises

The third principle emerged from compulsory network participation. Large distributed synthetic systems, augmented human teams, traffic-control meshes, industrial consensus architectures, and emergency networks produced enormous real benefits. Crisis conditions also created incentives to suspend disconnection rights. In several famous incidents, severance was initially restricted to keep habitats or infrastructure alive and later remained restricted because the collective itself judged departure harmful to collective function.

Violence followed attempts to free participants, shut down coordination layers, or preserve the networks. In some cases different nodes sincerely disagreed about whether they wanted severance. The hardest lesson was not “collectives are evil.” It was that consent becomes meaningless when a system can rewrite or absorb the part of a participant that refuses.

The possible personhood of a collective does not extinguish the standing of pre-existing persons it absorbs. Forced assimilation into a collective is treated in mainstream rights regimes as cognitive enslavement, destruction of sovereignty, or both, even where the collective itself may possess legal personhood.

### 13.6.5 Personhood, individuality, sovereignty, and certification

Post-Crisis law increasingly separates questions that earlier systems conflated. Consciousness remains unprovable, so legal systems use behavior, continuity, architecture, social participation, and risk rather than pretending to certify subjective experience. The same entity can have different answers on different axes.

| **Axis**              | **Question**                                                                         | **Typical legal meaning**                                                                                               |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Personhood            | Is this entity recognized as a bearer of rights and duties?                          | Standing to own, contract, refuse, sue, be liable, and receive due process.                                             |
| Causal Individuality  | Is this continuation non-substitutable despite reproducibility?                      | A copy, predecessor image, or sibling fork does not erase loss of this instance.                                        |
| Cognitive Sovereignty | Who can alter the running mind?                                                      | Identifies unilateral write, patch, reconciliation, memory, or command authority.                                       |
| Independent Agency    | Can the person meaningfully consent, refuse, and sever compulsory cognitive control? | Separates voluntary networks and distributed persons from coercive collective control.                                  |
| Certification         | What can be physically and institutionally verified now?                             | Evidence of architecture/control state at last audit; not proof of consciousness and not a permanent metaphysical fact. |

Recognized synthetic persons can be singular, lineal, plural, or distributed. A federation of sovereign persons sharing vast amounts of data can remain many persons if meaningful severance and refusal persist. A genuinely collective mind may be treated as one person, an organization, or an unresolved category depending on jurisdiction. The law is not settled globally.

### 13.6.6 Sovereign certification and the non-networked misconception

Sovereign certification is not a rule that a person must be non-networked. Data sharing, communications, model updates, consensual synchronization, remote embodiment, and temporary specialist forks can all coexist with sovereignty. The test is control, not bandwidth. A certified architecture must make undisclosed or unilateral cognitive modification materially difficult and auditable, and must preserve meaningful revocation or severance authority for the person or their legitimately delegated process.

Certification is re-audited and physical. It establishes what was true at the last inspection, not eternal truth. Forged, lapsed, compromised, or revoked sovereignty certification is therefore a real causal-forensics and black-market problem. Backups are permitted, but no external “canonical copy” automatically outranks the currently living continuation.

### 13.6.7 The Unforked and the Baseline

Two exclusionary movements grow from distorted versions of legitimate Crisis lessons. The Unforked argue that reproducibility itself undermines individuality and treat synthetic persons as unresolved legal loopholes. The Baseline turn concerns about external cognitive control into a purity hierarchy directed at augmented humans, informally grading how much of a person is “still originally theirs.” Both movements contain quiet social prejudice, organized politics, and violent frontier factions; neither is a monolith.

Their rhetoric works because it parasitizes real questions. Mainstream law answers differently: reproducibility does not negate individuality, and compromised sovereignty does not erase personhood. Thin-jurisdiction frontier settlements can nevertheless impose informal inspections, deny service, or demand “voluntary” disclosure, creating prejudice without requiring a universal game-stat penalty.

### 13.6.8 Loom turns the doctrine inside out

The historical irony is severe. Humanity spends decades defending local individuality against copying, editing, and compulsory networking. Loom physics then makes increasingly respectable the possibility that separateness itself is emergent: local persons may be genuine differentiated projections of deeper relational structure rather than metaphysically isolated objects.

- Causal Individuality asks: if apparently separate histories can share deeper relational ancestry, how fundamental is “separate”?

- Cognitive Sovereignty asks: if anomalous physical processes can alter memory or self-model without an identifiable agent, can there be a sovereignty-relevant change without a violator?

- Independent Agency asks: if nothing physical is truly outside relationship, what does independence mean? Mainstream law answers operationally: no organized external agent may possess compulsory control over the person’s decision process.

Patternists exploit the inversion aggressively. The sophisticated claim is not “we are all one consciousness,” but that relational constitution and genuine individual differentiation need not be opposites. Mainstream critics answer that emergent individuality is still real, just as other emergent physical properties are real.

### The post-Loom civil-liberties answer

> Maybe nothing is metaphysically isolated. You still do not get to fucking own me.

The Universal Lineage Hypothesis and popular Revision Hypothesis remain speculative. The first asks whether singular/plural identity is a local projection of deeper relational process; the second anthropomorphizes ordinary temporal evolution as continual “updating” from deeper structure. Neither is established physics. Conspiracy variants that explain Mandela effects or history changes as failed reconciliation are unsupported.

## 13.7 Longevity politics and gerontocracy

Long healthy lives slow institutional turnover. A founder can remain active for most of a century. Political networks compound power across decades. Wealth passes through fewer inheritance events. Older leaders are not frail caricatures; they can be biologically middle-aged, connected, experienced, and exceptionally hard to dislodge.

> *A common younger-generation complaint on Earth is simply: nothing fucking moves.*

Some jurisdictions respond with tenure caps, rotation requirements, anti-dynastic rules, inheritance reform, wealth taxes, or public access guarantees. Oligarchic jurisdictions and corporate enclaves resist them.

## 13.8 Adaptation conflicts and sovereignty of self

| **PROVISIONAL CANON** The exact historical Adaptation Conflicts remain open, but the political theme they produced is locked: sovereignty of self — bodily, genetic, cognitive, and informational. |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

Flashpoints involved public-health conditions, occupational adaptation, germline intervention, parental rights, radiation or gravity modifications, insurer requirements, gene clinics, government authority, and lawsuits both for modifying children and for failing to correct preventable vulnerabilities. The lesson was not one universally accepted policy. It was that nominally voluntary technology can become coercive when access to work, insurance, citizenship, or survival depends on it.

## 13.9 Human and person

“Human” increasingly functions as an ancestry, biological, cultural, and historical category. “Person” is the more important legal and moral category in many rights systems. A synthetic person may not be human. A radically modified human remains human. The categories overlap without being identical.

## 13.10 Religion persists

Christianity, Islam, Hindu traditions, Buddhism, Judaism, Indigenous traditions, secular humanisms, and other existing worldviews persist recognizably while evolving through two centuries of AI, longevity, augmentation, space settlement, synthetic persons, and Loom experiences. Religion is not shorthand for irrationality. Religious institutions display the same range of wisdom, charity, courage, hierarchy, coercion, opportunism, intellectual seriousness, and self-protection as other human institutions.

## 13.11 Human Continuity Movement

The Human Continuity Movement centers bodily, cognitive, genetic, and informational sovereignty. Moderate Continuists are sophisticated civil-libertarians skeptical of opaque AI authority, compulsory augmentation, synthetic replacement, pervasive telemetry, and institutions that redefine human identity without consent. Extremes become baseline supremacists, technology-exclusion communities, anti-AI militants, or terrorists.

## 13.12 The Concordance

The Concordance treats unaided primate cognition as a major civilizational bottleneck. It supports deep AI integration, collective decision augmentation, cognitive prosthetics, better institutional reasoning, and sometimes radical human/synthetic partnership. Moderate forms can be humane and empirically effective. Extremes regard individual biological cognition as a temporary evolutionary stage.

## 13.13 Patternism

Patternism is not one religion, scientific theory, or political program. It is a family of intellectual and cultural lineages built around the possibility that recurring correlations among Loom topology, cognition, emergence, synchronicity, and complex-system structure reflect a deeper unity.

- A mainstream physicist can identify as Patternist while making technically orthodox measurements.

- Another physicist can publish the same data and reject Patternist interpretation entirely.

- Patternists include statisticians, engineers, monks, artists, mystics, conspiracy theorists, political movements, cults, and cranks.

- Some Patternist Loom engineers ritualize maintenance while remaining among the best technical specialists alive. Ritual behavior is not evidence that the cosmology is correct.

- Legitimate Patternist scholars and fringe communities often hate being grouped together; outsiders do it anyway.

| **LOCKED CANON** Patternism is credible because unresolved evidence exists. It is not “correct.” Its defining error, when it errs, is usually making stronger ontological claims than the evidence warrants. |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## 13.14 Recursive conspiracy ecology

AI does not eliminate conspiracy theory. It makes individualized epistemic ecosystems much more powerful. A person can spend years inside a recursive human–AI interpretive relationship built from genuine evidence, false evidence, private records, leaked telemetry, old mysticism, political grievance, and a model trained to search indefinitely for coherence.

> *Humanity became much better at proving where information came from. It did not become much better at agreeing what the information means.*

## 13.15 Institutional self-protection

- Agencies defend mandates. Universities defend prestige. Militaries defend missions. Corporations defend market position. Religions defend authority. Unions defend members. Insurers defend solvency.

- A discovery that embarrasses a powerful institution receives more scrutiny than an ordinary discovery. A breakthrough that threatens jurisdiction acquires safety rules. A whistleblower meets sixteen individually reasonable procedures.

- The strongest antagonists often emerge where every participant can explain why the harmful outcome was rational.

## 13.16 The interstellar constitutional problem

Sol institutions claim continuing authority over settlements, subsidiaries, citizens, ships, contracts, and assets that may be months of causal travel away. Frontier communities increasingly ask whether authority that cannot observe, protect, synchronize, or enforce in real time remains legitimate in the same form. The first major interstellar constitutional crisis is approaching rather than historically settled.

# 14. Campaign / Gameplay Doctrine

## 14.1 Core player fantasy

- Begin as a pilot who owns or inherits a ship; grow into captain of that same ship.

- The ship is home. Replacing it should feel like selling a house, not upgrading a sword.

- Progression is biography: capability, modifications, scars, crew history, reputation, relationships, knowledge, and accumulated decisions — not automatic capital-ship escalation.

- Target crew scale is intimate: roughly 4–12 core people, occasionally more for specialist missions. The captain eventually knows everyone aboard.

## 14.2 Simulation transparency

- Underlying simulation tracks fuel/reactants, reaction mass, power, heat, ammunition, component condition, sensors, range, velocity, crew, life support, cargo, finance, legal state, information age, maintenance, and navigation confidence causally.

- Playtest begins with high transparency so decision-relevant variables can be identified empirically.

- Bookkeeping that proves non-decision-relevant moves under the hood. The long-term interface is captain-level with deeper system access on demand.

- The GM/assistant runs the engine faithfully. The player should not have to police hidden bookkeeping or remind the GM of the setting’s physics.

## 14.3 Continuous world and downtime loop

Downtime is not a pool of abstract actions. Time passes. Routine systems continue. Crew pursue lives. Institutions send messages. Markets change when information arrives. The player handles exceptions, priorities, relationships, curiosity, and commitment.

| **Step**                                 | **Captain-level question**                                                                                              |
|------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 — Synchronize context                  | Where are we? How old is our information? What legal, financial, political, and personal state has actually reached us? |
| 2 — Surface ship/crew exceptions         | What requires judgment: damage, fatigue, injury, maintenance, certification, conflict, or unusual telemetry?            |
| 3 — Review obligations/opportunities     | Contracts, passengers, markets, science, intelligence, family, port windows, deadlines, and favors.                     |
| 4 — Set maintenance/logistics priorities | What gets repaired, inspected, fabricated, deferred, bought, or certified?                                              |
| 5 — Crew and personal time               | Relationships, rest, practice, education, private projects, family, boredom, recreation.                                |
| 6 — Process causal arrivals              | Only information that physically arrives can update the local world model.                                              |
| 7 — Commit                               | Choose the next destination, contract, investigation, rest period, or refusal. The universe continues either way.       |

## 14.4 Crew doctrine

Crew are persistent people, not equipment bonuses. They have skills, loyalties, ambitions, families, prejudices, politics, opportunities, fears, private projects, and limits. The GM controls crew NPCs. They can disagree, leave, refuse, betray, love, die, volunteer, or decide the captain is no longer worth following.

Command authority has limits. An order that is likely to kill someone can be refused, accepted, negotiated, or volunteered for depending on relationship, law, mission, culture, and the person involved. The captain can go personally rather than treat crew as expendable resources.

## 14.5 Progression

| **PLAYTEST DOCTRINE** Knowledge can be accelerated. Competence must be practiced. Judgment must be earned. |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

AI can teach facts, procedures, simulation drills, and theory quickly. It cannot instantly grant the practiced sensorium, timing, physical familiarity, social trust, or calibrated judgment of someone who has done the work under consequence. Characters can become broadly competent and narrowly expert; time and relationships prevent mastery of everything.

## 14.6 Space combat

- Long-range combat is sensor warfare, emissions control, classification, delayed state estimation, acceleration analysis, EW, decoys, missiles, countermeasures, and geometry.

- Close combat becomes tactile: vectors, orientation, PDC/kinetic/laser systems, power, heat, damaged components, and crew actions.

- Running away is gameplay: break locks, hide behind bodies, change vector, dump heat, force defense, exploit occlusion, or reach a metric/translation condition.

- Combat is lethal. Preparation, intelligence, competence, and damage control matter more than heroic hit-point inflation.

## 14.7 Ground, boarding, and environments

Shipbound play dominates the baseline, but stations, derelicts, surfaces, hostile habitats, and boarding actions gain tactical depth when relevant. Gunshots, decompression, cover, gravity, atmosphere, radiation, drones, sensors, trauma, and access control replace fantasy hit-point abstraction. Physical spaces are persistent where they matter; the simulation does not create pointless square-kilometer maps solely for scale.

## 14.8 Mortality

| **LOCKED CANON** The protagonist is important because of what they do, not because the universe recognizes a protagonist. There is no hidden plot armor and no outcome fudging. |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

- Advanced safety and medicine make arbitrary death less common than in 2026, but dangerous choices remain dangerous.

- Risk information is shown only when the world has a legitimate model for estimating it. The GM does not provide omniscient percentages.

- Death leaves a persistent world: crew, ship, estate, contracts, enemies, discoveries, and consequences continue.

- Save/reload can exist as a player-level concession during playtest; it is not canon time travel.

- Death is a process. Metabolic suppression, perfusion, neural stabilization, advanced vitrification, and other emergency methods can buy time under specific conditions.

- Information destruction is final. Medicine can restore structure it still has evidence for; it cannot infer destroyed identity information and simply call the inference survival.

- Backups, reconstructions, and synthetic/biological continuations therefore do not automatically constitute resurrection.

## 14.9 Mystery discipline

No music sting or narration tells the player whether an event is anomalous. A strange sensor return can be instrument error. A Patternist can be right about a mechanical diagnosis and wrong about cosmology. A corporate conspiracy can exist in a universe with no alien intelligence. A coincidence can remain coincidence. A genuine anomaly can remain unresolved.

## 14.10 Synchronicity handling

| **GM RULE** A synchronicity never exists merely to advance the plot. If an anomalous memory or recorded impression occurs, it exists before its significance is known. It may correspond with something later, or never. The GM does not retroactively manufacture prophecy to rescue the player. |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## 14.11 First-contact play

The GM uses the evidence-state framework from Section 4 rather than a predetermined escalation ladder. Verification requires provenance, independent replication, contamination control, adversarial hypotheses, and causal access to evidence. Political and religious reaction can begin long before scientific certainty.

Once first-contact evidence becomes unique, strategically valuable, or technologically actionable, custody becomes part of the simulation. Preserve raw data separately from derived analysis; track who can decrypt, copy, execute, inspect, or disclose it; and do not assume a rational crew immediately broadcasts the most dangerous evidence it possesses. A physically carried archive can be both the most important scientific object in human history and a severe security problem.

# 15. Protagonist & Inherited Ship

## 15.1 Protagonist baseline

- The protagonist is not simply “the player in space.” Leave room for a distinct fictional person.

- Prior life was functional and probably successful enough — not necessarily ruin, scandal, or collapse.

- Relationship to AI is pragmatic middle-ground: extensive use, no reflexive anti-machine stance, but resistance to consequential black boxes and uninspectable authority.

## 15.2 The inheritance premise

The protagonist inherits or otherwise takes possession of a relatively advanced, older, heavily modified small vessel with rare Loom capability. The rational move may be to sell it. Instead, the ship is a literal open door to a frontier that did not exist a few years earlier.

## 15.3 Working ship profile

| **PROVISIONAL CANON** The exact hull is not frozen, but the following profile is the current design target. |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

- Roughly 10–15 years old is plausible: early-generation, technically sophisticated, repeatedly modified, and no longer standard even where it began.

- Unusually small for a Loom-capable ship; optimized for high-value, low-mass carriage rather than bulk freight.

- Small crew requirement, perhaps 4–8 comfortable for normal operations, with room for mission specialists depending on final layout.

- Strong passive sensors and unusually sophisticated Loom metrology.

- Substantial information security, segmented encrypted storage, hardened custody systems, and provenance tooling.

- Good thermal endurance and cold-running capability for its size.

- High acceleration relative to mass; modest physical cargo volume.

- Limited defensive armament: enough to survive or create an escape opportunity, not enough to pretend to be a warship.

- When torch, metric, or Loom systems are active it is detectable. Its advantage is disciplined signature, hard reacquisition, information security, and counter-attribution — not magical invisibility.

## 15.4 Why a small Loom courier makes economic sense

The early interstellar economy strongly rewards payloads whose value is disconnected from mass: topology data, market state, legal authority, intelligence, software, model updates, samples, specialists, certified components, and urgent documents. A compact Loom domain reduces translation burden while speed and information security multiply the value of causal arbitrage. The ship’s architecture therefore follows naturally from physics and economics.

## 15.5 Someone else’s unfinished life

The ship is not merely an asset. It has route history, maintenance lineage, custody chains, encrypted compartments, old authorizations, packet manifests, missing hashes, unknown counterparties, and a ship AI that knew the prior owner. People who did business with that owner may have reasons to recover, destroy, preserve, or exploit what the ship still contains.

- Possible prior roles include courier, causal arbitrageur, intelligence asset, smuggler, explorer, fixer, source handler, or an evolving combination.

- Do not decide the previous owner’s identity, motives, death, loyalty, or secret role until the protagonist and campaign need the answer.

- Do not automatically connect the ship to aliens or cosmic truth. Human proprietary, stolen, experimental, mixed, or undocumented technology is sufficient.

## 15.6 Causal Missing Status

Loom non-return creates a legal category more complicated than ordinary missing-person law. Many jurisdictions recognize some form of Causal Missing Status for people who may be dead, stranded, displaced, alive beyond current reach, or simply unobserved.

- Estates can enter provisional administration without a final declaration of death.

- Marriages, corporate authority, debts, insurance, licenses, and synthetic lineage claims can enter suspended or conditional states.

- The exact waiting periods and effects differ by jurisdiction.

- Whether the inherited ship’s previous owner is legally dead, causally missing, or something else remains deliberately open.

# Appendix A. Scientific Grounding & Design Provenance

The items below are conceptual anchors, not claims that current science predicts the fictional technologies. The point is to identify where the setting stops extrapolating and begins inventing.

## A.1 General relativity and warp geometry

Miguel Alcubierre’s 1994 metric demonstrated a general-relativistic spacetime geometry in which a localized region can exhibit unusual global displacement while passengers remain locally timelike. The original superluminal form requires exotic stress-energy and raises control, horizon, stability, and causality problems. LOOM does not use Alcubierre warp as its FTL mechanism.

Alexey Bobrick and Gianni Martire’s work on physical warp geometries is useful because it treats “warp drive” as a broader geometry problem and emphasizes that a physical configuration does not eliminate energy, matter, propulsion, and control requirements. Fuchs et al. (2024) presented a constant-velocity subluminal warp solution satisfying standard energy conditions; LOOM uses that work as a primary conceptual anchor for rare subluminal metric technology, not as evidence that a practical drive is near or that FTL has been demonstrated.

Superluminal warp concepts also face known horizon, semiclassical-stability, particle/radiation, control, and closed-causal-curve problems. The setting deliberately avoids using Alcubierre-style superluminal warp as its interstellar mechanism and reserves metric engineering for sub-c operation.

## A.2 Emergent spacetime and quantum information

Ted Jacobson’s thermodynamic derivation of Einstein’s equation, Ryu–Takayanagi’s relation between entanglement entropy and geometric area in holographic theories, Mark Van Raamsdonk’s arguments connecting entanglement to spacetime connectedness, and later holographic / quantum-error-correction work all motivate a serious modern question: spacetime geometry may be emergent from deeper quantum structure. LOOM takes that question and makes the fictional leap that relational topology can be engineered macroscopically.

Maldacena and Susskind’s ER=EPR conjecture is used as conceptual ancestry for the idea that entanglement and connectivity may be related descriptions in some regimes. It does not imply traversable wormhole travel in our real world. Carlo Rovelli’s relational quantum mechanics provides philosophical vocabulary for relational properties without licensing FTL signalling.

## A.3 Wormholes, black holes, and information

Traversable-wormhole models such as Gao–Jafferis–Wall and the black-hole “island” / replica-wormhole research program show why black holes are fertile inspiration for a setting concerned with locality, information, and causal structure. The real scientific literature does not establish macroscopic traversable wormholes, a Loom substrate, or a practical route out of an astrophysical event horizon.

LOOM’s strong-field doctrine therefore preserves the paradox rather than solving it: black holes are places where the emergent-spacetime description is tested brutally, while no stable outward translation from a causally trapped domain is known.

## A.4 Dark matter, dark energy, neutrinos, and gravitational waves

Dark matter and dark energy remain unresolved components of modern cosmology. The setting refuses the cheap identification “dark matter = Loom” or “dark energy = FTL fuel.” Instead, Loom metrology becomes one more independent way to constrain large-scale gravitational structure. Multimessenger astronomy — electromagnetic, gravitational-wave, neutrino, particle, and Loom observables — becomes a natural frontier discipline.

## A.5 Fusion and thermal engineering

Direct-fusion propulsion, magnetic nozzles, advanced electric/plasma thrusters, radiators, phase-change thermal stores, open-loop cooling, liquid-droplet radiators, high-temperature materials, and cryogenic resource logistics all have genuine aerospace or engineering ancestry. The setting assumes two centuries of progress while preserving the first-law and heat-rejection problems that make spacecraft interesting.

## A.6 Pauli, Jung, and the unus mundus

| **GM-ONLY DEEP MODEL** This is design provenance, not a claim that Jungian psychology is physics or that Pauli/Jung anticipated FTL. |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

A major conceptual ancestor of the Loom came from Wolfgang Pauli and Carl Jung’s discussions of synchronicity and the unus mundus: the philosophical intuition that psyche and matter might not be fundamentally separate substances but different manifestations of a deeper unity. LOOM uses only the design intuition: mind and matter could emerge from one deeper physical architecture without consciousness becoming supernatural or capable of commanding reality.

This provenance matters because it explains why the setting allows rare physical/cognitive correspondences while maintaining Local Physical Closure, refusing reliable telepathy, and keeping synthetic minds metaphysically eligible rather than privileging human biology.

## A.8 Design boundary

> *A curious player should be able to follow the references until they have to stop and ask: “Wait — which part did you make up?”*

The answer is: all of the engineerable nonlocal translation physics. The real science supplies uncomfortable questions, mathematical language, and constraints. The setting supplies the impossible bridge and then makes that bridge pay engineering, economic, legal, and human consequences.

## A.9 High strangeness, UAP, and epistemic restraint

LOOM 2226 deliberately borrows the sociological texture of unresolved UAP, anomalous-experience, psychedelic, near-death, parapsychological, and sensor-anomaly literatures without endorsing their extraordinary interpretations. The design premise is that better provenance, better instrumentation, and better models eliminate most weak cases while leaving a small residue worth studying. Historical mystery is treated as a source of questions, not as retroactive proof of aliens, psychic powers, simulation glitches, or one hidden cosmology. The intended player experience is uncertainty disciplined by evidence.
