## What the available authoritative metadata/abstract supports

The paper formulates parametric asteroid-mining mission economics for costs, revenues, return masses, profit, NPV, mass payback ratio and a new break-even mass ratio (BEMR). It considers whole-asteroid return versus in-situ processing, single versus multiple return trips, and Earth-supplied, orbital-refuelled, or asteroid-derived propellant strategies. Its numerical study spans specific impulse from 450 to 3000 s and target delta-v up to 10 km/s.

These statements are sufficient to confirm model identity and intended scope. They are **not sufficient to reconstruct the equations faithfully**.
