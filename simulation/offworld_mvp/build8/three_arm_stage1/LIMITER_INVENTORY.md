## Recorded comparison, with causal limits

- `three_arm_stage1/RECOVERED_FLYBY_POLICY_FULL_CLOCK_REPORT_TEST_ONLY.json`: same full-clock setting: control **249 funded stages, 90 bodies, 3.735 model units**; ceilings-off `ABSTRACT_FALLBACK` **249, 90, 27.9247392292036391656**; ceilings-off `BLOCK` **67 bodies, 26.9797392292036391656**, **87,860** `INFEASIBLE_FLYBY_QUOTE` blocked attempts. These are **joint** policy comparisons, not a clean single-switch estimate of ceilings.
- `three_arm_stage1/RECOVERED_EXTRAPOLATE_FULL_CLOCK_REPORT_TEST_ONLY.json`: ceilings-off `EXTRAPOLATE_PROXY`: **249 stages, 90 bodies, 160.5597066416862082528** model units; **40** extrapolated charges costing **133.2349674124825690872**; **3,170** cash blocks, **116** financier concentration blocks; 23/23 formerly blocked bodies funded. Status `EXTRAPOLATED_TEST_ONLY` is not feasibility.
- `three_arm_stage1/ROUTE1_FLYBY_PRICE_PAIRED_REPORT.json` records the earlier paired ceilings comparison; use its exact case hashes and metrics rather than treating this inventory as a substitute.
- Where no clean A/B exists, **measured toggle effect = not measured**. Do not infer a governor is harmless from zero observed blocks.
