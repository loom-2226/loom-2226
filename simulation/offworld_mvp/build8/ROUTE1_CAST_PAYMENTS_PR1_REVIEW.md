LAUNCH and RUN are required because the nine previously omitted sponsors include both categories. The prices, categories and same-year delivery convention are TEST_ONLY choices, not real firm cost or orbital service schedules. Every eligible executor offers its authored category service quote. Ranking is **lowest price → preferred relationship → stable actor ID**. The event records *all considered offers*, not just the winning supplier.

## Conserved payment and paid failure

Each successful financier decision already transfers sponsor budget into a project cash account using Route 1 commitments and disbursements. The new opt-in settlement checks that **this same account** holds exactly the winning quote, records a work authorization, then transfers the money by a kernel `CAPEX` transaction to a supplier account owned by the real cast executor, parented to the original disbursement. Zero new banks, loans, lender treasuries or money sources. A same-owner project/supplier event (SpaceX, Blue Origin, etc.) is explicitly marked instead of treated as a third-party sale. An authored `DELIVERED` result produces one **TEST_ONLY WORK TOKEN**, not a physical resource or completed spacecraft mission. An authored `FAILED` result consumes the full payment (explicit full-payment-on-failure assumption) and produces **no** deliverable. The supplier balances and project cash reconcile exactly, and the kernel accounting invariants pass.
