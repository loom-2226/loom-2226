# BUILD 6 EXPANSION GATE DISPOSITION 001 — DEFER MULTIPLE ECONOMIES / ACTIVATE MULTIPLE BODIES

## Decision

The FRD Section 37 recommended progression states:

`... multiple projects -> multiple economies -> multiple bodies/resources ...`

and immediately requires:

> each expansion shall earn its complexity by demonstrating a causal need not adequately
> represented at the previous level.

Build 6A and Build 6B have structurally earned:

- multiple concurrent projects;
- finite shared capital;
- deterministic elapsed project activities;
- opportunity waiting;
- real prospecting/study spending;
- sunk cost;
- persistent knowledge;
- staged project-study maturity;
- defer / advance / abandon outcomes.

The next research question is now explicitly spatial/resource-opportunity dependent:

> how do Moon, Mars, Ceres and Bennu compete for finite exploration/study capital from
> 2026 through 2041 when they begin with different admitted evidence, travel/access timing,
> study lead times and information quality?

A second Earth economy is not required to answer that question.

Therefore:

**the multiple-economies rung is DEFERRED, not deleted, and the multiple-bodies/resources
rung is ACTIVATED for one bounded structural portfolio qualification.**

## Rationale

Adding a second economy before the named-body portfolio would introduce ownership,
counterparty and distributional machinery without materially changing the immediate causal
question.

The named-body experiment, by contrast, requires multiple bodies because:

- admissible 2026 evidence differs by body;
- target access/travel opportunity differs by body;
- useful next information actions differ by body;
- information arrival calendars differ by body;
- the same finite capital cannot be spent on all targets simultaneously.

The additional body complexity is therefore causally necessary.

## Deferred multiple-economy standing

This disposition does not waive the multiple-economies expansion permanently.

It remains explicitly deferred until a later question requires, for example:

- distinct Earth institutional capital sources;
- country-specific public investment;
- multiple terrestrial buyers;
- ownership/distribution consequences by economy;
- trade counterparties;
- policy intervention;
- country-level economic consequence coupling.

Nothing in Build 6C may simulate those effects by proxy.

## Scope guard

Build 6C may activate multiple named Solar bodies/resources only for:

- project/prospect identity;
- admitted evidence;
- project-study actions;
- time/opportunity differences;
- capital competition;
- knowledge/maturity progression.

It may not activate:

- multiple Earth economies;
- endogenous macroeconomics;
- country policy;
- endogenous prices;
- coupled Earth/offworld economies;
- inter-body commodity trade;
- settlement;
- mine construction unless separately earned.

## FRD standing

This is a governed interpretation of the FRD's recommended progression and causal-need rule.

The guiding FRD remains byte-unchanged.

The deferred rung shall be preserved in all Build 6C validation and closure records.
