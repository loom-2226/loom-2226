# Phase 3B MVP Accounting Boundary Candidate 0.1

**Status:** DESIGN CANDIDATE / PRE-CONTRACT / SINGLE-AUTHORITY

## 1. Purpose

Define what the MVP accounting system represents so that explicit accounts are not mistaken for a complete macrofinancial economy.

## 2. In-boundary state

The MVP may represent:

- identified funds/project-cash/supplier/boundary accounts;
- balanced transactions;
- commitments, disbursements and lapses;
- expenditure;
- WIP and capitalization;
- depreciation/amortization;
- explicit ownership claims;
- retained/returned/reinvested surplus flows;
- FixedCapitalFormationEvent state;
- EarthImpact shadow entries;
- signed external clearing positions;
- physical inventory paired with relevant sale/consumption transitions.

## 3. Out-of-boundary mechanisms

Unless separately added, the MVP does not model:

- commercial bank balance sheets;
- endogenous money/credit creation;
- securities pricing/secondary markets;
- interest-rate term structure;
- insurer balance sheets;
- taxes;
- household balance sheets;
- complete national accounts;
- endogenous saving/consumption closure;
- bankruptcy law/recovery hierarchy;
- exchange rates;
- formal residency-based GFCF for offworld polities.

## 4. EARTH_MARKET_v0

EARTH_MARKET_v0 is a SYSTEM/deferred external clearing boundary.

Its signed balance is a net boundary position, not a deposit account, treasury balance, or infinite stock of liquid money.

Material purchased through the boundary must remain explicit inventory until consumed, transformed, installed, exported or otherwise disposed by a declared transition.

## 5. Earth reference relation

Earth INVESTMENT_REAL_PROXY / reference FCF remains an immutable modeled capital-formation scale, not spendable cash.

FINANCING_AUTHORIZATION and the Earth resource-allocation proxy remain explicit MVP abstractions. Expenditure, not commitment alone, drives modeled FCF/resource displacement effects.

## 6. Ownership

Moving funds into an offworld/local vehicle does not extinguish beneficial ownership. Claims must reconcile to 100% unless an explicit dilution, transfer, writeoff or other claim-changing event occurs.

## 7. Supply

A supplier account balance does not itself prove production capacity. Local/offworld supply requires explicit capacity or a registered abstraction. Earth-supplied expenditure is constrained by the declared Earth allocation proxy, not inferred from supplier cash.

## 8. Non-claims

A financially balanced MVP run is not evidence that the modeled economy is macroeconomically complete, solvent under real accounting standards, or empirically calibrated.

## 9. Replacement path

Later financial-system modules may replace abstractions without changing the core separation:

financing decision -> commitment -> disbursement -> expenditure -> WIP/FCF -> asset -> production/revenue -> surplus disposition.
