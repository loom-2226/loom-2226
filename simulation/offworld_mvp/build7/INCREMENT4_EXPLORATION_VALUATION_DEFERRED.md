# Deferred Build 7 Increment 4 issue: competing information opportunities

Increment 3 proves that a public actor can acquire decision-relevant information
endogenously. Its deterministic tie-break among otherwise equivalent eligible
REMOTE opportunities is bounded behavior, not a substantive priority model.

Increment 4 must reassess how actors value competing information-acquisition
opportunities once admitted economic, strategic, logistical, capital, risk, and
other decision-relevant context exists. This record does not authorize any of
those mechanisms or supply scores, weights, or destination preferences.
