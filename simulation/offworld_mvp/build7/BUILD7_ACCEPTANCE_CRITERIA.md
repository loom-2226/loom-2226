## 5. Actor-visible opportunity generation
### NEW

Before a project exists, Build 7 may derive **candidate opportunities**.

An opportunity is a transient/auditable decision input, not a project.

Candidate opportunities may use only actor-visible or legitimately modeled information such as:

- known body/location identity;
- admitted REAL evidence;
- actor beliefs/posteriors;
- relational accessibility;
- available technology/capability;
- estimated mission/project economics;
- risk;
- current demand/structural pressure where applicable;
- existing complementary infrastructure/capital;
- existing relationships/services;
- available financing state.

Opportunity generation shall not use:

- hidden resource quantity;
- hidden recoverability;
- hidden generated site attractiveness;
- future observations;
- future project outcome;
- authored body-importance scores;
- legacy Atlas/canon outcomes as an answer sheet.

\`UNKNOWN\` remains \`UNKNOWN\`. Required unknown inputs shall not be replaced by convenient defaults merely so an opportunity can qualify.

Opportunity derivation should remain a pure/reconstructable function of current state where practical. Unchosen opportunities need not become persistent entities.

## 7. Endogenous exploration
### NEW

The public/institutional explorer shall decide **whether and where to explore**.

The minimum decision basis is consistent with existing Mission/Knowledge research:

\`actor knowledge + accessibility + capability + finite budget + mission cost + expected value of information\`

The actor may choose:

- one legitimate remote observation mission;
- a legitimate higher-quality follow-on observation/prospecting mission where prerequisites exist;
- another candidate body;
- no mission.

A positive observation may make a later mission or commercial opportunity more attractive.

It shall not create a deposit or automatically create a project.

Remote and surface prospecting semantics already qualified in Build 7 remain required.

Only the authorized WORLD_SIM observation mechanism may consult hidden physical truth.

## 8. Earth-Offworld Capital Coupling Layer
### NEW

Build 7 shall use the agreed reduced country-level capital-to-cash abstraction rather than a banking, fiscal or capital-markets simulator.

For each **modeled** country \(c\), maintain:

\[
F_{c,t}
\]

cash available for new Offworld financing;

\[
X_{c,t}
\]

outstanding country-origin Offworld capital exposure;

\[
R_{c,t}
\]

realized Offworld cash returned during the period;

and:

\[
S_{c,t}
\]

strategic pressure.

For Build 7 development, this abstraction is accepted as the financing bridge. Its final Earth-side semantic refinement may be addressed after the emergent loop is working, provided financial conservation and explicit lineage are maintained.

No requirement exists to activate all 80 Earth economies in Build 7.

## 9. Earth economic capacity -> Offworld cash
### NEW

The current promoted Earth authority remains the source of annual:

- investment \(I_{c,t}\);
- productive capital \(K_{c,t}\);
- value added \(VA_{c,t}\);
- gross output;
- population/demographic state;
- relevant labor state.

These values do not become spendable cash directly.

The minimum mobilization abstraction is:

\[
M_{c,t}=I_{c,t}\times m_{c,t}
\]

with:

\[
0 \le m_{c,t} \le m^{max}
\]

and:

\[
m_{c,t}=f(O_{c,t},S_{c,t})
\]

where:

- \(O\) is commercial opportunity;
- \(S\) is strategic pressure.

Mobilization shall use explicit, versioned parameters and enough persistence/smoothing to avoid meaningless annual twitching.

No direct rule of the form:

\`Earth investment = financier account balance\`

is allowed.

### NEW

- expected return/value;
- required capital;
- operating-cost expectation;
- transport/access cost;
- risk/uncertainty;
- relevant demand.

The country-level signal:

\[
O_{c,t}=g(\text{eligible actor-visible opportunities})
\]

must distinguish:

- credible attractive opportunities;
- weak/unattractive opportunities;
- no qualified opportunity.

The aggregation must not reward a country merely because more bodies were enumerated.

A bounded top opportunity, small top-\(k\) aggregate, or similarly narrow rule is acceptable.

No universal Solar attractiveness score is required or desired.

## 11. Strategic pressure remains separate from commercial economics
### NEW

Strategic pressure shall remain a thin country-policy input, not a political simulator.

Minimum conceptual form:

\[
S_{c,t}=BaseSalience_{c,t}+RacePressure_{c,t}
\]

with race pressure derived from actor-visible/perceived rival capability, for example:

\[
RacePressure_{c,t}
=
\sum_j
Attention_{c,j}
\times
Visibility_{c,j,t}
\times
PositivePerceivedGap_{c,j,t}
\]

The epistemic distinction must remain:

\[
C^{actual}\neq C^{perceived}\neq C^{public}
\]

Strategic pressure may increase willingness to mobilize economic capacity. It shall not silently rewrite project expected returns or reveal hidden truth.

Commercial and strategic motivations shall remain distinguishable in financing provenance:

\`motivation = COMMERCIAL | STRATEGIC\`

Build 7 qualification need only demonstrate the seam with a bounded actor-visible strategic-pressure fixture. It does not require a geopolitical simulation.

## 12. Cash, commitments, exposure and returns
### NEW

Available financing must carry forward rather than disappear at annual boundaries.

Conceptually:

\[
F^{open}_{c,t}
=
F^{close}_{c,t-1}
+
M_{c,t}
+
R^{retained}_{c,t}
\]

Existing commitments may reserve available cash.

Commitment is not Earth diversion.

On actual disbursement:

\[
F_c\downarrow
\]

\[
X_c\uparrow
\]

Failed or written-off investment reduces outstanding exposure without inventing compensating return cash.

Actual Offworld cash returns contribute to \(R_c\).

Outstanding exposure shall reduce through recovered basis and/or explicit writeoff rather than assuming every returned dollar is principal.

Returned cash may be retained for Offworld reinvestment or returned to Earth under one small declared rule.

All new Earth-origin Offworld financing must reconcile to this coupling layer or an explicitly declared opening balance.

## 25. Emergence qualification
### NEW

Build 7 completion requires deterministic qualification of the following cases.

### A. No-target GENESIS

Start a normal campaign with:

- world seed;
- pinned authorities;
- declared parameters;

and no body/project target.

Verify:

- complete hidden WORLD exists;
- no development project exists;
- no settlement exists;
- no body is privileged by initialization;
- persist/reopen succeeds.

### B. Legitimate no-action case

Construct a case with insufficient opportunity/capital/strategic pressure.

Verify:

- no project is created;
- no Offworld disbursement occurs;
- \`WAIT\`/\`DECLINE\` is legitimate;
- simulation time advances and persists.

### C. Endogenous exploration selection

Provide several legitimately eligible exploration alternatives.

Verify:

- the actor chooses using only actor-visible inputs;
- only selected mission(s) execute;
- unchosen bodies remain untouched;
- replay produces the same choice/history.

### D. Hidden-truth firewall twin

Run otherwise-identical hidden worlds with identical initial actor information.

Verify:

- pre-observation actions are identical;
- hidden truth alone does not alter mobilization, destination choice or project creation;
- histories may diverge only after a legitimate distinguishing observation.

### E. Visible-information sensitivity twin

Hold hidden truth and deterministic decision keys fixed while changing one legitimate actor-visible input.

Verify that the qualified opportunity set or Agent choice may change for a traceable reason.

This pairs with D to prove that actor-visible information matters while hidden truth does not leak.

### F. Capital gating

Verify at minimum:

- zero mobilization/available finance prevents new funded Offworld activity;
- attractive commercial opportunity can increase mobilization within \(m^{max}\);
- strategic pressure can independently affect mobilization;
- commitments reserve but do not count as Earth diversion;
- disbursement changes \(F\) and \(X\);
- actual returns/losses change later financing state.

### G. Endogenous project creation

Verify:

- candidate opportunities can exist without projects;
- unchosen opportunities remain non-projects;
- sponsor \`INITIATE_PROJECT\` creates exactly one project;
- project creation is causally linked to its information/opportunity/decision;
- replay creates no duplicate project;
- created project enters the existing financing/lifecycle machinery.

### H. Path dependence

Demonstrate at least one case where a legitimate early realized difference changes a later opportunity or choice through persisted state.

The divergence must be traceable, for example through:

- changed belief;
- loss/return and available capital;
- created infrastructure;
- changed local demand/accessibility.

### I. Ten-year emergent chain

At least one 2026–2035 qualification run must begin with:

- no selected Offworld target;
- no pre-created development project;

and, through Agent decisions, legitimately reach:

\`exploration -> information -> project initiation -> financing -> development -> operation\`

where conditions legitimately permit.

If the existing qualified economics naturally permit deeper progression inside the ten-year run, continue through extraction/market/reinvestment/settlement/transport.

The ten-year qualification shall **not** force settlement merely to satisfy a calendar target. The inherited targeted deep-chain regression remains the proof that the already-qualified downstream settlement/transport machinery works.
