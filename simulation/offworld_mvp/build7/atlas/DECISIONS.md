## Causal sequencing subtleties

- **A choice helper is not an Agent policy epoch.** PUB target filtering and minimum-cost choice happen before the persisted explorer policy request.
- **The sponsor annual opportunity decision is not itself investment authorization.** `CONSIDER_PROSPECTING` leads to re-derivation, drift checks and a separate Sponsor prospecting policy.
- **The annual conductor records `eligible_for_investment` before processing the year's studies.** A study review completed during that year cannot open an annual opportunity until a later year.
- **One project per regional identity, not one per body.** Existing project identities are excluded from candidate generation; other regions remain candidates.
- **Policy parameters are not empirically calibrated preferences.** Portfolio and study-review policy sources explicitly describe test-only structural semantics. They can be active in the generated path without becoming empirical economic or scientific models.

## Evidence standard

The code supports the branches above. The completed console campaign establishes only the branches actually observed in that run. The atlas must never conflate the two.
