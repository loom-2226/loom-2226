## Identity and access boundaries

- `PUB`, `SPN`, and `FIN` are **simulation Agent identities**, not PostgreSQL users. `_agent_logins()` can map them to `agent_pub`, `agent_spn`, `agent_fin` database sessions; the generated-world conductor explicitly opens PUB and SPN login sessions at policy boundaries. See [`generated_campaign.py:575–584`](https://github.com/loom-2226/loom-2226/blob/66c641d25e641e0aff77940a8cadbf1653247113/simulation/offworld_mvp/build7/generated_campaign.py#L575-L584).
- `EARTH_MARKET`, `SUP`, `TRANSPORT_PROVIDER`, and `COUNTRY:USA` appear as **account owners/boundary identities**, not `AgentState` entries in this constructor. They must not be counted as independent Agents or given invented policies.
- The conductor, policy worker, kernel system, `WORLD_SIM`, and World Authority are **execution or authority layers**, not additional Agents. A `NO_ACTION` summary is not necessarily an Agent's WAIT decision.
- `FINANCE`, `EXTRACT`, `SELL`, etc. in the capability set denote **permission/potential**, not evidence of execution. The targeted Build 7 path has additional policies and accounts but is outside this generated-world register.
- Per-body material priors and beliefs belong to PUB and SPN separately. Hidden `wa_world` material truth is accessed only by authorized WORLD_SIM observation transitions, then becomes Agent evidence through observation and publication. The register describes policy access, **not a claim that an Agent directly reads the sealed PostgreSQL rows**.
