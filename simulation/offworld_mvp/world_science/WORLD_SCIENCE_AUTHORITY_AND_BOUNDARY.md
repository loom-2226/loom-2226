# World Science Authority and Boundary

## Purpose
This package supplies empirically grounded constraints for Offworld celestial bodies while preventing scientific evidence, fictional WORLD truth and realized simulation history from collapsing into one ontology.

## Scientific plane
Scientific assertions use the laboratory ontology `REAL_EVIDENCE`, `MODEL_INFERENCE`, `DERIVED`, and where appropriate `REALIZED_OBSERVATION`. Evidence retains source lineage, uncertainty, spatial scope, temporal support and representativeness.

Valid scope includes BODY, REGION, FOOTPRINT, LOCAL_SITE, SAMPLE and MODEL_DOMAIN. UNKNOWN, conflicting evidence and competing models are legitimate states.

## Hard firewall
`HIDDEN_WORLD` and `SCENARIO` are forbidden as scientific assertions. A local detection is not a global abundance. A sample is not automatically body-representative. A material detection is not an accessible resource. The Offworld resource distinction remains:

`R_in_situ != R_accessible != R_recoverable != R_reserve`

No generated deposit, grade, stock, recoverability or reserve may be promoted to empirical Solar evidence.

## Navigation boundary
Authoritative astrodynamics remain with governed JPL/NAIF/SPICE/Horizons sources and kernels. The science database records identities, provenance and coverage needed to bind evidence to bodies/regions/epochs; it is not a replacement ephemeris.

## Generation boundary
Real science may constrain BODY and REGION strongly and may constrain FOOTPRINT, LOCAL_SITE or SAMPLE where observations genuinely support it. It generally stops above fictional DEPOSIT truth. Future generators may select a scientifically admissible hidden realization, but must preserve the empirical UNKNOWN.

## Agent boundary
This package does not grant Agent knowledge. Runtime admission must separately determine which evidence or observations are available to a particular Agent perspective at a particular time. WORLD truth is never directly queryable by Agents.

## Authority
The guiding Offworld FRD governs runtime requirements. This package is a build-independent empirical dependency beneath that architecture. A numbered build may pin a version/hash, but may not silently redefine its scientific meaning.
