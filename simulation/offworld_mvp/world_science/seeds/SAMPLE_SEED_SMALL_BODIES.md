# Sample Seed: Small-Body Campaign

This is a representative specialization of `RESEARCH_SEED_TEMPLATE.md`, distilled from the asteroid campaigns.

```text
TARGETS: a bounded set of SPICE-supported asteroids/small bodies.

Emphasize:
- canonical NAIF identity and frame/ephemeris coverage;
- shape, dimensions, mass/GM, density, spin and uncertainty;
- taxonomy/spectroscopy without treating class as exact mineralogy;
- radar/thermal constraints;
- regolith/boulder/crater morphology at observed scope;
- returned samples at SAMPLE scope only;
- rubble-pile/monolith/interior alternatives as MODEL_INFERENCE;
- porosity and mechanical properties only where supported;
- observational coverage and unobserved terrain;
- mission footprints and shape/spatial products.

Hostile checks:
returned sample != whole-body composition;
spectral detection != abundance;
bulk density != unique porosity/internal architecture;
metal-rich != mineable metal reserve;
image availability != detailed geology;
unknown density/interior remains UNKNOWN.

Assess generator readiness for coherent whole-body latent structure and correlated project sites, but populate no hidden WORLD, project_site or deposit rows.
```
