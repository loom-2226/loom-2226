# Sample Seed: Sparse Outer-System Campaign

Representative specialization distilled from Uranian and Pluto-system work.

```text
TARGETS: flyby-limited or otherwise sparsely characterized outer-system bodies.

Emphasize:
- actual imaging/measurement footprint;
- uncertainty in size, mass, density and rotation;
- barycentric/circumbinary dynamics where applicable;
- volatile/atmosphere evidence with epoch support;
- spectral detections at supported scope;
- observed hemisphere != global geology;
- sparse evidence != permission to interpolate precision.

Create explicit observed-coverage regions/footprints where useful. Do not create matching regions/sites merely for structural symmetry.

Hostile checks:
unimaged hemisphere remains unknown;
mass upper bound != measured mass;
derived density retains uncertainty and input lineage;
surface ice interpretation != bulk composition;
time-variable atmosphere != timeless BODY property;
captured/origin history remains model interpretation;
small-body records may legitimately contain much less information than richly observed bodies.

The generator-readiness result may terminate high in BODY/REGION hierarchy. Do not fill SITE/DEPOSIT gaps.
```
