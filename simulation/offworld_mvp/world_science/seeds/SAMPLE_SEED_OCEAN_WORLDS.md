# Sample Seed: Ocean-World Campaign

Representative specialization distilled from the Galilean and Saturnian moon campaigns.

```text
TARGETS: ocean-world candidates and active icy satellites.

Preserve the evidence chain:
OBSERVATION
-> measured physical constraint
-> DERIVED constraint
-> interior MODEL_INFERENCE
-> ocean hypothesis/model.

Research:
- gravity/libration/magnetic induction;
- geology and tectonics at mapped scope;
- ice/non-ice surface composition;
- plume observations and epoch;
- thermal anomalies;
- atmosphere/exosphere/plasma environment;
- competing shell/interior/ocean models;
- mission spatial products and footprints.

Hostile checks:
plume composition != directly measured bulk ocean composition;
magnetic induction != directly observed ocean geometry;
ocean hypothesis != REAL_EVIDENCE;
inferred depth/salinity/thickness != measurement;
active plume at epoch T != timeless global activity;
water/ocean != accessible resource.

Keep multiple scientifically admissible models. UNKNOWN is preferred to false consensus.
```
