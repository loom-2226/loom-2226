# Repeatable Research / Ingestion Workflow
1. Bootstrap live GitHub authority and record commit.
2. Open predecessor SQLite databases read-only; inventory identities, assertions, coverage, source artifacts and material evidence.
3. Research independently from primary mission/data products, agencies and peer-reviewed mission science. Do not use predecessor values as an answer key.
4. Register source. If bytes are acquired, hash exact artifact; otherwise mark REMOTE_REFERENCE_ONLY and do not invent a hash.
5. Create spatial support before spatial assertion: region, sample, observation or spatial product.
6. Insert research only as CANDIDATE.
7. Preserve numeric uncertainty exactly where source supports it; otherwise use qualitative/detection/model forms.
8. Run semantic validation: identity, FK, scope, provenance, ontology firewall, candidate/preferred, detection/abundance, unknown preservation.
9. Reconcile conflicts without deleting alternatives.
10. Only a separate deterministic/adjudicated admission process may promote candidate evidence. This lab implements no promotion.
11. Rebuild from frozen script/schema and compare database hash.
12. Human/hostile review before any future LOOM admission.
## Mercury/Venus expansion note
For atmospheric evidence, assign a typed vertical coordinate before ingestion. ALTITUDE and PRESSURE_LEVEL are atmospheric support; DEPTH_BELOW_SURFACE is geological/subsurface support. Never reuse one merely because its numeric representation fits another. Preserve observation (for example radar backscatter change) separately from interpretation (for example active lava flow).

## Natural-satellite hierarchy
When predecessor navigation identity uses a system barycenter as parent, preserve that fact in source lineage but map the lab's geographic/body hierarchy to the physical parent body when the barycenter is not itself represented as a WORLD body. Formation/origin hypotheses never alter body identity.

## Fluid planets, rings and time-variable evidence
For fluid planets, do not invent a solid-surface geology layer. Bind atmospheric evidence to pressure/altitude support and observation epoch when material. Probe paths remain LOCAL_SITE. Deep atmosphere and interior structure inferred from gravity, winds, seismology or equations of state remain MODEL_INFERENCE.
Represent rings using generic associated-structure regions. Do not flatten ring mass/composition into body material inventory.
For evolving atmospheres and volatile surfaces, preserve observation intervals and distinguish observation from seasonal/transport models.

## Small-body exemplar discipline
Taxonomic class and meteorite analogue are comparative evidence, not bulk assays. Bulk density plus assumed grain density may constrain porosity only as MODEL_INFERENCE. Irregular-body shape products remain external registered products. Mission-local/flyby evidence retains FOOTPRINT or LOCAL_SITE scope. Returned samples retain SAMPLE scope unless an explicit supported inference broadens representativeness.
Navigation authority is referenced, not expanded into ephemeris time series: prefer mission SPICE kernels during mission/encounter coverage and governed JPL Horizons/generated SPKs outside those intervals.

## Time-variable and interior-rich worlds
For active or transient phenomena, preserve REGION/FOOTPRINT plus observation epoch before synthesizing body-level behavior. For ocean worlds, record the chain observation -> derived physical constraint -> MODEL_INFERENCE rather than promoting the inferred layer to REAL_EVIDENCE. Future realization may select among admissible model families, but that selected hidden state is outside this science lab.

## Dense atmosphere / hydrology workflow
For atmospheric profiles, bind observations to altitude or pressure support and epoch/trajectory. For surface-atmosphere cycles, preserve individual cloud/rain/channel/lake/sea observations at their actual scope before any BODY-level DERIVED synthesis. In-situ landing measurements remain LOCAL_SITE. Regional liquid geometry never implies global inventory or economic accessibility.

## Sparse-data workflow
For flyby-limited bodies, create explicit observed-coverage regions/footprints and attach morphology to those scopes. Do not create matching REGION/SITE detail for symmetry. For poorly constrained mass or density, preserve bounds or UNKNOWN rather than midpoint estimates. For time-variable atmospheres/activity, bind evidence to observation epochs before deriving any longer-lived interpretation.
