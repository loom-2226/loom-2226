## Epistemic firewalls

> Convergence raises priority, not truth.

> Authenticated artifact is not authenticated interpretation.

> Unresolved is not extraordinary.

> Government interest is not government validation.

> Multiple retellings are not multiple independent sources.

Anomaly reports, media narratives, psi results, consciousness studies, high-strangeness cases and material claims may generate research questions or methods. They may not calibrate LOOM fictional physics, M1/M2/M3, hidden topology, propulsion, or any physical parameter.
