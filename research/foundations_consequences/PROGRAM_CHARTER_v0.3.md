## 1. Mission

LOOM Foundations & Consequences runs five porous but epistemically distinct lanes:

1. **A — Empirical / Mathematical:** what can be derived, measured, falsified or formally constrained?
2. **B — Speculative / Synthetic:** what is worth constructing and exploring rigorously even when not established?
3. **C — Civilizational / Fictional:** what happens when humans, synthetics and institutions live with those possibilities?
4. **D — Adversarial / Wild:** what alternative assumptions, failures and negative controls expose hidden weaknesses?
5. **P — Phenomenological / Anomaly:** what is actually reported, measured, recurrent, correlated or experienced before deciding what caused it?

These are co-equal discovery engines with different burdens of proof.

> **We can go anywhere. We must know what kind of claim we are making while we are there.**

## 3. Main roster versus ancestral lineage

A squad roster is intentionally biased toward living, active and potentially contactable people. It answers: **whose current work would we ask to attack or enrich this problem now?**

An ancestral lineage is unrestricted by era or living status. It answers: **which prior thinkers, experiments, critiques and failed programs materially created the question or method?**

Ancestry is allowed to overlap squads. A person can also be a historical ancestor in one dimension and a current bridge in another.

Case/witness nodes are separate again: their claims and provenance may be important even when they supply no methodological authority.

## 4. Claim discipline

The core epistemic stack remains:
- **E** — established physics/mathematics;
- **A** — analogy / scientific or mathematical ancestry;
- **P** — explicit fictional postulate;
- **C** — constitutive closure / canon calibration;
- **D** — derived result;
- **O** — open problem.

The research corpus may additionally tag imported material:
- **H** — heterodox technical hypothesis;
- **M** — metaphysical/philosophical stimulus;
- **R** — red-team / negative control;
- **F** — fiction/cultural ancestry;
- **PH** — phenomenological report/corpus that does not establish ontology.

A claim may not silently change class because it moved between squads or appeared repeatedly.

## 6. P-lane workstreams

### P1 — Anomaly Registry & Evidence Taxonomy
Build a source/provenance-first grammar for anomalous events: witness, sensor, custody, environment, replication, contamination, independent channels, alternative explanations and residual state.

### P2 — Consciousness / Memory Phenomenology
Historical and modern descriptive work relevant to TAAC, TAAM, dissociation, altered states, NDE-like phenomenology and anomalous-memory claims. The aim is phenotype and methodology, not metaphysical settlement.

### P3 — Instrumented Physical Anomaly
Study real-world methodological examples such as Hessdalen, aviation UAP investigation and other sensor-first residuals to improve LOOM's instrumentation doctrine.

### P4 — Cross-Domain Correlation
Study the difficult case class in which a cognitive report and an independently instrumented physical anomaly coincide. Default explanation includes chance, selection, contamination and common cause. A residual may be retained without becoming one ontology.

### P5 — Historical Case Reanalysis / GHOST Library
Preserve influential cases, witnesses, hoaxes, dead ends, controversies and methodological lessons. Bob Lazar belongs here as a current/high-impact case node even though he is also given a tagged roster seat for discussion convenience.

## 7. Three-squad convergence gate

A topic receives **CONVERGENCE PRIORITY** when all three conditions are independently met:

1. **Freaks With Clipboards:** identify a recurrent/well-provenanced pattern or unresolved observational class.
2. **Respectably Unsupervised:** generate a structural hypothesis or reframing that makes discriminating predictions or distinctions possible.
3. **Adults With Red Pens:** formulate null models, observables, independent tests or a hard kill.

Convergence changes **research priority**, not posterior truth by fiat. Three interesting traditions pointing at the same question are still not three independent measurements.
