# LOOM 2226 — Current Intellectual Lineage Audit v1.0

**Status:** RESEARCH / DESIGN PROVENANCE AUDIT — NON-CANON — NON-RUNTIME  
**Date:** 7 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This audit compares the three-squad lineage model against current LOOM research/canon-facing material. The objective is **not** to rewrite locked canon on a research branch. It is to identify where LOOM already inherits external ideas, where attribution is strong, and where the next normal revision should add source-level provenance.

### Corrected architecture
- current/live reviewer **roster**;
- unrestricted **ancestral lineage**;
- current **bridge members**;
- **case/witness nodes** that belong to evidence/provenance without being treated as methodological authorities.

## 2. Executive finding

LOOM is already substantially shaped like the three-squad architecture.

The surprising finding is that the third squad does not require adding a paranormal layer to LOOM. Current CANON I already contains a mature **Strangeness Problem** with:
- physical residuals;
- consciousness residuals;
- cross-domain events;
- competing ordinary and exotic explanations;
- explicit selection/measurement effects;
- a firewall against common-cause overreach.

Likewise, Pauli/Jung are already explicitly credited as GM-only design provenance for the *unus mundus* intuition. The required work is therefore **attribution completion and lineage reconstruction**, not ontological expansion.

### Current state
CANON I explicitly names Wolfgang Pauli and C. G. Jung, synchronicity and *unus mundus*, and explicitly states that this is design provenance rather than physics or anticipation of FTL.

### Assessment
**KEEP. This is exactly the right epistemic framing.**

### Next-normal-revision recommendation
Add a compact source note identifying the actual lineage rather than only the names:
- Jung, **Synchronicity: An Acausal Connecting Principle** (1952);
- Jung & Pauli, **The Interpretation of Nature and the Psyche** (1952);
- Meier (ed.), **Atom and Archetype: The Pauli/Jung Letters, 1932–1958** (English ed. Princeton, 2001);
- Atmanspacher, **The Pauli–Jung Conjecture and Its Relatives** (2020) as modern formal descendant, explicitly marked later scholarship rather than original provenance.

### Why
The current canon has the idea right; the source trail should be strong enough that a future collaborator can reconstruct why the design move exists.

## 4. CANON I §6.21 Strangeness Problem: STRONG CONTENT / UNDER-CREDITED METHODOLOGY

### Current state
The section already says:
- improved provenance and modeling eliminate much weak anomaly material;
- residual does not imply one common cause;
- physical, conscious and cross-domain residuals are distinct;
- ordinary explanations remain powerful;
- five competing hypotheses are retained;
- old UAP/paranormal/mystical records can generate new questions but are not retroactively validated.

### Ancestral lineage that should be recorded in design notes
- **Charles Fort** — preserve excluded/anomalous observations, but LOOM adds much stronger controls.
- **J. Allen Hynek** — classify reports and separate strangeness from evidential/probability quality.
- **Peter Sturrock** — category-specific scientific review of physical evidence without requiring a preferred ontology.
- **Jacques Vallée** — high-strangeness/cross-category taxonomy, historical recurrence, reliability/classification work and resistance to one simple ontology.
- **Marcello Truzzi** — methodological rather than subject-matter boundary for anomalous claims; proportional burden of evidence.
- **Christopher French / anomalistic psychology** — ordinary cognitive/perceptual mechanisms remain first-class explanations for sincere experience.
- **Erling Strand / Hessdalen** — persistent instrumentation of a recurrent anomaly without first solving ontology.

### Recommendation
Do **not** add these as 'support for the Strangeness Problem being real.' Add a provenance note stating that the **methodological architecture** is descended from anomaly cataloguing/classification, scientific physical-evidence review, anomalistic psychology and instrument-first field work.

## 5. CANON I §6.13 TAAC: PHENOMENOLOGY NEEDS EXPLICIT ANCESTRY

### Current state
TAAC describes altered-state phenomenology—time distortion, ego dissolution, boundary loss, apparent agency, communication-like experience, overwhelming meaning—while explicitly denying that perceived agency establishes an external agent.

### Relevant ancestry
- **William James** — religious/mystical experience as a legitimate object of description without automatic metaphysical conclusion.
- **Charles Tart** — altered-state phenomenology and state-specific observation.
- **Etzel Cardeña** — modern anomalous-experience/dissociation/altered-state methodology.
- **Bruce Greyson / modern NDE measurement** — separate phenotype/instrument from ultimate ontology.
- **Christopher French** — cognitive/perceptual ordinary explanations and memory/sleep/expectation controls.

### Recommendation
Next design-provenance revision should cite these as **phenomenology/method ancestry only**. None licenses an external entity, survival claim or consciousness control of M2.

## 6. CANON I §6.14 TAAM: CASE-PROVENANCE METHODOLOGY NEEDS CREDIT

### Current state
TAAM is unusually disciplined already:
- reconstructive memory is leading explanation;
- prospective cryptographically timestamped records;
- Class I–IV evidence classes;
- tiny model-sensitive residual;
- non-addressability and approximately zero controllable channel capacity;
- 'write it down before you talk to anyone' contamination control.

### Relevant ancestry
- **F. W. H. Myers / early SPR** — anomalous-memory/apparitional case collection and witness investigation, historically important but methodologically mixed.
- **Ian Stevenson / UVA DOPS** — large structured case corpora, pre-verification statement recording where possible, coded variables, explicit alternative hypotheses and avoidance of hypnotic regression.
- **Christopher French** — false memory, suggestion and anomalistic-psychology controls.
- **Cardeña** — dissociation/anomalous experience.
- **Pauli/Jung → Atmanspacher** — synchronicity/correlation language as philosophical/design ancestry only.

### Recommendation
Explicitly credit the **case-method** lineage while preserving the current stronger standards. The modern LOOM apparatus—cryptographic timestamping, preregistration, channel-capacity tests—is a LOOM extension, not something to back-project onto historical researchers.

**Action:** feed S3B questions, never chronology evidence.

- Red Pens: Lloyd/Bell/information theory can impose channel-capacity/no-signalling tests.

### C4 — persistent physical anomaly with no agreed category
- Freaks: Hessdalen/UAP sensor-first cases as methodological analogues;
- Unsupervised: alternative ontology/model proposals;
- Red Pens: instrumentation, null models, statistical controls and physical discriminators.

**Action:** useful template for future LOOM in-world science and actual research workflow.
