# LOOM 2226 — Foundations & Consequences References v0.1

**Status:** REFERENCE REGISTER — NON-CANON  
**Purpose:** trace external intellectual ancestry used by the program and state exactly why each source is relevant.

Inclusion is not endorsement. Sources are cited for one or more declared roles: `FOUNDATIONAL_COMPARISON`, `METHOD`, `QUESTION_GENERATOR`, `NEGATIVE_CONTROL`, `FICTIONAL_ANCESTRY`, `HISTORICAL_CASE`.

## Scientific / foundational corpus

### Daniele Oriti
1. Oriti, D. (2018), **Levels of spacetime emergence in quantum gravity**. arXiv:1807.04875. https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.04875  
   **Role:** FOUNDATIONAL_COMPARISON / METHOD.  
   **Why LOOM cares:** explicitly separates multiple reconstruction levels required to recover classical continuum spacetime from deeper non-spatiotemporal structure. This supports LOOM's staged evidence ladder and argues against treating one attractive toy result as 'spacetime emergence'.
2. Margoni, E. & Oriti, D. (2024), **The emergence of spacetime: what role for functionalism?** arXiv:2404.11386. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11386  
   **Role:** QUESTION_GENERATOR.  
   **Why:** useful to S4/X1 because assigning a functional role to a structure is not automatically identical to reconstructing the physical feature in question.
3. Lam, V. & Oriti, D. (2024), **The quantum gravity seeds for laws of nature**, European Journal for Philosophy of Science 14, 63. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13194-024-00626-0 ; preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12248  
   **Role:** FOUNDATIONAL_COMPARISON / QUESTION_GENERATOR.  
   **Why:** discusses non-spatiotemporal lawhood using entanglement, transition amplitudes and quantum causal histories; relevant to X1/X2/S3 without implying LOOM transport.

### Sumati Surya
1. Surya, S. (2019), **The causal set approach to quantum gravity**, Living Reviews in Relativity 22, 5. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41114-019-0023-1 ; preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11544  
   **Role:** FOUNDATIONAL_COMPARISON / METHOD.  
   **Why:** causal sets make causal partial order and local finiteness fundamental, while manifold geometry is reconstructed. This is a direct comparator for S3's primitive-vs-emergent causal-order question and for geometry reconstruction diagnostics.
2. Glaser, L. & Surya, S. (2013), **Towards a definition of locality in a manifoldlike causal set**.  
   **Role:** METHOD.  
   **Why:** relevant to locality and dimensional reconstruction without supplied coordinates.

### Fotini Markopoulou / Quantum Graphity
1. Konopka, T., Markopoulou, F. & Smolin, L. (2006), **Quantum Graphity**. https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0611197  
   **Role:** FOUNDATIONAL_COMPARISON / HISTORICAL_CASE.  
   **Why:** a close conceptual ancestor to LOOM Stage-A work: dynamical graph degrees of freedom, high-connectivity/disordered behavior and attempted emergence of low-dimensional locality. Particularly useful as a warning about geometrogenesis being encoded rather than earned.
2. Markopoulou, F. (2009), **Space does not exist, so time can**. https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.1861  
   **Role:** QUESTION_GENERATOR.  
   **Why:** supports S3B's serious exploration of architectures in which space emerges while causal succession/time remains primitive.

### Julian Barbour
1. Barbour, J. (2011), **Shape Dynamics. An Introduction**. https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.0183  
   **Role:** ADVERSARIAL_COMPARISON / QUESTION_GENERATOR.  
   **Why:** forces S3B to test whether time/scale/background assumptions are more contingent than the current LOOM framing presumes.

### Sara Imari Walker / Paul Davies
1. Walker, S. I. & Davies, P. C. W. (2012/2013), **The Algorithmic Origins of Life**. https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4803  
   **Role:** QUESTION_GENERATOR.  
   **Why:** proposes that life may involve a transition in causal architecture where information becomes context-dependent and causally efficacious. LOOM uses this only to sharpen X2/S5 questions about control and information; it is not evidence for M2.

### Garrett Lisi and technical criticism
1. Lisi, A. G. (2007), **An Exceptionally Simple Theory of Everything**. https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.0770  
   **Role:** HISTORICAL_CASE / FICTIONAL_ANCESTRY.  
   **Why:** useful as a case of a bold outsider mathematical proposal made concrete enough for specialists to attack.
2. Distler, J. & Garibaldi, S. (2009), **There is no 'Theory of Everything' inside E8**. https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.2658  
   **Role:** METHOD / NEGATIVE_CONTROL.  
   **Why:** demonstrates the kind of precise technical falsification LOOM should invite rather than seeking broad approval.

## Additional serious comparison frameworks

### Renate Loll / Causal Dynamical Triangulations
**Representative corpus:** CDT reviews and modern work by Loll and collaborators on Lorentzian path integrals and causal microstructure.  
**Role:** FOUNDATIONAL_COMPARISON.  
**Why:** CDT is a key contrast in which causal restrictions are built into microscopic admissibility. It helps test whether LOOM's graph pathologies arise because causal/geometric structure is absent from the state space, rather than because a coefficient was tuned badly.

### Bianca Dittrich
**Representative corpus:** work on coarse-graining, renormalization, discrete quantum geometry and continuum limits in background-independent quantum gravity.  
**Role:** METHOD / FOUNDATIONAL_COMPARISON.  
**Why:** motivates S2 as a first-class branch: finite-size robustness and universality must be earned before toy geometry is treated as physics.

### Emily Adlam
**Representative corpus:** recent work on relational observables, structural realism, laws/constraints and arrows of time.  
**Role:** QUESTION_GENERATOR / CONCEPTUAL_AUDIT.  
**Why:** sharpens S4/X1: what physical facts make two externally different realizations the same subsystem rather than merely descriptively equivalent?

### Seth Lloyd
**Representative corpus:** quantum information, computation, resource accounting and physical limits of information processing.  
**Role:** QUESTION_GENERATOR / CONSTRAINT_ANCESTRY.  
**Why:** useful for no-copy/no-free-information and resource-accounting questions around S4/S5/X2.

## Maverick / heterodox / speculative corpus

The following sources are intentionally admitted as question generators or fictional ancestry. They are not promoted to established physics merely because LOOM finds them fertile.

### Stephen Wolfram — Wolfram Physics Project
**Reference:** https://www.wolframphysics.org/  
**Role:** QUESTION_GENERATOR / ADVERSARIAL_COMPARISON / FICTIONAL_ANCESTRY.  
**Why:** evolving hypergraphs, causal graphs, multiway systems and causal invariance offer a computational vocabulary for separating spatial structure from causal/event structure and for observer-dependent reconstruction. Highly relevant to X1/S3/X4; non-consensus as physics.

### Donald Hoffman
**Representative corpus:** Interface Theory of Perception / conscious realism and later spacetime-from-agent proposals.  
**Role:** QUESTION_GENERATOR / FICTIONAL_ANCESTRY.  
**Why:** a maximalist substrate-versus-interface thesis useful for testing hidden assumptions about objecthood and observer-accessible reality. LOOM does not import consciousness as substrate.

### Bernardo Kastrup
**Representative corpus:** analytic idealism and non-spatial metaphysical interpretations.  
**Role:** FICTIONAL_ANCESTRY / QUESTION_GENERATOR.  
**Why:** useful for F1/F4 and X1 as a philosophical stress test of what 'location' and 'object' mean if spacetime is not fundamental; not physics evidence.

### Federico Faggin
**Representative corpus:** speculative quantum-information panpsychism and private/intrinsic information claims.  
**Role:** QUESTION_GENERATOR / FICTIONAL_ANCESTRY.  
**Why:** prompts distinctions among copied information, private state and constitutive identity for S4/F2. Consciousness claims are not adopted as scientific support.

### Eric Weinstein
**Reference:** Geometric Unity material at https://geometricunity.org/  
**Role:** ADVERSARIAL_COMPARISON / QUESTION_GENERATOR.  
**Why:** useful as a radical reconstruction/unification program and as an example of asking whether familiar structures are projections of a larger geometric object. No evidentiary role for M1/M2.

### Nassim Haramein
**Representative corpus:** *The Schwarzschild Proton* and later unification/gravity claims.  
**Role:** NEGATIVE_CONTROL / FICTIONAL_ANCESTRY.  
**Why:** retained to study the failure mode of moving from numerical/structural analogy to sweeping mechanism and technology claims without adequate evidentiary bridges.

## Program citation rule

A branch brief that invokes an external thinker or framework SHALL record:

1. source or representative corpus;
2. declared role;
3. exact question/constraint imported;
4. epistemic class of the imported claim;
5. explicit statement when the source is analogy, heterodox, metaphysical or negative-control material.

No citation can itself promote a claim.
