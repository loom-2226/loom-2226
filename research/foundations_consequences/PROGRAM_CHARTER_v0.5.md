## 8. Three-squad convergence gate

A topic earns hotspot status when:
1. Freaks identify a recurrent/well-provenanced problem class **after source/media/reflexivity controls**;
2. Unsupervised independently supply a structural reframing with discriminating alternatives;
3. Red Pens define observables, nulls, replication or a hard kill;
4. historical lineages show the question is not merely current fashion;
5. weighted relevance is high enough.

**Media repetition does not count as recurrence. Documentary reuse does not count as replication. Audience-generated follow-up reports are not independent by default.**
