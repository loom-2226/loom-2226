## 1. Role model

LOOM distinguishes:

1. **MAIN SQUAD ROSTER** — preferably living, active and in principle contactable/currently reviewable. Membership reflects the job we want a person's published/public work to do, not blanket endorsement.
2. **ANCESTRAL LINEAGE** — unrestricted by era or living status; historical thinkers, dead researchers, prior schools, failed programs and intellectual ancestors.
3. **BRIDGE MEMBER** — a living/current thinker whose work genuinely crosses two squad missions.
4. **CASE / WITNESS NODE** — a person whose claims, records or cultural impact materially belong in the evidence/provenance problem but who is not treated as a methodological authority merely by inclusion.

> **Ideas may move freely; evidentiary status may not.**

## 2. Non-endorsement

No roster member, bridge, ancestor or case/witness node is represented as endorsing LOOM, knowing about LOOM, agreeing with LOOM, or approving our interpretation of their work.

## 8. Ancestral lineage rule

Every squad owns an unlimited, versioned ancestry corpus. It may include deceased thinkers, historical schools, living figures better treated as ancestry, failed theories, skeptics who killed weak versions, critics, and cultural/phenomenological sources when clearly labelled.

Ancestry may overlap. William James belongs naturally to B and C ancestry; Wheeler belongs to A and B ancestry for different reasons.

## 9. Convergence rule

Three-squad convergence is a **priority signal, not an evidence multiplier**.

A topic earns higher research priority when:

1. Freaks identify a recurrent/well-provenanced observational or phenomenological structure;
2. Unsupervised independently formulate a structural hypothesis/reframing;
3. Red Pens turn that proposal into observables, nulls, discriminators, replication or a hard kill.

Media repetition, documentary recycling, audience feedback and inherited intellectual lineages must be clustered before convergence is scored.

## 10. Required corpus objects

Each squad SHALL maintain:
- current roster and role tags;
- ancestral-lineage dossier;
- work-level bibliography;
- LOOM branch/canon cross-reference;
- key criticisms/counterweights;
- `does_not_establish` notes;
- current status: active source, historical ancestry, bridge, case/witness, negative control or GHOST.

For anomaly/media work also preserve:
- raw-source provenance;
- intermediary chain;
- publication/edit history;
- public contamination timeline;
- investigator participation;
- custody/handler history for physical artifacts.

## 11. Canon relationship

Squads are research/design infrastructure. They do not automatically appear as in-universe authorities and do not amend canon by citation.

Where current canon incorporates an intellectual lineage, document that ancestry precisely and queue source-level credit for normal revision rather than rewriting locked canon ad hoc.
