**Status:** GOVERNING RESEARCH-PROGRAM CONVENTION — NON-CANON — NON-RUNTIME  
**Date:** 7 September 2026

## 1. Correction to v1.0

The previous squad model mixed current reviewers, historical thinkers and evidentiary case figures too freely. LOOM now distinguishes four roles:

1. **MAIN SQUAD ROSTER** — preferably living, preferably still active, and in principle contactable/currently reviewable. Membership reflects the job we want their work to do, not blanket endorsement.
2. **ANCESTRAL LINEAGE** — unrestricted by era or living status. Historical thinkers, dead researchers, prior schools, failed programs and intellectual ancestors belong here. Ancestry is persistent and may overlap across squads.
3. **BRIDGE MEMBER** — a living/current thinker whose work genuinely crosses two squad missions.
4. **CASE / WITNESS NODE** — a person whose claims, records or cultural impact materially belong in the evidence/provenance problem but who is not treated as a methodological authority merely by inclusion.

The governing rule remains:

> **Ideas may move freely; evidentiary status may not.**

## 6. Ancestral lineage rule

Every squad owns an unlimited, versioned ancestry corpus. The ancestry can include:

- deceased thinkers;
- historical schools;
- living thinkers who are better treated as lineage than reviewers;
- failed theories;
- skeptics who killed weak versions;
- critics whose objections materially shaped later work;
- cultural/phenomenological sources when clearly labelled.

Ancestry may overlap. Example: William James belongs naturally to both the Respectably Unsupervised and Freaks With Clipboards lineages; John Wheeler can sit in both Red-Pen and Unsupervised ancestry for different reasons.

## 7. Convergence rule

Three-squad convergence is a **priority signal, not an evidence multiplier**.

If:

1. Freaks With Clipboards identify a recurring, well-provenanced phenomenological pattern;
2. Respectably Unsupervised independently generate a structural hypothesis that could explain or reframe it; and
3. Adults With Red Pens can turn that proposal into measurable discriminators or a kill test;

then LOOM should promote the topic in the research backlog.

The convergence does **not** mean the extraordinary interpretation is true. It means the question has earned disciplined attention.

## 8. Required corpus objects

Each squad SHALL maintain:

- current roster and role tags;
- ancestral-lineage dossier;
- work-level bibliography;
- LOOM branch/canon cross-reference;
- key criticisms/counterweights;
- `does_not_establish` notes;
- current status: active source, historical ancestry, bridge, case/witness, negative control or GHOST.

## 9. Canon relationship

The squads are research/design infrastructure. They do not appear automatically as in-universe authorities and do not amend canon by citation.

Where current canon already incorporates an intellectual lineage, the research corpus should document that ancestry precisely and recommend source-level credit in the next normal canon revision rather than editing locked canon ad hoc.
