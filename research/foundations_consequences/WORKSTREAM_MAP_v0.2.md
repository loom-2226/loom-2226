# A. Empirical / Mathematical branches

## S1 — Geometrogenesis
**Question:** Can locality, effective dimension and reconstructible geometry emerge without target geometry being supplied by construction?

**Current state:** ACTIVE / QUALIFICATION; frozen comparator in PR #19 remains isolated.

**Adults With Red Pens:**
- Markopoulou — Quantum Graphity / geometrogenesis comparison.
- Surya — locality/manifoldlikeness and dimension diagnostics.
- Oriti — levels of emergence / collective regime discipline.
- Loll — spectral dimension and reconstruction benchmark.
- Dittrich — phase/coarse-graining perspective.

**Respectably Unsupervised:**
- Wolfram — spatial graph versus causal graph distinction; rewriting as alternative architecture.
- Barbour — relational/global-vs-local observables.

**Consequence:** positive -> S2; negative -> ablation/alternative substrate; persistent negative -> retire fixed-degree graph locality line rather than tuning forever.

## S2 — Continuum & Universality
**Question:** Do results survive scale, refinement, microscopic changes and coarse-graining?

**Primary Red Pens:** Dittrich, Oriti, Loll.

**Required outputs:** finite-size scaling; order parameters; phase structure; correlation scales; refinement/renormalization logic; multiple dimension diagnostics; perturbation robustness.

**Consequence:** prevents finite toy geometry from being promoted as continuum physics.

## S3A — Causal Consistency
**Question:** What structure guarantees globally admissible causal order across ordinary and relational processes?

**Primary Red Pens:** Surya, Loll, Markopoulou, Smolin, Adlam.
**Unsupervised stimulus:** Wolfram causal/multiway separation.

**Competing architectures:**
- causal order emergent;
- causal order primitive/superselected;
- spatial and causal structure reconstructed by different mechanisms;
- global constraint/admissibility rather than simple time-step dynamics.

**Consequence:** may turn chronology from a narrative rule into a mathematically explicit admissibility condition—or prove that chronology remains a separate fictional postulate.

## S3B — Fundamental vs Emergent Time
**Question:** What is time in the deepest viable LOOM ancestry?

**Primary Red Pens:** Markopoulou, Smolin, Surya, Adlam, Loll.
**Comparator:** Barbour.
**Wild stimulus:** Wolfram.

**Consequence:** high scientific uncertainty but enormous F1/F4/F6 yield. No branch wins because it makes Loom easier.

## S4 — Identity & Protected Observables
**Question:** What makes a bounded subsystem the same physical subsystem across changed external relational description/embedding?

**Primary Red Pens:** Adlam, Dittrich observables ancestry, Lloyd information constraints.
**Unsupervised stimuli:** Faggin private-state distinction; Hoffman interface/reconstruction; Kastrup only as metaphysical contrast.

**Research ladder:** subsystem -> observables/algebra -> protected invariant -> external embedding -> preservation vs copy/branch/reconstruction -> M1-adjacent question.

**Consequence:** strengthens `A_I` and non-duplication even if M1 remains fictional.

## S5 — Matter, Information & Relational Control
**Question:** Can matter/information states alter relational structure without bolting a steering coupling onto the model after the fact?

**Primary Red Pens:** Lloyd for physical information/resource accounting; Oriti/Lam for non-spatiotemporal quantum structures.
**Border-Crosser:** Walker for causal efficacy and measurable information-flow architecture.

**Hard rule:** Walker-style causal efficacy is question ancestry, not M2 evidence.

**Consequence:** highest possible miracle-budget reduction; highest reverse-engineering risk.
