## P1 additions from the new roster

P1 must now explicitly distinguish:
- source/intermediary chain;
- raw versus edited/released artifact;
- artifact authenticity versus interpretation confidence;
- publication/release events;
- pre/post-publication contamination;
- investigator participation/reflexivity;
- object custody/handler history;
- intervention/ritual events as recorded actions without presumed efficacy;
- audience-feedback/new-lead lineage.

## Weighted convergence

Current and historical contributors are weighted by utility to LOOM and clustered to prevent lineage/headcount inflation.

Media repetition is not recurrence. Documentary reuse is not replication. Shared source ancestry must be detected before convergence is credited.

High convergence can raise priority and source depth. It cannot change scientific status, tune a frozen experiment, bypass dependencies or create extra WIP.

## Research-corpus convention

Preserve for every material influence:
1. source claim;
2. LOOM interpretation;
3. LOOM extension;
4. LOOM result;
5. `does_not_establish` boundary;
6. criticism/counterweight.

For media/anomaly material also preserve raw-source provenance, intermediary chain, edit/release history, public contamination timeline and investigator participation.

## Isolation contract

This workstream SHALL NOT:
- import production Navigator/GIS/runtime modules;
- modify production runtime/data/geometry to satisfy a research hypothesis;
- alter propulsion constants, hidden topology, certification, Mc-299m/NRE calibration or locked canon without separate promotion;
- make production CI/release qualification depend on exploratory research;
- fit scientific models to desired fiction/canon outputs;
- use UAP/psi/paranormal/xeno material to calibrate M1/M2/M3 or relational response parameters.
