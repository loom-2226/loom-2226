## Attribution rule

Inclusion never means endorsement.

A source can enter LOOM as:
- `method`
- `ancestry`
- `comparison`
- `question`
- `negative_control`
- `fiction_ancestry`

Any scientific promotion must independently satisfy LOOM qualification gates.

## Specific safeguards

1. Oriti/Markopoulou/Surya/Loll/Smolin do not make M1/M2 less fictional merely by supporting pregeometry or emergent-spacetime research.
2. Walker does not establish M2; she provides a disciplined vocabulary for causal efficacy and metrology.
3. Wolfram does not establish LOOM topology or chronology; his graph distinctions are research stimuli.
4. Hoffman/Kastrup/Faggin do not establish consciousness-first physics or synthetic personhood.
5. Weinstein/Lisi do not establish hidden larger geometry for LOOM; they are reconstruction/unification comparators.
6. Haramein is retained as a deliberate negative control against analogy inflation.
7. Failed or criticized sources keep their citations when preserved as `GHOST`s.
