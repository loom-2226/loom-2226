# LOOM 2226 — Foundations & Consequences Workstream Map v0.1

## Parent program

**LOOM Foundations & Consequences** sits under a future **LOOM Research & Speculative Development Portfolio** and runs in parallel to Navigator, Solar GIS, runtime engineering, canon maintenance and world simulation.

## A. Empirical / Mathematical branches

### S1 — Geometrogenesis
**Question:** Can locality, effective dimension and reconstructible geometry emerge from relational dynamics without target geometry being supplied by construction?

**Current consequence:** This remains the active Relational Foundations qualification program. A positive result opens scaling/universality work; a negative result identifies missing structure or kills the fixed-degree graph line.

**Potential:** scientific 5/5; speculative 4/5; world 2/5; play 1/5.  
**Hazard:** MEDIUM.

### S2 — Continuum & Universality
**Question:** Do geometry-like results survive increasing size, microscopic changes, ensemble changes and coarse-graining?

**Why:** Dittrich, Oriti, CDT and GFT/tensor work make clear that a small toy model is not a continuum theory. Finite-size scaling, universality, phase structure and reconstruction stability are mandatory.

**Consequence:** Prevents a visually appealing finite graph from being mistaken for emergent spacetime.

**Potential:** scientific 5/5; speculative 3/5; world 1/5; play 1/5.  
**Hazard:** LOW.

### S3A — Causal Consistency
**Question:** What mathematical structure guarantees globally acyclic physical order when ordinary and relational causal relations are composed?

**Why:** Causal-set, CDT, temporal-relational and computational-causal approaches demonstrate that causality may need independent structural treatment.

**Consequence:** Could constrain chronology protection without pretending current Stage-A spatial graphs solve it.

**Potential:** scientific 5/5; speculative 5/5; world 4/5; play 4/5.  
**Hazard:** MEDIUM.

### S3B — Fundamental vs Emergent Time
**Question:** Is time primitive, emergent, relational, thermodynamic, computational, or reconstructed from causal order?

**Why:** Surya, Loll, Smolin, Markopoulou, Barbour, Adlam and Wolfram point toward genuinely different architectures.

**Consequence:** May never alter a drive equation, but can transform LOOM chronology, philosophy, institutions and crew interpretation.

**Potential:** scientific 4/5; speculative 5/5; world 5/5; play 4/5; joy 5/5.  
**Hazard:** HIGH.

### S4 — Identity & Protected Observables
**Question:** What does it mean for a bounded subsystem to remain the same subsystem while its external relational embedding changes?

**Research ladder:** subsystem identity -> protected observable/algebra -> external embedding -> preservation vs copying/branching/reconstruction -> only then M1-adjacent questions.

**Consequence:** Could strengthen the mathematical meaning of `A_I`, non-duplication and continuity even if no transport mechanism is derived.

**Potential:** scientific 4/5; speculative 5/5; canon 5/5; world 5/5; play 5/5.  
**Hazard:** HIGH.

### S5 — Matter, Information & Relational Control
**Question:** Under what conditions can matter or information-bearing states influence relational structure without the required control coupling being inserted after the fact?

**Why:** The existing M2 one-theory test remains binding. Walker-style causal-information questions are useful ancestry, not evidence.

**Consequence:** Highest upside for reducing the M1/M2 miracle budget; also highest risk of post-hoc mechanism fitting.

**Potential:** scientific 5/5 upside; speculative 5/5; canon 5/5; world 4/5.  
**Hazard:** EXTREME.

## B. Speculative / Synthetic branches

### X1 — Reconstruction, Observers & Effective Reality
**Question:** Which aspects of spacetime, location, objecthood and causal history are fundamental, and which are reconstructions available to bounded observers?

**Relevant ancestry:** Oriti, Adlam, Wolfram, Hoffman, Kastrup.

**LOOM consequence:** Provides a rigorous place to investigate the analogy between objective GM relational truth and actor-specific reconstructed reality without treating software architecture as physics evidence.

**Potential:** scientific 3/5; speculative 5/5; canon 4/5; world 5/5; play 5/5.  
**Hazard:** EXTREME.

### X2 — Information as Causal Structure
**Question:** When does information become causally efficacious rather than merely descriptive?

**Relevant ancestry:** Walker, Lloyd, quantum-information and complex-systems work.

**LOOM consequence:** Connects life, synthetics, control, M2-adjacent questions and xeno systems while remaining open research.

**Potential:** scientific 4/5; speculative 5/5; world 5/5; play 4/5.  
**Hazard:** HIGH.

### X3 — Xeno Ontology
**Question:** Could an older civilization engineer the same physics using a different decomposition of matter, information, geometry, observer and control?

**Consequence:** Makes alien technology conceptually strange without requiring arbitrary new laws. Reef/TMP artifacts can be difficult because human categories are wrong, incomplete or computationally inadequate.

**Potential:** speculative 5/5; canon 5/5; world 5/5; play 5/5; joy 5/5.  
**Hazard:** MEDIUM if kept fictional; EXTREME if back-projected as evidence.

### X4 — Wild Foundations / Negative Controls
**Question:** What useful question appears when we temporarily take a heterodox or failed framework seriously enough to stress-test LOOM?

**Relevant ancestry:** Wolfram, Barbour, Hoffman, Kastrup, Faggin, Weinstein, Lisi, Haramein; also historical ghosts.

**Consequence:** Institutionalizes intellectual risk-taking while preserving evidence discipline.

**Potential:** science 3/5; speculative 5/5; joy 5/5.  
**Hazard:** EXTREME.

## C. Civilizational / Fictional branches

### F1 — Schools of Physics & Metaphysics
Build competing 2226 interpretations of relational physics, time, objecthood and causality. Engineering agreement need not imply ontological agreement.

**Consequence:** produces credible academic, cultural and political disagreement instead of a single authorial cosmology.

**Potential:** world 5/5; play 4/5; joy 5/5.

### F2 — Synthetic Identity & Personhood
Develop structural, embodied, lineage, private-state, legal and memory-continuity schools and their consequences for law, inheritance, contracts, marriage, criminal responsibility, citizenship, testimony and reconciliation.

**Consequence:** turns existing synthetic continuity mechanics into society-level conflict and gameplay.

**Potential:** world 5/5; play 5/5; canon 5/5.

### F3 — Xeno Epistemology & Ontology
Maintain explicit distinctions among missing evidence, unknowability, computational inaccessibility, observer limitations, deliberate concealment and category error.

**Consequence:** TMP/TID/Reef/Kite become intelligence and interpretation problems rather than lore dumps.

**Potential:** world 5/5; play 5/5; canon 5/5.

### F4 — Religion, Philosophy & Meaning
Explore how discontinuous re-embedding, synthetic continuity, causal-order disputes and alien artifacts alter belief, ritual, metaphysics and personal meaning without declaring any school correct.

**Potential:** world 5/5; play 3/5; joy 5/5.

### F5 — Institutions, Regulation & Radical Science
Build foundations institutes, military programs, certification bodies, corporate research, synthetic collectives, fringe conferences, scientific scandals, failed theories, classified programs and crackpot ecosystems.

**Consequence:** creates two centuries of intellectual and institutional history behind 2226 technology.

**Potential:** world 5/5; play 4/5; joy 5/5.

### F6 — Operational Weirdness
Investigate what crews actually observe when systems operate correctly but human intuition about location, continuity, causality or reconstruction fails.

**Constraint:** must respect existing Formation / Commit / Transition / Relaxation / Emergence / Reacquisition distinctions and may not add arbitrary magic effects.

**Potential:** play 5/5; atmosphere 5/5; canon 4/5.  
**Hazard:** HIGH for technobabble.

### F7 — Failed Futures / Intellectual Ghosts
Preserve disproved, superseded and half-right theories as historical causes, institutional lineages, cult beliefs, commercial hype, design ancestry or xeno-interpretation tools.

**Consequence:** prevents LOOM history from reading like a clean march toward truth.

**Potential:** world 5/5; play 4/5; joy 5/5.

## D. Adversarial branch

### D1 — Alternative Foundations / Red Team
Every significant positive result must face a structured question: what alternative framework explains the same observation with fewer assumptions or exposes answer-smuggling?

Candidate comparators include causal sets, CDT, GFT/tensor models, tensor networks, gauge/moduli models, computational rewriting, timeless/shape approaches and information-first frameworks.

**Consequence:** prevents Relational Topography from becoming a protected pet theory.
