## Mission

LOOM Foundations & Consequences exists to pursue the deepest scientific, speculative, phenomenological and fictional questions underneath the setting **without allowing the number of interesting questions to become the plan**.

The program runs five porous but epistemically distinct lanes:

- **A — Empirical / Mathematical**
- **B — Speculative / Synthetic**
- **C — Civilizational / Fictional**
- **D — Adversarial / Wild**
- **P — Phenomenological / Anomaly**

The three permanent current-review squads are:

- **The Adults With Red Pens** — scientific murder board.
- **The Respectably Unsupervised** — radical-question / alternative-framing bench.
- **The Freaks With Clipboards** — anomalous phenomenology, evidence and provenance bench.

Each squad has an unrestricted ancestral lineage. Bridges and case/witness nodes remain explicitly typed under `SQUAD_ARCHITECTURE_v1.1.md`.

## Governing epistemic rule

> **Ideas may move freely; evidentiary status may not.**

The program may explore any useful question. No citation, philosophical fit, anomalous report, canon compatibility or three-squad convergence changes a claim's scientific status without independent qualification.

## Governing focus rule

> **Follow the strongest stream, not every stream.**

The active portfolio is limited to:

1. one scientific execution/qualification stream;
2. one additional science/discovery stream;
3. one fiction/world stream;
4. one bounded enabling-method item.

No fifth substantive active stream exists.

## Weighted convergence

Convergence is now a first-class portfolio variable under `CONVERGENCE_PRIORITY_MODEL_v1.0.md`.

The program gives unusually high priority to questions that independently recur across:

- high-value current squad members;
- high-value historical ancestors;
- more than one squad;
- more than one methodological mode;
- more than one LOOM domain.

Contributor relevance is maintained in `research_corpus/CONTRIBUTOR_WEIGHT_REGISTER_v1.0.md`.

### What convergence means
Convergence means **the question deserves more attention**.

### What convergence does not mean
Convergence is not an evidence multiplier, vote, consensus measure or proof.

## Three-squad convergence gate

A topic earns **CONVERGENCE HOTSPOT** status when:

1. the Freaks identify a recurrent, well-provenanced pattern/problem class;
2. the Unsupervised independently supply a structural reframing that creates discriminating alternatives;
3. the Red Pens can define observables, nulls, replication or a kill test;
4. historical lineages show the question is not merely a current-fashion artifact;
5. the combined contributor relevance is high enough under the weighted model.

Hotspot status raises portfolio priority and source depth. It does not bypass WIP or dependency gates.

## Scientific outcomes

A science branch may legitimately produce:

- DERIVATION;
- CONSTRAINT;
- FALSIFICATION;
- INSTRUMENTATION;
- INCONCLUSIVE;
- GHOST.

A negative result is not a failed PI if it kills a tempting path cleanly.

## Fiction outcomes

A fiction/world branch may legitimately produce:

- new institutions;
- competing schools;
- laws/customs;
- gameplay consequences;
- cultural history;
- xeno interpretation frameworks;
- unresolved but structured disputes.

Fiction may generate scientific questions. Fiction does not supply scientific evidence.

## Phenomenology outcomes

The P lane may produce:

- better evidence schemas;
- ordinary explanations;
- improved instrumentation;
- cleaner taxonomies;
- residual classes;
- failed-case lessons;
- new formal questions.

`UNEXPLAINED` never means `EXOTIC` by default.

3. LOOM extension;
4. LOOM result;
