## 3. Claim discipline

Inherited epistemic classes:
- **E** — established physics or mathematics;
- **A** — analogy / mathematical ancestry;
- **P** — fictional postulate;
- **C** — constitutive closure / canon calibration;
- **D** — derived result;
- **O** — open problem.

Additional research-use tags:
- **H** — heterodox hypothesis;
- **M** — metaphysical stimulus;
- **R** — red-team / negative control;
- **F** — fiction / cultural ancestry.

A work item may move between lanes. A claim may not silently change epistemic class.

## 4. Mandatory intellectual ancestry

Any work item materially derived from an external thinker SHALL record:
1. person/collaboration;
2. exact source work;
3. year and DOI/arXiv/stable URL;
4. imported idea;
5. LOOM extension;
6. epistemic tag;
7. what the source does not establish for LOOM if confusion is plausible.

Attribution survives `RETIRED` and `GHOST` status.

## 5. Program outcomes

A branch is valuable if it produces:
- **DERIVATION** — reduces a fictional permission by deeper structure;
- **CONSTRAINT** — sharpens required physics/engineering/world conditions;
- **FALSIFICATION** — kills an attractive mechanism;
- **GENERATIVITY** — creates scientifically honest unresolved structure with fiction value;
- **INSTRUMENTATION** — improves measurement or discrimination;
- **HISTORICAL VALUE** — preserves failed ideas as useful `GHOST`s.

## 6. Scoring

Score 0–5 on:
- Scientific leverage
- Speculative reach
- Canon leverage
- World yield
- Play yield
- Rabbit-hole joy

Separate Epistemic Hazard: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / EXTREME.

High hazard is a labeling/control requirement, not a creativity veto.

## 7. Portfolio states

SEED -> PROBE -> RABBIT_HOLE -> PROGRAM_CANDIDATE -> QUALIFICATION -> PROMOTION_CANDIDATE

Terminal/non-terminal preservation states:
- RETIRED
- GHOST

## 8. WIP rule

Default PI allocation:
- ~70% disciplined qualification/validation
- ~20% adjacent exploration/comparison
- ~10% wild-card work

Default science WIP: one execution branch plus at most two discovery branches.
Default fiction/speculation WIP: no more than three active design branches unless explicitly time-boxed.

## 9. Traffic rules

Allowed:
- fiction -> scientific question;
- speculation -> mathematical candidate;
- science -> fiction constraint;
- failed science -> history/worldbuilding;
- maverick idea -> hypothesis or negative control;
- squad source -> branch ancestry or adversarial comparator.

Forbidden:
- fictional desirability -> scientific evidence;
- canon fit -> preselection of foundations mathematics;
- analogy -> calibration;
- source prestige or outsider status -> evidentiary promotion;
- toy success -> proof of M1/M2.

## 12. Cultural rule for LOOM

LOOM should credit radical ideas honestly even when they fail. It should also credit technical criticisms that kill attractive ideas.

The intellectual history we want is not "geniuses who were secretly right." It is a two-century ecology of good questions, bad theories, brilliant constraints, failed models, rediscoveries, institutional fashions and engineering consequences.

The Adults With Red Pens and the Respectably Unsupervised are permanent tools for building that ecology.
