# LOOM 2226 — Foundations & Consequences Scaled-Agile Operating Model v1.0

**Status:** ACTIVE PROGRAM GOVERNANCE — NON-CANON — NON-RUNTIME  
**Date:** 7 September 2026

## 1. Why this exists

LOOM has outgrown ad-hoc rabbit-hole management but does not need heavyweight enterprise ceremony.

This operating model uses the parts of scaled agile that solve our actual problem:

- portfolio hierarchy;
- explicit strategic value;
- weighted convergence;
- WIP limits;
- dependency gates;
- enablers;
- program increments;
- system demos / evidence reviews;
- inspect-and-adapt;
- architectural runway and institutional memory.

It explicitly rejects ceremony that does not improve focus, evidence or delivery.

## 3. Portfolio object hierarchy

### Portfolio Epic
A major LOOM question with multi-domain consequences, e.g. `Identity & Protected Observables`.

### Capability
A coherent outcome required to advance the epic, e.g. define identity invariants; distinguish copying from continuation; create qualification criteria.

### Enabler
Method/infrastructure needed to make later work defensible, e.g. P1 evidence schema, attribution registry, null-model harness.

### Experiment / Story
A bounded research test, literature synthesis, formal definition, fiction design artifact or gameplay prototype.

### GHOST
A retired or falsified idea preserved because its intellectual/history/worldbuilding value remains useful.

## 5. Planning cadence

LOOM uses **milestone-driven Program Increments**, not arbitrary calendar bureaucracy.

A PI begins when the previous anchor reaches a verdict/milestone and ends when the new anchor does the same.

A PI SHOULD normally remain small enough to contain:

- 1 T0 anchor;
- 1 T1 science/discovery objective;
- 1 T1 fiction/world objective;
- 1 enabling-method objective.

## 6. PI planning inputs

At PI start inspect:

1. current execution verdicts;
2. `SVS` and `LOOM-WSJF` scores;
3. convergence-hotspot changes;
4. dependency graph;
5. canon/runtime constraints;
6. available research/test artifacts;
7. reviewer/ancestry additions;
8. user appetite and current device/work context.

## 7. PI objectives require exit criteria

No objective may enter active WIP with only “research this more.”

Examples:

### S4 discovery exit criterion
Produce a formal identity taxonomy and qualification criteria distinguishing:
- gauge-equivalent representation;
- physical transformation;
- copying;
- branching;
- destruction/reconstruction;
- protected continuation.

### F2 design exit criterion
Produce internally coherent synthetic-personhood schools, legal consequences and gameplay-facing distinctions with explicit ancestry and no hidden GM resolution.

### P1 enabler exit criterion
Define a machine-readable anomaly evidence object with independence, provenance, contamination, selection and residual fields before any deep anomaly case ingestion.

## 8. WIP policy

### Hard limits
- 1 scientific execution/qualification stream.
- 1 additional scientific discovery stream.
- 1 fiction/world design stream.
- 1 small enabler.

### No silent WIP expansion
A new stream must replace, complete or formally pre-empt an existing active item.

### Corpus maintenance exception
Citation updates, source preservation, criticisms and lineage maintenance do not consume an execution slot unless they become a substantive new synthesis.

## 9. Convergence governance

Use `CONVERGENCE_PRIORITY_MODEL_v1.0.md`.

A convergence hotspot can:
- raise backlog priority;
- trigger a short memo;
- justify deeper bibliography;
- justify asking a new formal question.

It cannot:
- promote a claim's epistemic status;
- tune a frozen experiment;
- bypass dependencies;
- create a second science execution slot.

## 10. System demo

Every active objective closes with something inspectable:

- experiment verdict / JSON output;
- formal definition/criteria document;
- tested schema/tool;
- canon-design packet;
- fiction/world artifact;
- red-team memo;
- explicit `INCONCLUSIVE` outcome.

“Interesting discussion” is not a completed capability.

## 11. Inspect & Adapt

At PI close:

1. state what changed;
2. state what died;
3. state what remained unresolved;
4. update contributor/lineage links;
5. rescore active and READY streams;
6. identify convergence changes;
7. verify no epistemic/canon leakage;
8. promote no more than the WIP limit permits.

## 12. Architecture runway

The following are treated as architectural runway for future research:

- research corpus and ancestry dossiers;
- contributor weights;
- work-item schema;
- P1 anomaly evidence schema;
- identity taxonomy/criteria;
- chronology branch survey;
- convergence ledger;
- test harnesses and reproducible experiment runners;
- attribution policy.

Runway is built only when it unlocks a scored stream.

## 16. Pre-emption rule

An active stream may be pre-empted only when:

- a hard dependency changes;
- a formal verdict kills its premise;
- a new stream exceeds it materially in SVS and is execution-ready;
- user intent explicitly changes.

A fun new idea is not a pre-emption event.

## 17. Anti-bureaucracy rule

If a process artifact takes longer to maintain than the research decision it improves, simplify it.

The purpose of this framework is to protect attention, not manufacture administration.
