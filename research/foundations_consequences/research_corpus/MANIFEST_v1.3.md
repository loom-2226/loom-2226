## Convergence handling

The contributor register assigns 1–5 **LOOM Utility Weights**. The convergence model then clusters related intellectual lineages and applies diminishing returns so headcount cannot masquerade as independent convergence.

Historical convergence may be weighted as strongly as current-roster convergence when it is genuinely independent and materially relevant to LOOM.

Convergence changes **portfolio priority**, never scientific truth status.
