## Intended use

Before beginning a new Foundations & Consequences research item:
1. check the bibliography for primary sources;
2. read the relevant dossier sections;
3. follow primary links for the full source when deeper interpretation is required;
4. record ancestry in the new work item;
5. cite competing criticism where material;
6. distinguish source claim from LOOM extension.

## Persistence

This corpus SHALL be retained through branch restructuring. Future versions add or supersede dossiers but do not delete older cited records unless they contain a factual citation error; corrections should be auditable.

## Full-text policy

The manifest intentionally does not claim the repository is a mirror of the papers themselves. Detailed LOOM-authored research notes and source metadata are versioned here; full texts remain at lawful primary repositories unless redistribution is clearly licensed and vendoring is necessary for reproducibility.
