## Jeremy Corbell — documentary / release-ecology seat

### Relevant work
- documentary filmmaking focused on UAP, advanced-technology and anomalous claims, including **Bob Lazar: Area 51 & Flying Saucers**.
- co-host, **Weaponized**, with George Knapp.
- public release of UAP-related imagery/video and source claims.
- 2021 USS Omaha imagery/video release; Pentagon spokesperson Susan Gough confirmed the referenced footage was taken by Navy personnel and included in UAPTF examination.

### Why LOOM cares
Corbell supplies a clean lesson that **provenance authenticity and explanatory interpretation are different variables**. A media artifact can be genuinely military-origin footage while the depicted object's nature remains unresolved.

He also exposes the public transformation layer: raw artifact, selected excerpt, caption/context, documentary/podcast framing, audience interpretation and derivative retellings.

### LOOM use
P1 media-artifact lifecycle; release chronology; public contamination; player-facing evidence packages; F5 disclosure-media ecology.

### Does not establish
Authenticity of origin does not validate the strongest claims attached to the artifact. Editing and framing are epistemic transformations and must be recorded.
