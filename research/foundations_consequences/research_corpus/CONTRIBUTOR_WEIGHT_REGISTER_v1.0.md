## Purpose

This register supports `CONVERGENCE_PRIORITY_MODEL_v1.0.md` by assigning a **LOOM Utility Weight (LUW)** from 1.0–5.0 to current and ancestral contributors.

The weight answers only:

> **How much can this contributor's LOOM-relevant body of work help the overall project?**

It is not a scientific-credibility score, intelligence ranking, popularity score or truth vote.

A high-weight critic/negative control can be as valuable as a high-weight proponent.

Weights are portfolio estimates and may be revised as branches mature.

# Weighting rules

## 1. No double counting

A person appearing as both current and ancestry (for example Vallée or Strand) may contribute to both **current-state relevance** and **historical depth**, but the convergence model must cluster the shared intellectual lineage so the same body of work is not counted twice as independent evidence.

## 2. Shared ancestry clusters

Examples of clusters that should receive diminishing returns:

- Pauli → Jung → Primas → Atmanspacher;
- Fort → Hynek → Vallée;
- Rhine → PEAR → Radin;
- Bekenstein/Hawking → Jacobson → holographic emergence lines;
- Wheeler/DeWitt → modern quantum-gravity time programs.

## 3. Cross-mode independence matters more than celebrity count

A branch becomes particularly interesting when high-value contributors approach it through different modes, e.g.:

- Surya formal causal order;
- Barbour conceptual time reconstruction;
- Cardeña/French subjective-time phenomenology and controls;
- Hawking chronology constraints.

That is more valuable than four adjacent papers from one program.

## 4. Recalibration

Weights SHOULD be reviewed when:

- a branch enters formal qualification;
- a current contributor publishes materially relevant new work;
- a major criticism/replication changes usefulness;
- a new lineage is added;
- the LOOM canon architecture changes enough that a formerly peripheral contribution becomes central.

## 5. Governing caution

> **LUW measures usefulness to LOOM's questions. It is not permission to believe the contributor's answers.**
