## Daniele Oriti

### Core works
1. **Oriti, D. (2017), _The universe as a quantum gravity condensate_.** arXiv:1612.09521. https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.09521
   - Treats cosmology as a hydrodynamic/collective regime of group-field-theory quantum-gravity degrees of freedom.
   - LOOM use: S1/S2. Important less for any particular cosmological prediction than for the methodological idea that continuum dynamics should arise as a collective phase of microscopic pregeometric degrees of freedom.
   - LOOM consequence: if Stage-A graph structure ever looks geometric, the next question is not "does it look like space?" but "what collective variables, phase structure and effective dynamics survive scale change?"

2. **Oriti, D. (2018), _Levels of spacetime emergence in quantum gravity_.** arXiv:1807.04875. https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.04875
   - Separates spacetime emergence into multiple reconstruction moves as familiar spacetime structure is progressively removed at deeper levels.
   - LOOM use: governing ancestry for the RF graduation ladder: locality -> dimension -> continuum/universality -> causal/Lorentzian structure -> IR physics -> autonomous domains -> M1/M2 questions.
   - Why it matters: it directly warns against treating "emergent spacetime" as one binary achievement.

3. **Margoni, E. & Oriti, D. (2024), _The emergence of spacetime: what role for functionalism?_** arXiv:2404.11386. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11386
   - Examines whether functionalism can explain recovery of spatiotemporal structure from a non-spatiotemporal substrate; finds the strategy viable but its explanatory import unsettled.
   - LOOM use: X1 and S4. Helps distinguish successful operational reconstruction from a stronger ontological identity claim.

4. **Lam, V. & Oriti, D. (2024), _The quantum gravity seeds for laws of nature_.** Eur. J. Phil. Sci. 14, 63. DOI:10.1007/s13194-024-00626-0. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13194-024-00626-0
   - Highlights entanglement, transition amplitudes and quantum causal histories as candidate non-spatiotemporal structures from which laws and eventually spacetime may be reconstructed.
   - LOOM use: X1, S3 and X2; useful for talking about physical law when ordinary spacetime is not fundamental.

### Red-Pen question for LOOM
"Which level of spacetime reconstruction has actually been demonstrated, and which levels are being rhetorically bundled together?"

### Not evidence for
M1, M2, re-embedding, Mc-299m, controllable macroscopic geometry, or any LOOM transport mechanism.

## Bianca Dittrich

### Core works
1. **Dittrich, B., Eckert, F.C. & Martin-Benito, M. (2012), _Coarse graining methods for spin net and spin foam models_.** New J. Phys. 14, 035008; arXiv:1109.4927. https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.4927
   - Applies Migdal-Kadanoff and tensor-network-renormalization techniques to discrete quantum-gravity models, with attention to gauge-symmetry-preserving coarse graining and fixed-point structure.
   - LOOM use: S2. Direct methodological ancestry for asking whether a microscopic discrete model possesses robust large-scale phases.

2. **Dittrich, B., Martín-Benito, M. & Schnetter, E. (2013), _Coarse graining of spin net models: dynamics of intertwiners_.** New J. Phys. 15, 103004; arXiv:1306.2987. https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.2987
   - Uses tensor-network renormalization to determine phase structure and effective continuum behavior in simplified spin-net models.
   - LOOM use: S2; reinforces that phase diagrams and fixed points matter more than attractive single configurations.

3. **Dittrich, B., Martin-Benito, M. & Steinhaus, S. (2014), _Quantum group spin nets: refinement limit and relation to spin foams_.** Phys. Rev. D 90, 024058; arXiv:1312.0905. https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.0905
   - Studies refinement limits, nontrivial fixed points and extended phases.
   - LOOM use: S2 graduation criteria: size scaling, refinement stability, universality/phase structure.

4. **Borissova, J.N. & Dittrich, B. (2023), _Towards effective actions for the continuum limit of spin foams_.** Class. Quantum Grav. 40, 105006. DOI:10.1088/1361-6382/accbfb.
   - LOOM use: what an actual continuum-program step looks like once a discrete model has microscopic dynamics.

5. **Asante, S.K., Dittrich, B. & Steinhaus, S. (2024), _Spin Foams, Refinement Limit, and Renormalization_.** Handbook of Quantum Gravity. DOI:10.1007/978-981-99-7681-2_106.
   - LOOM use: current review-level map for refinement/renormalization questions.

### Red-Pen question for LOOM
"What is the renormalization/refinement story, and what observable remains stable when the microscopic description changes?"

### Not evidence for
That LOOM's current graph ensemble is a spin foam, or that tensor-network tools automatically rescue an expander-dominated model.

## Sumati Surya

### Core works
1. **Surya, S. (2019), _The causal set approach to quantum gravity_.** Living Rev. Relativity 22; arXiv:1903.11544. https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11544
   - Reviews causal-set theory: locally finite partial orders as fundamental structure, causal order as proto-causality, continuum approximation, Lorentz-invariant discreteness and characteristic nonlocality.
   - LOOM use: S3A/S3B and D1. Provides a serious architecture where causal order is fundamental instead of emerging after spatial locality.

2. **Glaser, L. & Surya, S. (2013), _Towards a Definition of Locality in a Manifoldlike Causal Set_.** arXiv:1309.3403. https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.3403
   - Proposes locality/manifoldlikeness diagnostics using order-interval abundance and obtains a continuum dimension estimator.
   - LOOM use: S1/S3 diagnostics. Especially important because it separates manifoldlikeness from generic discreteness and shows locality can be nontrivial even when Lorentz invariance is preserved.

### Red-Pen question for LOOM
"Why are you trying to make causality emerge from a structure that does not contain causal order, and what observable would distinguish manifoldlike locality from graph-theoretic prettiness?"

### Not evidence for
That causal sets are LOOM's hidden substrate. They are a comparator architecture and diagnostic ancestry.

## Fotini Markopoulou

### Core works
1. **Markopoulou, F. (1999/2000), _Quantum causal histories_.** arXiv:hep-th/9904009. https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9904009
   - Defines causal sets with Hilbert spaces at events and local evolution maps while preserving causal-order properties.
   - LOOM use: S3/X1; demonstrates a concrete information/quantum formulation in which causal pre-structure is primary.

2. **Hawkins, E., Markopoulou, F. & Sahlmann, H. (2003), _Evolution in Quantum Causal Histories_.** arXiv:hep-th/0302111. https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0302111
   - Uses locally finite causal pre-spacetime with matrix algebras and completely positive maps; connects quantum causal histories to quantum information and algebraic QFT.
   - LOOM use: S3, S4 and X1.

3. **Konopka, T., Markopoulou, F. & Severini, S. (2008), _Quantum Graphity: a model of emergent locality_.** arXiv:0801.0861. https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0861
   - Dynamical graph model with a highly connected high-energy phase and proposed low-energy ordered/local/low-dimensional phase.
   - LOOM use: S1's closest historical cousin and caution. It makes geometrogenesis an explicit dynamical phase problem.
   - Critical relevance: LOOM's expander-soup result is exactly the kind of failure mode that should be compared against graphity's mechanism rather than hidden by action tuning.

4. **Markopoulou, F. (2009), _Space does not exist, so time can_.** arXiv:0909.1861. https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.1861
   - Argues for a spaceless rather than timeless quantum-gravity ontology: time fundamental, space/geometry/gravity emergent.
   - LOOM use: S3B and F1. Serious alternative to "everything including time must emerge."

### Red-Pen question for LOOM
"What today would convince us that geometrogenesis occurred rather than that the desired geometry was encoded in the Hamiltonian/action?"

### Not evidence for
LOOM's specific graph action or M1/M2.

## Lee Smolin

### Core works
1. **Cortês, M. & Smolin, L. (2013), _Energetic Causal Sets_.** arXiv:1308.2206. https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.2206
   - Events form causal processes carrying conserved energy-momentum; spacetime is not fundamental and can emerge semiclassically.
   - LOOM use: S3A/S3B, and a sharp comparator where causality is primary while locality/spacetime are not.

2. **Smolin, L. (2018), _Temporal relationalism_.** arXiv:1805.12468. https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.12468
   - Reviews a program assuming relational physics plus fundamental time and causality, including energetic causal sets and relational approaches to quantum foundations.
   - LOOM use: S3B. Prevents "emergent time" from becoming an unexamined default.

3. **Smolin, L. (2023), _The path integral formulation of energetic causal set models of the universe_.** arXiv:2303.15546. https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15546
   - Extends energetic causal-set path integrals toward QFT and discusses conditions under which spacetime can emerge semiclassically.
   - LOOM use: comparator for how an explicitly causal pregeometry tries to recover familiar field physics.

### Red-Pen question for LOOM
"What if time/causal succession is the thing we are not allowed to derive away?"

### Not evidence for
A particular chronology-protection rule or superluminal transport.

## Emily Adlam

### Core works
1. **Adlam, E. (2021/2022), _Laws of Nature as Constraints_.** arXiv:2109.13836; Foundations of Physics 52. https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.13836
   - Develops a modal/constraint-based approach to laws suitable for nonlocal, atemporal, all-at-once or retrocausal possibilities that do not fit a simple time-evolution paradigm.
   - LOOM use: S3B and chronology architecture; useful for distinguishing "law as evolution rule" from "law as global admissibility constraint."

2. **Adlam, E. (2024), _How do we Observe Relational Observables?_** arXiv:2410.05508. https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05508
   - Asks how relational observables connect to embodied observation in diffeomorphism-invariant settings; emphasizes localization and internal reference frames.
   - LOOM use: X1, S4 and GM/player epistemology. Directly useful for distinguishing objective relational structure from what a bounded agent can operationally reconstruct.

3. **Adlam, E. (2025), _Relational Observables, Quiddities, and Structural Realism_.** arXiv:2507.20313. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20313
   - Uses relational observables and "internal views" to explore modal ontic structural realism.
   - LOOM use: S4 identity/protected observables and X1 reconstruction.

### Red-Pen question for LOOM
"When you say the same domain occupies a different embedding, what observable fact makes it the same physical system rather than merely a functionally similar realization?"

### Not evidence for
Personal identity persistence through Loom transition. It sharpens the conceptual criterion we still have to earn.

## Renate Loll

### Core works
1. **Ambjørn, J., Jurkiewicz, J. & Loll, R. (2005), _Reconstructing the Universe_.** arXiv:hep-th/0505154. https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0505154
   - Reports a large-scale four-dimensional semiclassical regime emerging from causal dynamical triangulations and examines nonclassical short-scale structure.
   - LOOM use: S2. A benchmark for what "reconstruction" should mean: large-scale observables, dimensions and dynamics, not visual resemblance.

2. **Ambjørn, J., Jurkiewicz, J. & Loll, R. (2005), _Spectral Dimension of the Universe_.** arXiv:hep-th/0505113. https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0505113
   - Measures scale-dependent spectral dimension in CDT, with approximately four dimensions at large scale and lower effective dimension at short scale.
   - LOOM use: S1/S2 dimension diagnostics and reminder that effective dimension can run with scale.

3. **Loll, R. (2019), _Quantum Gravity from Causal Dynamical Triangulations: A Review_.** arXiv:1905.08669. https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08669
   - Reviews CDT's phase structure, scaling limit, time/causality, topology, renormalization and invariant observables.
   - LOOM use: S2/S3A/D1. Especially valuable as a hostile comparator because causality is built into the microscopic ensemble rather than expected to appear later.

### Red-Pen question for LOOM
"If causal structure matters macroscopically, why is it absent from the microscopic admissibility rules?"

### Not evidence for
That CDT's microscopic choices are uniquely correct or should be copied into LOOM's model.

## Seth Lloyd

### Core works
1. **Lloyd, S. (2005), _A theory of quantum gravity based on quantum computation_.** arXiv:quant-ph/0501135. https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0501135
   - Proposes pairwise quantum interactions as fundamental processes with spacetime geometry constructed from quantum-information processing; derives superpositions of four-dimensional Regge-like spacetimes in the model.
   - LOOM use: X2, S4/S5 and D1. Strong ancestry for asking whether information-processing relations can reconstruct geometry without treating "information" as a free physical substance.

2. **Lloyd, S. (2013), _The universe as quantum computer_.** arXiv:1312.4455. https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.4455
   - Reviews the idea that physical evolution can be regarded computationally and explores order/complexity in a quantum-computational universe.
   - LOOM use: X2/F1 and resource-accounting language.

### Red-Pen question for LOOM
"What physical resources carry the information, what is conserved, and where exactly would copying, control or hidden free computation enter the story?"

### Not evidence for
M1/M2 or a literal computational substrate underlying LOOM canon.

# Squad A synthesis for LOOM

Across the Red-Pen corpus, the recurring demands are remarkably consistent:

1. pregeometry is legitimate, but **reconstruction must be staged**;
2. locality is not automatic;
3. dimension must be measured in more than one way and across scales;
4. continuum behavior needs refinement/renormalization/universality evidence;
5. causal order may be fundamental rather than emergent;
6. relational observables require an account of actual observers/internal frames;
7. identity needs an operational/mathematical invariant, not a slogan;
8. information cannot substitute for physical resource accounting;
9. no serious quantum-gravity program currently derives LOOM's M1 or M2.

That is why this squad exists: not to bless LOOM, but to give us increasingly precise ways to fail.
