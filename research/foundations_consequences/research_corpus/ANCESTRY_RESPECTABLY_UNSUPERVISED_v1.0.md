# LOOM 2226 — Respectably Unsupervised Ancestral Lineage v1.0

**Scope:** historical and intellectual ancestry for Squad B. Inclusion means conceptual ancestry, question-generation, methodology, or cautionary value. It does not mean scientific endorsement.

## 1. Wolfgang Pauli + C. G. Jung — core lineage

### Relevant works
- C. G. Jung, **Synchronicity: An Acausal Connecting Principle** (1952; later Collected Works vol. 8).
- C. G. Jung and Wolfgang Pauli, **The Interpretation of Nature and the Psyche** (1952), pairing Jung's synchronicity essay with Pauli's study of archetypal ideas in Kepler.
- C. A. Meier (ed.), **Atom and Archetype: The Pauli/Jung Letters, 1932–1958** (Princeton University Press, English ed. 2001).
- Wolfgang Pauli, **The Influence of Archetypal Ideas on the Scientific Theories of Kepler** (published with Jung in 1952).
- Pauli, **Modern Examples of Background Physics** (`Hintergrundsphysik`), preserved with the correspondence and relevant to his attempt to think about an integrated description of nature involving physics and psyche.

### Why LOOM cares
Current CANON I Appendix A.6 already names Pauli/Jung and the *unus mundus* as explicit design provenance. The useful inheritance is not paranormal validation. It is the possibility that the mental/physical distinction is not necessarily an ontological split at the deepest level, while ordinary local physical closure remains intact.

### LOOM branches
X1 Reconstruction/Observers/Effective Reality; X2 Information as Causal Structure; S4 identity questions; F1/F2/F4 Patternist and personhood traditions; Freaks cross-domain phenomenology.

### Hard boundary
Pauli/Jung did not derive FTL, M1, M2, reliable telepathy or a controllable physical synchronicity mechanism.

## 2. Baruch Spinoza — neutral/dual-aspect precursor

### Relevant work
- **Ethics** (1677), especially the single-substance framework and mental/physical attributes.

### Why LOOM cares
Provides deep ancestry for later dual-aspect approaches in which mental and physical descriptions may be different aspects rather than independent substances.

### Boundary
Metaphysical ancestry only.

---

## 3. Friedrich Wilhelm Joseph Schelling — identity/Naturphilosophie precursor

### Relevant work
- **Ideas for a Philosophy of Nature** (1797).
- **System of Transcendental Idealism** (1800).
- later identity-philosophy writings.

### Why LOOM cares
Historical precursor to attempts to treat mind and nature within one underlying order rather than as unrelated domains; explicitly identified in modern dual-aspect-monism literature as part of the lineage preceding Pauli/Jung.

## 4. William James — radical empiricism, pluralism, psychical research

### Relevant works
- **The Principles of Psychology** (1890).
- **The Varieties of Religious Experience** (1902).
- **A Pluralistic Universe** (1909).
- **Essays in Radical Empiricism** (1912).
- extensive Society for Psychical Research / American SPR involvement.

### Why LOOM cares
James is important twice: he loosens the subject/object split through radical empiricism, and he demonstrates that anomalous experience can be studied without making ontology the first question.

### Branches
X1, F1/F4, Freaks ancestry, anomalous-experience methodology.

## 5. Charles Sanders Peirce — continuity, chance, abduction, disciplined weirdness

### Relevant bodies of work
- pragmatism/pragmaticism;
- **synechism** (continuity as a fundamental philosophical principle);
- **tychism** (objective chance/indeterminacy);
- abductive inference;
- skeptical but non-dismissive engagement with psychical-research claims.

### Why LOOM cares
Peirce gives LOOM a stronger philosophical ancestry for inference under incomplete evidence than generic 'open-mindedness'. His abduction framework is especially useful for X1, D1 and Freaks-to-Red-Pens handoff.

## 6. Henri Bergson — duration and time

### Relevant works
- **Time and Free Will** (1889).
- **Matter and Memory** (1896).
- **Creative Evolution** (1907).

### Why LOOM cares
A major historical alternative to spatialized clock-time, relevant to S3B and to in-world philosophical schools that distinguish lived duration from operational causal ordering.

### Boundary
Does not supply physical chronology mechanics.

---

## 7. Alfred North Whitehead — process and relational ontology

### Relevant works
- **An Inquiry Concerning the Principles of Natural Knowledge** (1919).
- **The Concept of Nature** (1920).
- **Science and the Modern World** (1925/26).
- **Process and Reality** (1929).
- **Adventures of Ideas** (1933).

### Why LOOM cares
Whitehead replaces substance-first ontology with event/process/relationship language. His actual occasions and process-relational framework are useful ancestry for X1, S4 and F1 without being imported as physics.

Reference: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Whitehead and process philosophy entries: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/whitehead/ ; https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/process-philosophy/

## 8. Erwin Schrödinger — life, mind and unity

### Relevant works
- **What Is Life?** (1944).
- **Mind and Matter** (1958).
- **My View of the World** (essays collected in English editions).

### Why LOOM cares
Connects physics, information-like questions in biology, and philosophical reflection on subject/object unity. Useful X2/F2 ancestry, not a consciousness-physics result.

## 9. Eugene Wigner — measurement and mind-body speculation

### Relevant work
- **Remarks on the Mind–Body Question** (1961), in *The Scientist Speculates*.

### Why LOOM cares
Historical example of a first-rate physicist entertaining a consciousness-linked interpretation of measurement. Useful as intellectual history and as a caution that prestige does not settle ontology.

## 10. David Bohm — implicate order and wholeness

### Relevant works
- **Causality and Chance in Modern Physics** (1957).
- **Wholeness and the Implicate Order** (1980).
- Bohm & Hiley, **The Undivided Universe** (1993).

### Why LOOM cares
The implicate/explicate distinction is one of the clearest historical ancestors for the idea that familiar spatially separated objects may be effective unfoldings of a deeper order. It is highly generative for X1 and F1/F4.

### Boundary
Bohmian mechanics and implicate-order metaphysics do not predict Loom topology.

## 16. Harald Atmanspacher — living formal descendant / B↔C bridge

### Relevant works
- Atmanspacher, Römer & Walach, **Weak Quantum Theory: Complementarity and Entanglement in Physics and Beyond** (Foundations of Physics, 2002).
- Atmanspacher & Fach, **A structural-phenomenological typology of mind-matter correlations** (Journal of Analytical Psychology, 2013), DOI 10.1111/1468-5922.12005.
- Atmanspacher, **Psychophysical correlations, synchronicity and meaning** (2014), DOI 10.1111/1468-5922.12068.
- Atmanspacher, **The Pauli–Jung Conjecture and Its Relatives: A Formally Augmented Outline** (Open Philosophy, 2020), DOI 10.1515/opphil-2020-0138; open ETH record https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/entities/publication/3b2bb992-57f2-466b-afb8-0e4380911e24
- Atmanspacher, **Psychophysical neutrality and its descendants: a brief primer for dual-aspect monism** (Synthese, 2024), DOI 10.1007/s11229-023-04449-z.

### Why LOOM cares
This is the most direct modern intellectual descendant of the Pauli/Jung provenance already embedded in CANON I. It gives us a precise vocabulary for neutral background domains, decomposition into mental/physical aspects, observable partitions and psychophysical correlations.

### Boundary
Still philosophical/formal ancestry, not an empirically validated Loom mechanism. Modern formalization does not convert synchronicity into a controllable channel.

## 17. Lineage consequence for LOOM

The middle squad's ancestry is not 'people who believed weird things.' It is a long argument over whether:

- substance or relation/process is primary;
- time is fundamental or reconstructed;
- subject/object is ontic or epistemic;
- information/organization can become causally explanatory;
- mind and matter require two substances or can be aspects of one deeper order;
- anomalous correlations can be described without granting supernatural control.

That is exactly why Pauli and Jung belong here as **ancestry**, not as present-day squad members.
