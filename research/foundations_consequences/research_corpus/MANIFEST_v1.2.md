## Completeness convention

The corpus is **complete for the currently identified LOOM-relevant lineage**, not a claim to contain every work in every person's career.

When a branch enters formal qualification, serious design production, or reviewer outreach:
1. re-check source currency;
2. deepen that branch's bibliography;
3. add major critiques/replications/counterarguments;
4. preserve exact source identifiers and `does_not_establish` boundaries;
5. update this manifest or issue a successor.

## Storage rule

LOOM stores detailed original summaries, bibliographic metadata, stable primary/source links, methodological critiques, counterweights, and lawful excerpts. It does not wholesale mirror copyrighted publications for convenience.

Public-domain or explicitly redistributable material may be vendored when it improves reproducibility and its provenance/license is recorded.

## Core credit rule

For every material external influence preserve:
1. original source claim;
2. LOOM interpretation;
3. LOOM extension;
4. LOOM result;
5. what the source does not establish where over-reading is plausible.

Historical thinkers remain credited even after a branch is retired or becomes `GHOST`.
