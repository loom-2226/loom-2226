## Bibliography status

Existing `BIBLIOGRAPHY_v1.0.md` and `REFERENCES_v0.1.md` remain valid seed bibliographies but are no longer exhaustive after addition of ancestral lineages.

The ancestry dossiers themselves are therefore the **authoritative work-level source lists for v1.1** until a consolidated machine-readable bibliography is generated.

## Storage rule

LOOM vendors:
- detailed original summaries;
- bibliographic metadata;
- stable source/DOI/arXiv links;
- methodological critiques and counterweights;
- explicit `does_not_establish` boundaries.

LOOM does not bulk-mirror copyrighted works merely for convenience. Public-domain and clearly redistributable material may later be vendored when useful for reproducibility.

## Completeness convention

`v1.1` means **complete for the currently identified LOOM-relevant lineage**, not complete bibliographies of every person's career. When a branch activates, its bibliography should be deepened before formal qualification or outreach.

This convention prevents false completeness while ensuring the intellectual supply chain is reconstructible now.
