## Stephen Wolfram

### Core works / technical corpus
1. **Wolfram Physics Project — Technical Introduction: spatial, causal, multiway and branchial graphs.** https://www.wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/
   - Models fundamental evolution through local rewriting of hypergraphs and distinguishes several graph objects that are often conflated in casual discussion: spatial hypergraph, causal graph, multiway state graph, multiway causal graph and branchial graph.
   - LOOM use: S3/X1/X4. The separation between "what is connected spatially" and "what events causally depend on what" is directly useful to our chronology branch.

2. **Causal Invariance.** https://www.wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/the-updating-process-in-our-models/causal-invariance/
   - In the project's terminology, different allowed update sequences may branch and later reconverge while yielding the same ultimate causal graph for causally invariant rules.
   - LOOM use: S3A question generation: could chronology/observer consistency depend on a confluence-like property of underlying transitions rather than on ordinary spatial geometry?
   - Caveat: the project itself contains technical discussion and later internal critique of simple equivalences between confluence and causal invariance; LOOM must treat this as a research program, not settled physics.

3. **Graph Types appendix.** https://wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/additional-material/appendix-graph-types/
   - Explicitly distinguishes spatial and causal representations and describes foliations as reconstructions of histories.
   - LOOM use: X1 and GM/player epistemology: an observer's reconstruction can be different from the deeper relational object without implying that "anything goes."

4. **Multiway systems / rule-space material.** https://wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/the-updating-process-in-our-models/multiway-systems-for-our-models/
   - Explores branching update histories and structural equivalence of states independent of node labels.
   - LOOM use: S4 question generation around identity under representational relabeling versus genuine physical inequivalence.

### Unsupervised question for LOOM
"Are we forcing spatial graph, causal graph, state-space graph and observer reconstruction into one data structure when they should be distinct objects?"

### Epistemic status
Technically explicit, computationally rich, heterodox fundamental-physics program; not established as a physical theory of our universe.

### Not evidence for
LOOM hidden topology, M1/M2, causal invariance as chronology protection, or a literal ruliad ontology.

## Julian Barbour

### Core works
1. **Barbour, J. (2011), _Shape Dynamics. An Introduction_.** arXiv:1105.0183. https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.0183
   - Develops a background-independent framework eliminating absolute spatial structures in favor of relational shape variables; replaces four-dimensional diffeomorphism invariance with spatial diffeomorphism plus conformal invariance in the gravity formulation.
   - LOOM use: S3B/X4. Forces us to separate "relational" from "spacetime-emergent" and to ask which absolute structures we have quietly retained.

2. **Barbour, J., Koslowski, T. & Mercati, F. (2015), _Entropy and the Typicality of Universes_.** arXiv:1507.06498. https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.06498
   - Uses a relational N-body model and scale-invariant measures to argue for Janus-point structure and emergent arrows of time without a special low-entropy initial condition in that model.
   - LOOM use: S3B/F1/F4. Useful counterexample to the assumption that arrow-of-time explanations must be phrased only in conventional initial-condition terms.

3. **Barbour, J., Koslowski, T. & Mercati, F. (2016), _Janus Points and Arrows of Time_.** arXiv:1604.03956. https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.03956
   - Further develops the two-arrow Janus-point picture in unconfined systems.
   - LOOM use: chronology schools and in-world foundational debate.

4. **Barbour, J., Lobo, F.S.N. & Lourenço, M.I.R. (2026), _Structural morphology and the gravitational arrow of time_.** arXiv:2607.27526. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27526
   - Studies how global scale-invariant "variety" can coexist with sharply different local morphology, emphasizing complementary local relational observables.
   - LOOM use: S1/S3B methodology: global order parameters may not uniquely characterize local structure.

### Unsupervised question for LOOM
"Which structures we call time, scale, distance or history are actually gauge/relational conveniences rather than primitive furniture?"

### Epistemic status
Serious theoretical-physics work, but used in LOOM as an adversarial relational/timeless/fundamental-time comparator rather than as the preferred foundation.

### Not evidence for
LOOM chronology, re-embedding or a timeless substrate.

## Donald D. Hoffman

### Core works
1. **Hoffman, D.D., Singh, M. & Prakash, C. (2015), _The Interface Theory of Perception_.** Psychonomic Bulletin & Review 22, 1480–1506. DOI:10.3758/s13423-015-0890-8. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26384988/
   - Argues using evolutionary-game-theory models that fitness-tuned perceptual representations need not be veridical representations of objective structure.
   - LOOM use: X1. Useful question generator for the distinction between operationally adaptive reconstruction and ontological transparency.

2. **Hoffman, D.D. (2016), _The Interface Theory of Perception_.** Current Directions in Psychological Science 25(3). DOI:10.1177/0963721416639702.
   - Presents the "desktop/interface" analogy in which spacetime and objects may function as species-specific perceptual representations rather than literal depictions of deeper reality.
   - LOOM use: X1/F1/F4 and actor-specific reconstruction.

3. **Hoffman, D.D. (2019), _Did we evolve to see reality, or are spacetime and objects just our user interface?_** Annals NYAS. DOI:10.1111/nyas.14222.
   - Extends the interface framing specifically to spacetime/object perception.

4. **Prentner, R. & Hoffman, D.D. (2024), _Interfacing consciousness_.** Frontiers in Psychology 15:1429376. DOI:10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1429376.
   - Connects interface theory to a much stronger conscious-agent ontology outside spacetime.
   - LOOM use: X4/F4 only at the stronger ontological level.

### Unsupervised question for LOOM
"What if a correct operational map of reality can systematically fail to resemble the deeper structure it helps an observer navigate?"

### Epistemic status
Interface-theory modeling is a real research program in perception; Hoffman's stronger conscious-agent/conscious-realism ontology is highly speculative and not accepted fundamental physics.

### Not evidence for
Consciousness as substrate, mind-first physics, LOOM topology, or synthetic consciousness.

## Bernardo Kastrup

### Core works
1. **Kastrup, B. (2017), _An Ontological Solution to the Mind-Body Problem_.** Philosophies 2(2), 10. DOI:10.3390/philosophies2020010.
   - Develops analytic idealism: a consciousness-only ontology intended as an alternative to physicalism and constitutive panpsychism.
   - LOOM use: F1/F4 and X4 metaphysical red team.

2. **Kastrup, B. (2019), _Analytic Idealism: A consciousness-only ontology_.** Doctoral dissertation, Radboud University.
   - Systematic presentation of the idealist framework and its claimed explanatory advantages.
   - LOOM use: fictional/philosophical ancestry for Pattern Idealism and related schools; not science support.

3. **Kastrup, B. (2022), _What lurks behind spacetime?_** essay indexed at https://www.bernardokastrup.com/p/essays.html
   - Explores the idea that complexity/structure need not require literal spacetime extension.
   - LOOM use: X1/X4 question generation: distinguish mathematical/relational complexity from metric extension.

### Unsupervised question for LOOM
"Can structure and differentiation be meaningful without assuming that extension in ordinary spacetime is fundamental?"

### Epistemic status
Metaphysics/philosophy, not an empirical quantum-gravity theory.

### Not evidence for
Any physical mechanism, M1/M2, consciousness field, or xeno explanation.

## Federico Faggin

"Which parts of a system's constitutive state are in principle exportable descriptions, and which identity claims require more than duplicating externally available information?"

### Epistemic status
Explicitly speculative consciousness ontology built on quantum-information concepts.

### Not evidence for
Synthetic souls, non-clonable personhood, M1 continuity, or consciousness-based physics.

## Eric Weinstein

### Core work
1. **Weinstein, E.R. (2021), _Geometric Unity: Author's Working Draft v1.0_.** Official project page: https://geometricunity.org/
   - Attempts to recover seemingly incompatible geometries/field content from a more general geometric structure; introduces the "Observerse" and associated bundle/geometric machinery.
   - LOOM use: X4/D1 and F5/F7. The interesting LOOM lesson is not that GU is correct; it is the strategy of asking whether familiar field/geometric structures are projections/restrictions of a larger mathematical object.
   - Methodological relevance: a useful outsider-program case for distinguishing a compelling organizing picture from a mature theory with validated predictions.

2. **2013 Oxford lecture / later public release.** https://geometricunity.org/2013-oxford-lecture/
   - Useful historical record of the program's conceptual motivations and reconstruction strategy.

### Unsupervised question for LOOM
"Are we mistaking mutually incompatible effective descriptions for fundamental incompatibility because we have not found the larger representation that contains them?"

### Epistemic status
Self-published, controversial and not an accepted unification theory; use as conceptual/methodological comparator.

### Not evidence for
Metric engineering, M1/M2, Standard-Model completion in LOOM, or any claimed engineering extrapolation.

## A. Garrett Lisi

   - Proposes embedding Standard Model and gravitational structures into an E8-based connection framework.

   - LOOM use: gold-standard example of productive scientific failure: "beautiful" becomes useful when the proposal is precise enough to admit a kill argument.

### Unsupervised question for LOOM
"Have we made our ambitious proposal precise enough that someone can prove it cannot work?"

### Epistemic status
Historically serious but technically challenged unification proposal; the critique is part of the required reference set, not an optional footnote.

### Not evidence for
LOOM unification or E8 ancestry.

## Nassim Haramein

   - Models a proton using a Schwarzschild-condition analogy, relates vacuum-energy estimates, gravitational/strong-force scale comparisons, and semi-classical magnetic-moment numerics.
   - LOOM use: D1/F7 **negative control**. This is specifically useful for training ourselves to detect the move from striking numerical/structural analogy to sweeping causal or engineering claim without the intervening derivation, predictive discrimination and empirical validation.

"Are we treating a numerical coincidence, scale resemblance or dimensional analogy as though it were a derived mechanism?"

Highly nonstandard and not accepted as a replacement for established proton/QCD physics. Included deliberately as an epistemic stress test, not as hidden support.

### Not evidence for
Anything in LOOM's physics stack. Its value is methodological and fictional sociology.

# Squad B synthesis for LOOM

The Respectably Unsupervised are not a shadow peer-review panel. Their durable value is different:

1. **Wolfram** keeps spatial, causal, branching and observer-reconstruction structures separate.
2. **Barbour** attacks hidden assumptions about absolute time, scale and configuration.
3. **Hoffman** supplies a maximal test of operational representation versus ontological resemblance.
4. **Kastrup** asks whether structure requires spacetime extension at all, but from metaphysics rather than physics.
5. **Faggin** provokes the distinction between accessible information and constitutive identity.
6. **Weinstein** supplies a larger-representation/reconstruction strategy and outsider-theory sociology.
7. **Lisi + Distler/Garibaldi** demonstrate why radical ideas become scientifically useful when they can be technically killed.
8. **Haramein** is a standing negative control against analogy inflation and coincidence-driven mechanism claims.

Their job is to make sure LOOM does not become merely careful. The Red Pens keep the claims clean; the Unsupervised keep the questions dangerous.
