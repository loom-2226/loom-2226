**Branch:** `research/reviewer-constellation-cross-reference`  
**Purpose:** preserve two deliberately different reviewer/reference branches and identify the parts of each person's work that may be scientifically, philosophically, artistically, or adversarially useful to LOOM 2226.

## Epistemic rule

This document does **not** treat intellectual proximity as validation. A source may be useful as:

- **E** — established/accepted mathematics or science;
- **A** — mathematical/conceptual ancestry or analogy;
- **H** — heterodox but technically interesting hypothesis;
- **M** — metaphysical/philosophical stimulus;
- **R** — red-team / negative-control material;
- **F** — fiction/artfulness inspiration.

Only E material may be treated as ordinary scientific support. A/H/M/F material may sharpen questions, generate models, enrich fiction, or expose hidden assumptions, but may not be promoted into evidence for M1, M2, Metric, Loom translation, chronology control, Mc-299m, or any other canon technology.

# Research-use firewall

1. Branch A sources may strengthen method, define comparators, sharpen open questions and identify legitimate mathematical ancestry. They do not establish M1/M2.
2. Branch B sources may generate hypotheses, language, fictional schools of thought, character/institutional positions and adversarial questions. They do not receive evidentiary promotion merely because their vocabulary resembles LOOM.
3. No source is allowed to alter the frozen deterministic-curvature comparator or other preregistered experiments after results are known.
4. Canon remains downstream of research. Research literature is not selected because it conveniently reproduces canon.
5. Where a maverick claim and mainstream result conflict, record the conflict explicitly rather than averaging them together.
