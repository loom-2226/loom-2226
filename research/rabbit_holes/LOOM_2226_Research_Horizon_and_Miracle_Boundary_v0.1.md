# LOOM 2226 — Research Horizon and Miracle Boundary v0.1

**Classification:** `CLASS: RESEARCH` · `RESEARCH_METHODOLOGY` · `NON-CANON` · `NON-QUALIFICATION` · `NOT ACTIVE LOOM WIP` · `ARCHIVAL POLICY DRAFT`

**Status:** Defines how LOOM may later declare a rabbit hole sufficiently explored and place an explicit fictional postulate beyond the research horizon. It does **not** declare any current rabbit hole closed.

**Authority note:** GitHub remains authoritative for LOOM governance. This document is research-method doctrine only and does not alter canon, CCR state, WIP allocation, PR #19, PR #16, PR #20, P1, runtime, data, or engineering.

---

## 1. Purpose

LOOM does not need to prove every frontier hypothesis it explores.

The research objective is narrower:

> Push each rabbit hole through established knowledge, known prior art, reproducible tests, hostile controls, and the bounded experiments LOOM can credibly perform; then stop at the nearest genuinely unresolved frontier and place any required fictional bridge explicitly beyond that boundary.

The desired end state is not proof.

It is a **well-surveyed edge of ignorance**.

LOOM may place a miracle only at the narrowest unresolved gap immediately beyond the strongest defensible real-world result it can reach.

---

## 2. Research zones

### R0 — Established Ground

Theorems, accepted mathematics, established physics, well-supported empirical results, or reproducible methodology.

LOOM may rely on these directly, subject to ordinary provenance and scope discipline.

### R1 — Reproduced Neighborhood

LOOM independently reproduces, validates, or operationally confirms relevant known behavior closely enough to establish that its machinery is behaving as intended.

This is not novelty.

### R2 — Frontier Probe

LOOM performs one or more bounded discriminating tests that move beyond simple reproduction and attack the nearest unresolved question accessible to its methods and compute.

The purpose is not to solve the field. It is to determine whether the candidate seam survives cheap explanations.

### R3 — Research Horizon

The next meaningful question exceeds LOOM's credible bounded research capability, or the literature itself reaches a genuine unresolved frontier.

Examples include:
- a new theorem is required;
- substantially more advanced mathematics is required than LOOM can independently validate;
- HPC-scale computation exceeds the intended research budget;
- experimental apparatus or inaccessible data are required;
- the remaining question is itself an active professional research problem;
- further bounded work has sharply declining information value.

At R3, stopping is allowed.

### M — Miracle Stake

A deliberately fictional postulate placed immediately beyond R3.

It must be:
- explicit;
- narrow;
- non-evidentiary;
- non-retroactive;
- unable to upgrade anomaly reports, simulations, or speculation into evidence;
- stated so future science can revise or remove it cleanly.

---

## 3. Horizon closure criteria

A rabbit hole may be considered for `FRONTIER_REACHED` only when all of the following are satisfied.

### H1 — Serious literature mapped

LOOM has identified the nearest relevant theory, theorem, prior model, major contrary result, strongest known criticism, and closest published analogue.

This does not require exhaustive bibliography. It requires sufficient coverage that another broad landscape search is unlikely to materially change the decision boundary.

### H2 — Cheap explanations attacked

Known trivial, numerical, methodological, finite-size, provenance, measurement, model-selection, or implementation explanations have been tested or explicitly bounded.

A result does not qualify merely because a preferred observable moved in the expected direction.

### H3 — Bounded experiments completed

LOOM has performed the discriminating work realistically available to it without converting the project into an open-ended professional research programme.

### H4 — Remaining obstacle is genuinely frontier-level

The next unresolved step is not merely omitted work. It requires materially stronger theory, data, compute, apparatus, or professional specialization than LOOM intends to supply.

### H5 — Diminishing information value

Additional bounded work is unlikely to change the placement, width, or interpretation of the proposed miracle boundary enough to justify continued rabbit-hole consumption.

---

## 4. Required distinctions

LOOM must distinguish:

- **UNKNOWN** — genuinely unresolved after appropriate research;
- **UNTESTED** — LOOM has not yet done the relevant work;
- **INACCESSIBLE** — relevant evidence or capability exists but is unavailable;
- **OUT_OF_SCOPE** — work is possible but intentionally outside the bounded LOOM research programme;
- **KNOWN_CONFLICT** — established result materially contradicts the candidate seam;
- **FICTIONAL_POSTULATE** — deliberate LOOM miracle beyond the research horizon.

`UNKNOWN` must never be used as a synonym for `UNTESTED`.

## 5. Miracle Boundary Record

When a rabbit hole is eventually closed at R3, create a separate record rather than editing this policy.

Suggested identifier:

`MB-<DOMAIN>-NNN`

Each record should contain:

- question investigated;
- established foundation;
- nearest prior art;
- strongest negative results;
- LOOM reproductions;
- LOOM discriminating experiments;
- last defensible LOOM-supported result;
- unresolved frontier question;
- explicit reason research stops;
- capabilities required to continue;
- exact miracle stake;
- evidentiary classification of that miracle;
- forbidden inferences;
- revision / invalidation triggers;
- citations and provenance;
- relevant frozen experiment SHAs or qualification records.

The governing principle is:

> **We did not prove the miracle. We earned the right to place it here.**

## 6. WALTER research-horizon gate

WALTER may later operate a bounded `RESEARCH_HORIZON / MIRACLE_STAKE` assurance gate.

WALTER does **not** decide whether a miracle is true.

WALTER checks whether LOOM has earned the right to stop and whether the proposed fictional bridge is narrow enough.

Candidate outcomes:

- `CONTINUE_RESEARCH`
- `RUN_ONE_MORE_DISCRIMINATOR`
- `FRONTIER_REACHED`
- `MIRACLE_STAKE_ALLOWED`
- `MIRACLE_STAKE_TOO_BROAD`
- `KNOWN_SCIENCE_CONFLICT`
- `RESEARCH_INCOMPLETE`

Questions WALTER should ask:

1. Have the nearest serious explanations been identified?
2. Have contradictory and null results been preserved?
3. Have cheap explanations been attacked?
4. Is the remaining uncertainty genuinely unresolved rather than merely unresearched?
5. Would another bounded test materially change the boundary?
6. Does further progress require frontier-level capabilities?
7. Is the proposed miracle narrower than the unresolved gap?
8. Does the miracle avoid rewriting established science beneath the boundary?
9. Is evidentiary status preserved across the boundary?
10. Are future revision triggers explicit?

WALTER may recommend closure. He may not convert uncertainty into evidence.

## 7. Relational Topography — current provisional status

**Rabbit hole:** Relational Foundations / Relational Topography

**Current status:** `HORIZON_NOT_YET_REACHED`

Current work has materially raised the bar for what would count as interesting topographic emergence.

The research target is no longer merely movement in path length, spectral gap, curvature, or spanning-tree count.

The relevant frontier question is approximately:

> Can a minimally constrained, coordinate-free relational statistical system self-organize into robust, distributed, scalable topographic structure without explicit coordinates, target dimension, lattice structure, desired path lengths, desired spectral gaps, or geometry-coded objectives — and survive hostile explanations based on graph invariants, bottlenecks, finite-size effects, proposal bias, metastability, and diagnostic degeneracy?

Before any future `FRONTIER_REACHED` determination, current work requires at minimum:

- completion of the F3 proposal-kernel / weighted-MCMC audit;
- determination that intended equilibrium claims are sampler-qualified or explicitly bounded;
- adversarial graph controls capable of faking old locality signals;
- successor consideration of low-tree-entropy morphology and density of states;
- a final frontier synthesis establishing what remains unresolved after those controls.

This section is provisional research bookkeeping only. It does not amend PR #19 or PR #16.

## 8. Relationship to anomaly / phenomenology research

This policy applies equally to LOOM's anomaly and phenomenology rabbit holes.

The anomaly corpus may reach a research horizon without resolving exotic ontology. A properly placed miracle stake may begin only after provenance, source dependence, contamination, sensor effects, conventional mechanisms, and alternative hypotheses have been exhausted to the bounded standard appropriate to that domain.

Convergence across physics, anomaly, phenomenology, or other research lanes may raise research priority.

It does not raise truth by itself.

> **Convergence raises priority, not truth.**

---

## 9. Anti-drift rules

The research horizon must not move merely because:

- a new interesting paper exists;
- another possible experiment can be imagined;
- a rabbit hole is enjoyable;
- a favorable result increases emotional investment;
- an unfavorable result creates pressure to redefine success;
- LOOM can technically continue computing.

The boundary may move only when new evidence or a materially stronger discriminating method changes the frontier itself.

---

## 10. Core principle

The intended LOOM sequence is:

```text
established knowledge
        ↓
prior art / theorem / evidence
        ↓
LOOM reproduction
        ↓
hostile controls
        ↓
bounded frontier probe
        ↓
RESEARCH HORIZON
        ↓
explicit fictional miracle stake
        ↓
derived LOOM consequences
```

The miracle is not allowed to reach backward across the boundary and reinterpret the evidence that preceded it.

The purpose of this policy is to give LOOM a finish line without pretending that the finish line is truth.
