# Synthetic-person assumptions

Synthetic persons are recognized persons represented by a separate aggregate
stock. They have no biological age, fertility, mortality, or health cohorts.

The exploratory central path uses:

- 2100 initial stock equal to 0.01% of global biological population;
- annual gross instantiation equal to 0.002% of biological population, multiplied
  by legal access and a bounded substrate-support index;
- 1% annual retirement/loss;
- zero net global migration;
- legal access rising from 20% in 2100 to 70% in 2160 and 100% in 2226;
- labor participation rising from 60% to 75%;
- effective labor per participating person rising from 1.25 to 1.50 biological
  labor units.

The support index is inherited computer-hardware capital per biological worker,
relative to 2100, raised to 0.25 and bounded to 0.5–2.0. Country additions are
allocated across the qualified economic 80 using that support evidence. The
result is 9.792 million synthetic persons in 2226. No endpoint target or fitted
parameter is used.

Country allocation becomes concentrated, including a 23.6% synthetic labor share
in Ireland. This is disclosed as a sensitivity requirement and should not be
treated as a promoted geographic forecast.
