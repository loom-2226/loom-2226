# Lean biosynthetic successor v0.1

**Designation:** `EARTH_LEAN_BIOSYNTHETIC_COUPLED_SUCCESSOR_v0_1_CANDIDATE_2026_09_24`

**Status:** bounded research candidate; non-canon, non-production, not promoted.

This second pass preserves every v0 artifact and makes two bounded additions:

1. a three-case global medical/longevity sensitivity and three-case synthetic
   stock sensitivity, producing exactly nine coupled economic runs;
2. a generic geographic allocation repair blending absolute enabling capacity
   with capacity intensity.

All nine cases pass the biological, synthetic, labor, 2100 handoff, production,
capital, sector, and asset gates. No case uses an endpoint target.

The working central scenario is `MED_CENTRAL × SYNTH_CENTRAL` because all cases
qualify and the evidence does not establish another case as uniquely superior.
Its 2226 endpoint is:

- biological population: 7,163,281,708;
- synthetic persons: 9,672,506;
- recognized persons: 7,172,954,214;
- approximate median biological age: 48.43;
- biological labor: 1,455,527,767;
- synthetic labor: 10,881,569;
- machine task capacity: 52,627,339;
- total effective labor: 1,519,036,675;
- qualified 80-economy VA proxy: 241.454 trillion.

The medical sensitivity range is 6.991–7.465 billion biological people. The
synthetic sensitivity range is 3.901–24.940 million people. These ranges are
scenario results rather than probabilities or canon bounds.
