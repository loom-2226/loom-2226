# Medical sensitivity

The recovered lineage requires near-universal broad medicine by 2226, unequal
premium longevity, rare extreme rejuvenation, and mortality-based causal
effects. It supplies no qualified calendar adoption curve. The three cases use
small global changes only:

- `MED_CONSERVATIVE`: exact v0 path;
- `MED_CENTRAL`: broad access reaches 50% in 2135 and 85% in 2160, with modestly
  stronger mature-age mortality reductions;
- `MED_HIGH`: broad access reaches 50% in 2125 and 95% in 2160, with stronger but
  bounded mortality reductions. High-end access is still only 40% and extreme
  access 0.2% in 2226.

All cases retain positive old-age mortality and the same fertility path.

| Case | Biological population | Median age | 65+ | 80+ | 100+ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 6.991B | 47.42 | 30.62% | 15.79% | 3.04% |
| Central | 7.163B | 48.43 | 32.04% | 17.29% | 3.91% |
| High | 7.465B | 50.24 | 34.47% | 19.94% | 5.72% |

The full bounded change from conservative to high is +473.8M people, or 6.78%.
Population remains below the recovered v0.4 endpoint in every case; medical
timing alone therefore does not explain the full difference.
