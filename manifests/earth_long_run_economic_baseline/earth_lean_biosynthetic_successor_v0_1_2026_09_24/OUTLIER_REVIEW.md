# Outlier review

The candidate is numerically coherent, but several scenario-sensitive results
must remain visible.

## Economic level

Qualified 80-economy value added in 2226 is 236.154 trillion proxy units, 28.14%
below promoted v4. This is the causal result of the smaller biological population
and the cohort-based labor path; no endpoint rescaling was applied. ARE has the
largest relative country change at -53.26%, partly because the new interface
repairs v4 employment above its health-capable resident population at 2100.

India, China, and the United States are the top three qualified economies. Angola
is seventh. The detailed top 20 and rank changes are in `results/OUTLIER_REVIEW.json`.

## Person and automation geography

Synthetic labor is globally small but concentrated: Ireland reaches a 23.62%
synthetic share, followed by Malaysia at 4.88%. The machine-task share reaches
21.21% in Angola and 16.60% in Pakistan. These values follow the inherited
computer-hardware and automation-enabling capital signals. They satisfy the
configured bounds and accounting rules, but the geographic sensitivity is not
qualified strongly enough for promotion.

## Demography

Biological population falls from 10.187 billion in 2100 to 6.991 billion in
2226. The 2226 top five are India, Pakistan, DR Congo, Nigeria, and Ethiopia.
The model has 30.62% age 65+, 15.79% age 80+, 3.04% age 100+, and 0.004% age
150+. These emerge from the saved fertility path and explicit mortality schedule;
they are not canon targets.

## Second pass

A bounded sensitivity grid is warranted for mortality access/effect strength,
synthetic support allocation, and machine-task elasticity before considering
promotion. A migration model, single-year ages, or 237-economy expansion is not
required for that pass.
