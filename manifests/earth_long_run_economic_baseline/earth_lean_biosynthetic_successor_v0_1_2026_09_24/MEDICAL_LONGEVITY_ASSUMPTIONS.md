# Medical and longevity assumptions

The technology timeline is a boundary source. `MED-MOD-RADIATION` and
`MED-MOD-GENERATIONS` are off-Earth milestones and are not converted into Earth
population or longevity multipliers.

The selected experimental access path is:

| Tier | 2100 | 2160 | 2226 | Mortality effect |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Broad advanced medicine | 0% | 75% | 99.5% | Age-interpolated multiplier: 0.90 at age 0, 0.70 at 65, 0.50 at 80, 0.35 at 100+ |
| High-end longevity | 2% | 10% | 30% | 0.55 multiplier for the covered share at age 65+ |
| Extreme sustained rejuvenation | 0% | 0.01% | 0.1% | 0.10 multiplier for the covered share at age 100+ |

Access is interpolated linearly. Effects are mixed across access shares and
remain bounded mortality reductions. Terminal mortality at age 155+ remains
positive. There is no immortality or fixed quasi-immortal population fraction.

The schedule is a compact LOOM setting calibration based on the recovered
lineage. It was not fitted to an endpoint. It does not claim to reconstruct
cohort-level adoption, disease incidence, or unequal access within countries.
