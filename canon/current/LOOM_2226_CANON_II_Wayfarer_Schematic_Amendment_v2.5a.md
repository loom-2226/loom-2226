# LOOM 2226 — CANON II WAYFARER SCHEMATIC AMENDMENT v2.5a

**Status:** GOVERNING SCHEMATIC / PHYSICAL-PACKAGING AMENDMENT  
**Parent authority:** `canon/current/LOOM_2226_CANON_II_Engineering_Ships_Operations_v2.5.md`  
**Scope:** Reference courier Wayfarer planetary-access launch packaging only

This amendment preserves the inherited semi-recessed planetary launch packaging, now under CANON II v2.5. **The reference water/remass/fuel/mass and six-mode torch values are changed by CCR-2026-0004 in CANON II v2.5**, not silently held to the v2.4 ledger. Outside that scoped update, machine physics, thermal requirements and operating doctrine remain unchanged.

## 1. Integrated planetary-access launch bay — CANON

The reference courier carries its dedicated atmospheric courier launch in a **semi-recessed, unpressurized launch bay integrated into the mothership primary structure**. This supersedes the provisional external-sidecar attachment concept for the reference schematic.

The launch is a normal carried mission component for orbit-to-atmosphere-to-surface access. In the stowed state it is **substantially enclosed and protected** by the Wayfarer structure and external protective doors/panels. The bay itself remains vacuum and is not a pressurized hangar.

Crew transfer is through a pressure-tight connection from the inhabited ship so routine boarding does not require EVA.

```text
REFERENCE LAUNCH-BAY GRAMMAR
semi-recessed / substantially enclosed stowage
unpressurized bay
pressure-tight crew-transfer interface
primary-frame structural attachments
power / data / service / refuel umbilicals
protective external doors or panels
launch departs laterally from the mothership
```

The launch bay is part of the reference courier's physical packaging and mass-state model. Launch `DOCKED / ABSENT` state, attachment state and resulting mass distribution shall be explicit in any geometry, flight or relational-domain solution that depends on them.

## 2. Phase-2 packaging reservation

The current engineering reservation for the launch bay is **approximately x = 16–28 m from the bow**, overlapping the forward service / mass-center region.

This is a design-baseline reservation, not a frozen launch length or exact attachment station. Final bay depth, launch dimensions, door topology, ground-vehicle packaging and whether minor portions of the launch project beyond the nominal ~9 m main-body envelope remain open pending detailed physical closure.

The launch bay shall not be represented as a large pressurized aircraft hangar. It is a compact structural cradle / boat-bay analogue sized around the dedicated courier launch.

## 3. Relationship to existing reference-courier schematic grammar

The following current reference schematic elements remain in force:

```text
4 axial longerons
4 major working-fluid / remass tanks in quadrature
4 major deployable radiator assemblies in quadrature
1 axial primary fusion-torch / magnetic-nozzle system
```

The integrated launch bay is mission hardware and may introduce controlled local asymmetry. It does not replace the fundamental thrust-axis structural symmetry of the mothership.

## 3A. Phase 1b mission courier systems, distinct from planetary launch

The mission-courier *systems* (19.450370894400045 t) move to a forward, outboard equipment sector **x14–17.5 m, r4.6–6.1 m, az235–305°**. The service arm is a separate equipment sector **x14–17.5 m, r2–4.3 m, az225–315°**. This does **not** move the integrated planetary-access launch itself or cancel its pressure-tight crew-transfer and semi-recessed bay rules. External mounts, MMOD and strake heat qualification remain OPEN. The four in-core D–He3 lobes, separate C-lite tank and 5% liquid-basis ullage are governed by CANON II v2.5 §13.3.

## 4. Superseded visual assumption

Any generated render, prompt, sketch or provisional schematic showing the courier launch as a permanently exposed external sidecar is non-governing for reference-courier packaging after this amendment.

Generated imagery remains visual reference only; deterministic physical geometry is controlled by canon plus the active engineering packaging plan.
