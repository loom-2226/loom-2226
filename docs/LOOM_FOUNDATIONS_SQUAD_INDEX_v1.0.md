## The two squads

### The Adults With Red Pens
Daniele Oriti · Bianca Dittrich · Sumati Surya · Fotini Markopoulou · Lee Smolin · Emily Adlam · Renate Loll · Seth Lloyd

**Job:** try to kill weak science, expose answer-smuggling, demand observables/scaling/causal consistency and sharpen identity/information criteria.

### The Respectably Unsupervised
Stephen Wolfram · Julian Barbour · Donald Hoffman · Bernardo Kastrup · Federico Faggin · Eric Weinstein · A. Garrett Lisi · Nassim Haramein

**Job:** ask radical questions, supply heterodox comparators, preserve useful failed theories and provide explicit negative controls against seductive nonsense.

### Border-Crosser
Sara Imari Walker — attached to both squads for causal architecture, information, assembly theory and complexity metrology.

## Workstream cross-reference

### Geometrogenesis / continuum
Oriti, Dittrich, Surya, Markopoulou, Loll; Wolfram/Barbour as alternative-question sources.

### Causality / chronology / time
Surya, Markopoulou, Smolin, Loll, Adlam; Barbour and Wolfram as alternative architectures.

### Identity / relational observables
Adlam, Lloyd and relevant discrete/gauge observable work; Faggin/Hoffman as explicitly speculative question generators.

### Matter / information / control
Lloyd and Walker; Oriti/Lam as non-spatiotemporal structural ancestry. Walker is question/metrology ancestry, not M2 evidence.

### Observer reconstruction
Oriti, Adlam and Markopoulou on the serious side; Wolfram, Hoffman and Kastrup as increasingly speculative comparators.

### Synthetic identity/personhood
Adlam/Lloyd constrain the questions; Faggin/Hoffman/Kastrup supply philosophical/speculative schools; Walker contributes causal/history-sensitive organization.

### Xeno interpretation
Walker supplies complexity/assembly/metrology; Wolfram supplies bounded/computational reconstruction ideas; speculative metaphysics stays actor/institution-level unless independently earned.

### Failed futures / radical-science sociology
Lisi with Distler/Garibaldi, Weinstein and Haramein are explicit methodological/history cases. The point is neither ridicule nor secret endorsement: it is to preserve how radical programs succeed, fail, attract criticism and influence institutions.
