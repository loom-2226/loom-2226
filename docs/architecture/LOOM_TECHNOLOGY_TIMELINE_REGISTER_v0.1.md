# LOOM — Technology Timeline Register v0.1

**Created:** 2026-09-23  
**Primary change class:** `class:engineering` (non-executable simulation input/interface reference)  
**Status:** PROVISIONAL MODEL-INPUT REGISTER; NOT CANON; NOT A CALIBRATED TECHNOLOGY MODEL  
**Horizon:** 2026 onward, with existing fictional 2226 history recorded solely as a comparison/reference scenario.  
**Consumer:** Codex / Earth-to-Solar-System simulation technology and agent-decision design.  
**Mutation scope:** this document only. No runtime, canon, SQLite, model parameters, research-lab objects, or release changes.

## 1. Authority and operating rule

This register extracts *fictional-history observations* from the current LOOM canon, separates them from proposed model interfaces, and explicitly records missing information. Sources [S0–S4] are linked below. It is **not** a new governing chronology, an external scientific forecast, a scientific qualification, a canon change, or evidence that the simulation will reach any fictional 2226 state. The 2026 empirical baseline and any numeric costs, production capacities, efficiencies, population uptake and diffusion rates still require independently sourced input. Fictional reference dates must never be treated as observations of the real future.

**Critical rule:** Canon is a comparator, not a destination or validation target. Do not fit parameters backward from canonical 2226 population, settlement geography, industry, chronology, wealth, synthetics, fleet count, or propulsion adoption. Earth is the measured starting world; Earth orbit, Luna and Mars are possible early opportunities, not guaranteed milestones. All later destinations are selected endogenously. If the simulated history diverges, retain the divergence and compare it against the reference; do not repair the simulated output to match canon.

Reference period is **not** `earliest_enabled_date`. An innovation must be discovered/demonstrated, physically feasible, adequately funded and supplied, manufactured, certified if necessary, and adopted before an actor may use it. An event such as a probe launch or an archaeological discovery is not itself a deployable industrial capacity. Absence of a dated fact means `UNKNOWN`, **not** zero, never, or a guessed year. The historical 2017 discovery is recorded as canon-sourced here rather than claimed as independently externally audited.

## 2. Smallest model-facing schema

For each milestone preserve: `milestone_id`, `technology_id`, `family`, `reference_period`, `capability_change`, `prerequisites`, `resource_requirements`, `performance`, `evidence_status`, `source_ids`. These extend the seven essential substantive fields with identity, family and source traceability. `reference_period` is a string, permitting ranges (`2100–2140`), approximate dates (`c. 2221`), `BY_2226_UNDATED`, and `UNKNOWN`; do not parse it as a mandatory unlock year. Actual simulation fields such as `discovery_time`, `demonstration_time`, `deployment_time`, `local_capacity`, `adoption_share`, `unit_cost`, and `effective_performance` are **endogenous outputs or separately calibrated inputs**, not values supplied by this document.

**Evidence codes:** `C_HISTORY` = dated fictional history stated in [S1]; `C_STATE` = capability stated to exist in fictional 2226, without an earned first date; `DESIGN` = proposed simulation interface or prerequisite, not a sourced historical claim; `OPEN` = missing date, magnitude or engineering route. Combined codes describe mixed rows. Source links never upgrade `DESIGN` to fictional or empirical fact.

**Minimum invariant:** track (1) *knowledge/demonstration*, (2) *operable technology*, and (3) *installed local capacity* independently. Record capability availability per actor/location; no global binary `tech_unlocked` switch. Performance is mode-, route-, environment- and process-specific. Costs require a declared currency, base year, physical denominator and accounting scope; a bare numerical cost is invalid.

**Separation that must survive implementation:** reactor fuel ≠ reaction mass ≠ available electric power ≠ torch jet power ≠ waste-heat rejection [S1 §5.2; S2 §§2–3]. Synthetic persons ≠ non-person machine-task equivalents [S4]. Scientific knowledge ≠ flight-grade material ≠ installed propulsion hardware [S2 §3]. Physical metric certification ≠ roadstead ≠ traffic control ≠ port [S0, S2]. Inside Sol, light-speed radio/laser outruns subluminal metric ships; across Loom links information travels on physical packet carriers [S0–S2]. Torch, metric and Loom have different physical and economic roles [S0, S3].

## 5. Dependencies and agent behavior — proposed, NOT historical event requirements

A useful *minimal* dependency graph is: `SCI → candidate design → demonstration → ENE + MFG + CON + qualified inputs → installed capacity → TRN/EXT/HAB/AUT operations → trade/migration/investment decisions`, with reciprocal feedback from profits, discoveries and institutional financing. This is a modeling decomposition, not a claim that every technology follows an identical linear process. Off-world extraction can reduce import needs **only if** its refining, processing, energy, construction, maintenance and transport costs close; access to ore alone is insufficient. Autonomous task equivalents can supply work **only if** equipment, power, compute, supplies and permissible agent control exist. Biological settlement requires viable health, reproduction, life support, finance and recurrent replacement flows, not just a habitable bed count. Physical reach, transport service, emergency return and commercially insurable access are different states.

For each agent/location/year, evaluate physical feasibility; availability of validated design; input stock and ongoing throughput; finance; location-specific build capacity; certification/legal rights; reliability and supply continuity; then compare feasible alternatives. Research effort, technological diffusion and discoveries can change these states, but canonical milestone dates may appear **only in comparator reports or explicitly labeled reference-scenario experiments**, never as hidden runtime defaults. No required colonization beyond Mars; no fixed Moon or Mars success deadline; no population/economy endpoint reconciliation.

## 6. Open inputs / blocking unknowns for quantitative implementation

1. **2026 empirical baseline:** technology vintage, existing capacity, cost units/base year, power/industrial stock, transport and automation by region; [S0–S4] do not supply these.
2. **Stage transitions:** research effort to demonstration; demonstration to reliable use; qualified manufacture to volume production; diffusion, learning and retirement rates. The fictional timeline supplies reference observations only.
3. **Material/energy accounting:** fuel versus remass, supplied power versus jet power, waste-heat capacity, mine-to-refined-material yields, spare parts and equipment replacement.
4. **Space infrastructure:** access prices and annual lift, local construction rate, feedstock grade, industrial supply chains, site energy and habitat reliability; no assured launch costs or self-sufficiency years in these sources.
5. **Synthetic distinction:** baseline machines/task-equivalents versus synthetic persons, reproduction/instantiation rights, substrate and power, geography, installed automation and task productivity; no universal global automation scalar.
6. **Medicine/habitation:** site-specific radiation, low-g reproduction, longevity distributions, consumables/recycling, health service quality and failure risks. Fictional 2226 health claims are not calibration datasets.
7. **Discovery scenarios:** 1I mission funding, 2066 confirmation, 2125 access and subsequent forensic evidence are fictional reference events, not guaranteed exogenous interventions; no alien recipe or automatic Mc shortcut. If a scenario elects to include an event, its mechanism and evidence must be separately declared.
8. **Relational technologies:** no extrapolation of one certified ship's speed card, 2226 Mc production calibration or provisional fleet estimates into an economy-wide learning curve. Metric and Loom remain different mechanisms; environmental certification and arrival-state matching cannot be bypassed [S0–S3].
