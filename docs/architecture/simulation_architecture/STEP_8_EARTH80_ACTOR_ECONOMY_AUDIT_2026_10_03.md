# Step 8 — Earth-80 / actor-economy integration audit

**NON_CANON / PRE-ACTOR-EXECUTION / GOVERNED-EARTH-DERIVED**

## Finding

The validated Earth snapshot contains exactly **80 economically qualified economies**. Every one has annual 2026–2226 aggregate economic state and ten-sector state. The current 233-node institutional roster is geographically much narrower and must not be the sole mechanism by which countries participate in civilization propagation.

LOOM therefore separates the Earth opportunity environment from institutional actors. All 80 economies generate economic/industrial/demographic context from governed Earth state. Autonomous institutions become relevant to opportunities and must separately clear authority, access, finance, capability and physical gates.

COUNTRY OPPORTUNITY != COUNTRY CAPABILITY.
COUNTRY SECTOR CAPITAL != NAMED ACTOR CASH.
COUNTRY INDUSTRIAL SCALE != SPACECRAFT ACCESS.
OPPORTUNITY != PROJECT COMMITMENT.

## Earth authority available for coupling

For each economy the authority provides aggregate value added, gross output, investment, capital and population; ten sectors with value added, gross output, investment, capital and employment; four sector asset classes (machinery, structures, transport equipment, other assets) with capital and depreciation; demographics including births/deaths/median age/age bands; legacy labor/employment; and from 2100 onward where governed, biological/synthetic effective labor, synthetic population, machine task capacity and total effective labor.

At 2026, sector effective_labor is NULL; asset investment/replacement/expansion fields are NULL; biosynthetic labor rows do not yet exist. These are unavailable, not zero.

## Derived opportunity surface

EARTH_COUNTRY_OPPORTUNITY_2026_V0_3 contains **800 rows = 80 economies × 10 sectors**.

Each row preserves raw governed state and derives transparent diagnostics: sector global scale share, investment intensity, capital intensity, sector employment share, labor depth, demographic working-age support, productive asset composition and transport asset composition.

A scalar browse index exists only for diagnostics. Runtime actor selection must consume named dimensions appropriate to the causal trigger; it may not treat the scalar as a capability score.

## Use by actor role

Demand/offtake may consume sector output, scale, demographics and modeled constraints. Capital may consume aggregate/sector investment and capital as financing-market context. Project development may consume resource opportunity plus industrial/labor context. Manufacturing may consume relevant sector output/capital/employment/assets. Transport/infrastructure may consume TRANSPORT_LOGISTICS and asset composition. Labor/settlement may consume demographics and legacy labor at 2026, with biosynthetic/machine labor only when governed. Certification/information/services may consume their sector context without gaining legal authority or information possession.

Named actors still require their own willingness, financial capacity, technical capability, access, contracts and other role-specific gates.

## Country participation

A country does not require a pre-seeded named space actor to generate an opportunity. A country opportunity may recruit domestic actors where a qualified relationship exists, cross-border actors where access permits, context/market nodes, or later endogenous institutions.

This avoids both a US-centric future caused by the seed's named-actor geography and fictional 2026 space agencies invented merely to give all 80 economies an agent.

## Actor geography gap

The actor seed has no structured country/jurisdiction field. Geographic references mostly live in names and basis_2026 prose. Production matching needs typed jurisdiction links with evidence status and relation semantics such as HOME, STATE_AUTHORITY, OPERATING_PRESENCE, MARKET_ACCESS, PROVIDER_SITE or MULTINATIONAL. Geography must not be inferred from category alone.

## Long-run rule

The surface must be computed from year-specific governed Earth state, not frozen at 2026. New labor dimensions become available when their authority begins. Named actor state evolves endogenously and must not be overwritten by later aggregate country values.

Earth drives the environment. Actors drive decisions. Transactions/projects alter causal state. No layer gets to impersonate another.
