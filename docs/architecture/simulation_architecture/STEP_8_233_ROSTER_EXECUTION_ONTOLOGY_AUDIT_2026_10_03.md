## Why retain the other 43

Non-autonomous nodes encode useful 2026 structure. Examples include public equity markets, the Shanghai STAR Market, Artemis Accords, export-control regimes, LunaNet, launch/ground-site infrastructure, mineral disclosure/classification rules, historical space-resource firms, proposed institutions, composite customer classes and terrestrial labor/governance analogs.

They should constrain, inform, connect or seed later endogenous institutions. They should not independently decide to finance, buy, insure, launch or build projects.

## Deliberate labor exception

LABOR has only one autonomous 2026 institutional actor after analog regimes are correctly removed from execution. This is accepted at the 2026 boundary rather than inventing fictional off-world labor institutions.

Settlement/labor analog nodes remain available as model templates. New labor/governance actors should emerge only after causal population/settlement state exists and an institutional birth condition is satisfied.

## Activation rule

Candidate identity + 2026 functional status + autonomous execution ontology are all required before a node may become RELEVANT.

Markets, regimes, infrastructure objects, analogs, proposals, historical entities and composite abstractions may never acquire consequential authority merely because their seed category matches an activation trigger.
