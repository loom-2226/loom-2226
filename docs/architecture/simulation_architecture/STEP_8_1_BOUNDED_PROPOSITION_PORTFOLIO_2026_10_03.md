# Step 8.1 - Bounded proposition formation and portfolio competition

**NON_CANON / UNPROMOTED / EXPLORATORY-INTENT ONLY**

## Purpose

Step 8.1 converts a RELEVANT autonomous actor into a bounded exploratory decision without granting transaction, project, budget, capability, access, facility, or physical-state authority.

Causal sequence:

Earth/world opportunity -> bounded actor neighborhood -> RELEVANT actor -> staged proposition screen -> portfolio competition -> EXPLORE or DEFER.

A proposition is an investigation object, not a project.

## Staged gates

P0 AWARENESS requires governed relevance matching.
P1 MANDATE requires a role relevant to the Earth sector.
P2 MATERIALITY evaluates a role-specific vector of named Earth dimensions. The generic opportunity scalar is forbidden for runtime intent.
P3 PLAUSIBILITY requires positive governed economic context. It does not prove technical feasibility.
P4 EXPLORATORY AFFORDABILITY compares an explicit best-estimate investigation cost with the estimated 2026 actor-capacity bootstrap. This authorizes investigation only.
P5 ROLE INTEREST requires the role-specific materiality/plausibility screen.
P6 PORTFOLIO COMPETITION ranks viable candidates and admits only bounded actor attention.

## Role vector and portfolio

Different roles consume different combinations of sector scale, investment intensity, capital intensity, labor depth, demographic support, productive-asset share and transport-asset share.

Transforms, weights and exploratory costs are explicit sensitivity-testable model parameters, not empirical claims about institutional psychology.

Exploration attention derives from the actor's 2026 scale score and grows sub-linearly. It is organizational attention, not spendable project capital. Ranking is deterministic.

EXPLORE allocates exploratory attention to a viable proposition.
DEFER means the proposition passed all pre-portfolio gates but lost scarce attention to another proposition.

The contract reserves IGNORE, WATCH and REJECT for later richer belief/history behavior. Neither EXPLORE nor DEFER grants ACTIVE_TRANSACTIONAL.

## First governed test

Fixture: all ten Australia 2026 country-sector contexts from validated Earth snapshot earth-v0-1-9934d0ac-20260925, current actor baseline, execution ontology and jurisdiction links.

Observed:
- 10 Earth contexts.
- 57 actors with at least one viable candidate.
- 121 proposition decisions.
- 65 EXPLORE.
- 56 DEFER.
- deterministic replay passes.
- at least one actor has both EXPLORE and DEFER, proving portfolio competition.
- every proposition remains non-transactional and no actor gains consequential-action authority.

## Current limitations and next boundary

Actors in the same category currently share role-vector weights. Differentiation comes from relevance/jurisdiction, lifecycle, exploratory affordability, institutional scale and portfolio capacity.

Actor-specific beliefs, information possession, relationships, experience, strategic history, prior success/failure, risk tolerance and learned behavior are not yet attractiveness inputs. They should enter from governed actor state, not opaque personality coefficients.

Counterparty recruitment is not enabled yet. Next, an EXPLORE proposition decomposes missing dependencies and emits typed requests for transport, buyer/offtake, finance, insurance, supplier, certification or registry support. Requests may activate counterparties but cannot create a project until role-specific gates clear.
