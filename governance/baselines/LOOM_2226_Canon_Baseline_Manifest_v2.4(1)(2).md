## 2.4 Institutional / security / societal integration — 24 August 2026

Promoted into the existing runtime set without adding source files:

- **Solar versus interstellar communications:** inside Sol, radio/laser at `c` outruns every certified subluminal metric ship; metric carries urgency, people and custody-bound physical payloads, not faster-than-light information. Across Loom links, legal, financial, scientific and personal synchronization still requires physical packet carriage.
- **Jurisdiction and law enforcement:** no single Solar police authority exists. Civil jurisdiction remains local; registry/charter authority, treaty obligations, port/gateway control, standards bodies, insurers and security/rescue networks overlap by function.
- **Metric interdiction:** security doctrine concentrates on acquisition/collapse volumes, roadsteads, ports, certification access, repair/refuel and other endpoints. Certification revocation can deny trusted operation and infrastructure access but is not a magical remote drive-disable.
- **Military doctrine:** sensor/track quality, missiles/drones/kinetics/directed energy, EW, thermal/signature management and delegated mission command are canonical at qualitative level. Torch is tactical maneuver; Metric is Solar operational/strategic repositioning; Loom is interstellar. Exact force structure and orders of battle remain OPEN.
- **Habitat attack taboo:** deliberate mass-casualty attacks on pressurized habitats or equivalent life-support infrastructure are treated as WMD-adjacent conduct under strong treaty/customary-law regimes; exact statutes and jurisdiction vary.
- **Merchant reserve:** commercial torch shipping is the strategic sealift base. Reserve chartering, remass tankers, modular conversions, casualty evacuation and war-risk insurance are canonical structures; exact mobilization percentages and auxiliary counts remain provisional/open.
- **Synthetic crew sovereignty:** requisition of a vessel does not itself grant root/patch/reconciliation authority over recognized synthetic crew.
- **Prize/salvage law:** soft capture, boarding, causal-provenance audit, neutral admiralty practice and insurer subrogation exist across many jurisdictions; no single universal prize code or exclusive court list is promoted.
- **Societal consequences:** life-support infrastructure can become coercive power without creating blanket legal permission to weaponize it; synthetic compute/substrate geography is politically important but its embodiment/residency split remains OPEN; gerontocratic pressure and body debt are canonical qualitative consequences without inventing an immortality cohort or repossessable body parts.
- **Artifact economy:** the existing Partition remains multidimensional. No 45/40/15 ownership split is promoted; the exact 6,572 kg working ledger remains provisional.
