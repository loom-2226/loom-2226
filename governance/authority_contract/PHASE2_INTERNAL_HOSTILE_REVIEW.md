## Verdict

**ACCEPTABLE ARCHITECTURE, SUBSTANTIVE REPAIR REQUIRED BEFORE PHASE 2 CLOSURE.**

No finding requires reopening D2.1 or D2.2. Four findings require repair.

## MAJOR M-01 — UNKNOWN mixes epistemic availability with governance admissibility

D2.3 defines UNKNOWN using an admissible value for intended use and treats evidence found but not admitted as UNKNOWN for that use. A proposition can be asserted in an evidence plane while quarantined or inadmissible to a consumer. Governance inadmissibility must not erase epistemic availability.

**Repair:** UNKNOWN is absence of an asserted value in the relevant proposition/perspective, not absence of a value admissible for a use. Admissibility belongs to standing/governance. Evidence found but not admitted may coexist with an asserted source claim.

## MAJOR M-02 — Warrant roots mix roots with derivative relations

Canon warrant appears normally derivative from explicit authorization under canon governance. Institutional/normative warrant requires recognition of jurisdiction/competence. Formal warrant supports entailment under definitions/premises, not empirical applicability of premises.

**Repair:** preserve the five adopted categories as warrant families/sources, but do not claim all are primitive roots. Primitive-root status remains unresolved until recognition rules are defined.

## MAJOR M-03 — Standing risks circularity with qualification

If standing considers qualification while qualification consumes standing, the model permits standing -> qualification -> standing.

**Repair:** define pre-qualification standing from semantics, warrant, dependencies, scope, time, conflict, authorization and uncertainty. Qualification separately assesses that object against a contract/use. Effective standing for use may then include qualification outcome. A qualification decision cannot depend on an effective-standing result that already depends on that same decision.

## MAJOR M-04 — Perspective and admissibility are distinct

The offworld case exposes two independent questions: where a proposition is known/believed/available, and whether a consumer may use it.

**Repair:** separate epistemic perspective/information domain from use admissibility. Phase 3 owns knowledge-state representation; Phase 4 owns runtime enforcement; Phase 2 defines the semantic distinction.

## MINOR m-01 — Normalize proposition/assertion/claim

Define proposition as semantic content in context; assertion as an occurrence/source/agent/governance act asserting it; and claim as the governed contract object if retained. New supporting sources create new assertions/support, not automatically new propositions.

## MINOR m-02 — Controlled vocabularies remain open

This is acceptable if later contract/state/qualification work explicitly owns token freezing. Phase 2 must not claim a finished serialization ontology.

## MINOR m-03 — Conflict needs an overlap rule

Conflict requires compatible normalized subject/predicate, overlapping applicable scope/context/time, and mutually incompatible asserted content. Exact algorithms can remain downstream.

## MINOR m-04 — Bridges produce new dependents

An authorized bridge permits substitution for an operation but must not mutate the original proposition. Its output is a new dependent claim/state with bridge lineage.
