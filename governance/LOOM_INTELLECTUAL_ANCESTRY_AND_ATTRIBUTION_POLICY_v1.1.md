# LOOM 2226 — Intellectual Ancestry & Attribution Policy v1.1

**Status:** REPOSITORY GOVERNANCE — research/speculative artifacts; does not itself promote content into canon/runtime.  
**Date:** 7 September 2026  
**Supersedes:** v1.0 for new work; v1.0 retained as history.

## 1. Purpose

LOOM combines original experiments, hard-SF postulates, external scientific ancestry, philosophy, anomalous phenomenology, worldbuilding and software. Credit and epistemic status must survive migration among those layers.

This policy prevents:
- **credit amnesia**;
- **epistemic laundering**;
- **roster/ancestry confusion**;
- **witness-authority confusion**;
- **convergence inflation**.

## 2. Four-part attribution model

Every material external influence is separated into:
1. **Original source claim** — what the source actually proposed, measured, proved, argued or reported.
2. **LOOM interpretation** — why it matters to a LOOM question.
3. **LOOM extension** — the new model, analogy, hypothesis, mechanic or fiction we created from it.
4. **LOOM result** — what our own analysis/experiment/canon process independently establishes.

## 3. Four role classes

### MAIN_ROSTER
Prefer living, active and potentially contactable people whose current work can review or enrich a problem.

### ANCESTRY
Unrestricted by era/living status. Historical thinkers, prior programs, critics, failed theories and foundational works belong here.

### BRIDGE
A current thinker whose work genuinely crosses squad functions.

### CASE_WITNESS
A person whose testimony/claims/provenance materially belong in the research corpus but whose inclusion confers no methodological or scientific authority.

These roles may overlap only when explicitly labeled.

## 4. Permanent squads

### A — The Adults With Red Pens
Scientific/mathematical review and kill tests.

### B — The Respectably Unsupervised
Radical reframing, heterodox comparison, metaphysical stimulus, useful failures and negative controls.

### C — The Freaks With Clipboards
Anomalous phenomenology, witness/sensor provenance, classification, cross-domain case handling, instrument-first investigation and skeptical ordinary-explanation controls.

Squad names are internal LOOM humor. Epistemic status attaches to claims, not people.

## 5. Main roster versus lineage

Do not put a historical thinker on a current roster merely because they are important. Put them in ancestry unless there is a specific reason to maintain a historical honorary seat.

Main roster answers: **who would we read/contact now?**

Ancestry answers: **who built the intellectual road we are on?**

Example: Pauli and Jung belong in Squad B ancestry; their modern descendants may occupy current/bridge roles.

## 6. Anomalous phenomenology rule

For Squad C material, preserve separately:
- raw report;
- witness statement;
- physical/sensor artifact;
- custody/provenance;
- environmental context;
- investigator interpretation;
- competing mundane explanations;
- replication/independence status;
- residual status.

`UNEXPLAINED` is never automatically promoted to `EXOTIC`.

Witness sincerity is not event accuracy. Researcher prestige is not case validation. Skeptical explanation is not automatically correct either.

## 7. Three-squad convergence rule

When Freaks phenomenology, Unsupervised reframing and Red-Pen formalization converge, the result may be promoted in **research priority**.

It is prohibited to count the three intellectual traditions as independent evidence for the underlying claim.

## 8. Minimum ancestry record

Where available preserve:
- author(s)/collaboration;
- title;
- year;
- venue;
- DOI/arXiv/stable primary URL;
- role (`method`, `ancestry`, `comparison`, `question`, `negative_control`, `fiction_ancestry`, `phenomenology`, `case_witness`);
- epistemic tag;
- derived LOOM branch/work item;
- explicit `does_not_establish` note where confusion is plausible;
- prominent criticism/counterweight where materially relevant.

## 9. Persistence

Attribution is immutable historical metadata through:
- `QUALIFICATION`;
- `PROMOTION_CANDIDATE`;
- `RETIRED`;
- `GHOST`.

Failed theories keep both their original proponents and the critics who killed weak versions.

## 10. Canon/runtime promotion

A research citation never changes canon merely through repetition.

When an externally influenced concept is promoted, the promotion artifact identifies:
1. external ancestry;
2. established portion;
3. LOOM extrapolation/fictional portion;
4. independent LOOM evidence/result;
5. whether the source inspired the question or constrains the answer.

Locked canon should not be patched ad hoc solely to add citations; queue source-level provenance for the next normal revision unless omission causes an immediate material attribution problem.

## 11. High-strangeness / xeno firewall

No UAP, paranormal, psi, NDE, cryptid, religious, witness or anomalous-experience corpus may calibrate M1/M2/hidden topology or automatically connect to xenoarchaeology.

No alien artifact may retroactively validate historical anomalous claims.

If a future xeno site exhibits independently instrumented anomalous behavior, that behavior receives its own evidence object and analysis.

## 12. Copyright/storage

Store detailed original summaries, metadata, source links, counterarguments and lawful excerpts. Do not wholesale mirror copyrighted papers for convenience.

Public-domain/openly redistributable material can be vendored when useful for reproducibility, with license/provenance recorded.

## 13. Governing maxim

> **Credit the people who gave us the idea. Credit the people who killed the bad version of it. Credit the witnesses for what they actually reported. Credit ourselves only for the part we actually did.**
