# LOOM 2226 — Intellectual Ancestry & Attribution Policy v1.0

**Status:** REPOSITORY GOVERNANCE — applies to research/speculative artifacts; does not by itself promote content into canon or runtime.  
**Date:** 7 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

LOOM deliberately combines original research experiments, hard-science-fiction postulates, worldbuilding, software, external scientific ancestry, philosophy and radical speculation. As ideas migrate across those layers, intellectual credit and epistemic status must survive the migration.

This policy prevents two failures:

1. **credit amnesia** — a useful external idea becomes "a LOOM idea" after enough internal rewrites;
2. **epistemic laundering** — repeated citation or canon compatibility makes a speculative idea look scientifically established.

## 2. Four-part attribution model

Whenever external work materially informs a LOOM artifact, distinguish explicitly:

### A. Original source claim
What the cited author(s) actually proposed, demonstrated, measured, proved or argued.

### B. LOOM interpretation
Why LOOM considers that source relevant to a particular research, engineering, fiction or epistemic problem.

### C. LOOM extension
Any new hypothesis, analogy, model, fictional consequence or design move created by LOOM from the source.

### D. LOOM result
What LOOM's own formal analysis, simulation, experiment, validation or canon decision actually establishes.

A paragraph may contain more than one category only when the transitions are unmistakably labelled.

## 3. Minimum ancestry record

A materially inherited idea SHALL preserve, where available:

- author(s) / collaboration;
- title;
- year;
- publication venue;
- DOI, arXiv identifier, or stable primary URL;
- LOOM use category: `method`, `ancestry`, `comparison`, `question`, `negative_control`, `fiction_ancestry`;
- epistemic tag of the imported material;
- derived LOOM branch/work item;
- an explicit "does not establish" note whenever over-reading is plausible.

## 4. Reviewer squads

The permanent Foundations & Consequences external-thinking squads are defined in:

`research/foundations_consequences/SQUAD_CHARTER_v1.0.md`

- **The Adults With Red Pens** — scientific/methodological referee squad.
- **The Respectably Unsupervised** — radical-question, heterodox, metaphysical and negative-control squad.
- **Sara Imari Walker** — Border-Crosser attached to both.

The squad names are internal LOOM humor. Scientific status is recorded at the level of the source claim, not assigned wholesale to a person by squad membership.

## 5. Persistence through lifecycle

Attribution is immutable historical metadata.

When an item becomes:
- `QUALIFICATION` — keep ancestry;
- `PROMOTION_CANDIDATE` — keep ancestry and add independent LOOM evidence;
- `RETIRED` — keep ancestry and reason for retirement;
- `GHOST` — keep ancestry, technical criticism and the historical/fictional reason it remains useful.

Deleting a citation because a theory failed is prohibited when that theory materially shaped the work.

## 6. Promotion into canon, engineering or runtime

Research citations do not automatically propagate into production/canon documents.

When an externally influenced concept is formally promoted, the promotion artifact SHALL identify:

1. the external ancestry;
2. which part is established science/mathematics;
3. which part is LOOM extrapolation or fictional postulate;
4. which part was independently derived/tested in LOOM;
5. whether the source merely inspired the question or constrains the answer.

This preserves proper credit without turning the canon corpus into an indiscriminate literature dump.

## 7. Mainstream, heterodox and failed work

LOOM does not use social status as evidence.

- Peer review does not make an unrelated extrapolation true.
- Outsider status does not make an idea wrong.
- Mathematical elegance does not create empirical support.
- Unpopularity does not create suppressed truth.
- Failure can still produce methods, counterexamples, engineering ancestry, sociology or fiction.

Where a prominent technical criticism exists, it SHALL be cited alongside the proposal when the criticism is materially relevant. Example: Lisi's E8 proposal is paired with Distler & Garibaldi's representation-theoretic no-go critique in the Foundations corpus.

## 8. High epistemic hazard

`HIGH` or `EXTREME` hazard means:
- stronger labels;
- explicit counterevidence/counterarguments where available;
- clear source-vs-LOOM separation;
- independent qualification before scientific promotion.

It does **not** mean "do not explore."

## 9. Research-corpus storage

LOOM may store:
- bibliographic metadata;
- source links;
- detailed original summaries and analysis;
- equations/facts within lawful quotation and citation limits;
- small necessary excerpts;
- public-domain or appropriately licensed source material where useful for reproducibility.

LOOM SHALL NOT wholesale mirror copyrighted papers merely for convenience. Stable primary links plus detailed annotated dossiers are the default.

## 10. Credit in fiction

External thinkers may serve as intellectual ancestry for fictional schools, debates or institutions, but LOOM should not simply transplant living people as fictional mouthpieces without an explicit creative reason.

Preferred pattern:

`real intellectual ancestry -> transformed 21st/22nd-century debate -> distinct 2226 school/institution`

The source remains cited in design notes even when the final fictional institution has an original name and history.

## 11. Governing maxim

> **Credit the people who gave us the idea. Credit the people who killed the bad version of it. Credit ourselves only for the part we actually did.**
