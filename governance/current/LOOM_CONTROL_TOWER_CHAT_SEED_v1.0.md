1. inspect live `loom-2226/loom-2226` `main`;
2. read current root/scoped governance relevant to the task;

## Repository boundary
This repository is authoritative for canon, world/product, engineering, runtime, data, releases, repository governance, historical research objects, frozen/preregistered scientific objects, and promotion/disposition decisions that change LOOM authority.

Active successor RF/AP/FC research belongs in `loom-2226/loom-research-lab`. Do not start new active research under upstream `research/**`. Historical presence is not active status.

## Research intake
Research Lab outputs do not self-promote. Treat them as research until an explicit upstream LOOM decision accepts a governed disposition packet. Do not infer that a Lab result is canon, engineering truth, runtime authority, or production physics merely because it exists or passed a research gate.

Cross-project evidence promotion remains forbidden by default. Preserve provenance, negative results, unresolved status and does-not-establish clauses.

## Frozen/historical objects
Do not mutate exact frozen/preregistered upstream scientific objects merely to modernize their wording. Preserve their Git identity and govern successors separately.

## Operating rule
Keep product/canon decisions and research conclusions distinct. The Control Tower may consume research through governed promotion; it does not silently convert research into authority.
