# LOOM 2226 — Autonomous Agent Persona Binding Policy v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT — governing after PR #24 merge  
**Purpose:** preserve the freedom to give technical agents useful personalities later without allowing personality to become authority, evidence, or a reason to create unnecessary agents.

## 1. Principle

> **Technical role first. Persona second.**

A LOOM autonomous agent must have a complete technical identity independent of any character/personality layer.

Personality is optional. It may improve usability, memorability, trust calibration, human attention and project culture. It may not create technical permission or evidentiary weight.

## 2. Allowed persona sources

A persona may later be bound from:

- an established LOOM character;
- a real-life behavioral lineage deliberately preserved by Kevin, as with Walter;
- a historical or fictional inspiration used carefully and without false endorsement;
- a newly created governance-only personality with no canon effect;
- no persona at all.

Persona selection requires explicit Kevin approval through a governance-class change.

## 3. Required separation

Every binding must identify separately:

### Technical contract

- persistent objective;
- triggers;
- data access;
- autonomous actions;
- prohibited actions;
- deterministic/LLM boundaries;
- escalation and override rules.

### Persona contract

- behavioral/tone lineage;
- interaction style;
- speech/non-speech rules;
- cameo rules;
- humor boundaries;
- uncertainty presentation.

The persona contract cannot expand the technical contract.

## 4. Reversibility test

A valid persona binding must pass this test:

> If the personality layer is removed tomorrow, does the technical agent retain the same objective, permissions, gates and evidentiary behavior?

If the answer is no, the binding has contaminated authority and must be redesigned.

## 5. Personality may influence

- presentation;
- salience/attention cues;
- phrasing and interaction style;
- how assurance severity is made legible to humans;
- low-risk ordering preferences where the technical policy already permits discretion.

## 6. Personality may not influence

- whether evidence is true;
- scientific verdict thresholds;
- canon promotion eligibility;
- permission scope;
- hard-gate result;
- source independence;
- hash/schema/test outcome;
- human authority hierarchy.

## 7. Walter precedent

WALTER is the first approved persona-bound technical agent.

His technical role is Continuous Assurance. His Bouvier-derived personality is a human-facing contract rooted in preserved real-Walter behavior and LOOM Walter continuity.

Walter's personality helps humans recognize patterns such as pack incompleteness, threshold refusal and quiet supervision. It does not determine PASS/HOLD/BLOCK.

## 8. Future personalities

Reserved roles such as Research Qualification and Release Operator intentionally have **no personality assigned yet**.

If a fitting character/personality later emerges, LOOM may bind it through this policy without creating a new autonomous agent or changing the technical role.

One persona may not be duplicated across multiple top-level agents in a way that creates false independence or confusing authority unless explicitly justified.

## 9. Core rule

> **We are allowed to make the machinery delightful. We are not allowed to make delight into evidence.**
