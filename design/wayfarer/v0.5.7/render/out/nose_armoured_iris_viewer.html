Limits of this test

An optional fixed 0.12 m-radius armour centre boss covers the zero-radius tip. With truncated leaves this gives ideal nominal area coverage but its circular lap joint and loaded seal remain unqualified. The fixed boss permanently obscures about 1% of the sampled Pilot/NAV forward cone. Outer and inner tiers are staggered ~0.413 m; flush closure across their seams has not been achieved.

The open-state blades turn into an opaque annulus. The pressure-head slot is an idealised axial interval, not a manufactured bearing, track, slot or actuating mechanism. An apparent collision-free state does not demonstrate ballistic protection, thermal, pressure or hardware qualification.
