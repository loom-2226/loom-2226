Important limitations

Vertices use metric metres and Rev 7 source positions. The faceted exterior is a triangulated concept mesh, not CAD manufacturing geometry; tessellation is visual only. Transparent patches are proposed pressure-bearing openings, not a qualified pane design. Shutter storage, interlayer support and sensor relocation remain unverified.
