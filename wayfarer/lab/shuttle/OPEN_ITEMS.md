## Physical research holds (not additional numerical gates)

- **Stage 1 pressure sphere:** Qualify titanium shell external-pressure buckling with hot material allowables, knockdown, hatch/penetrations, real seals, and actual reinforcement mass.
- **Stage 1 / Stage 2 entry and landing:** Verify Venus heatshield, structure/landing gear, rotor downwash, and surface-touchdown mechanics; the dry and water PASS screens do not certify entry.
- **Stage 2 dense-CO₂ torch ignition:** Demonstrate live magnetic-nozzle plume, CO₂ ingestion/recirculation, thermal load, nozzle survival and actual atmospheric transition at the computed handover altitude.
- **Stage 2 ground-vent surface cooling:** Qualify existing hot CO₂ exchanger, parked-rotor hub vent, ambient compressor, plume direction **up away from pod/door/ramp**, structure, and full hot-loop energy disposal. DS2/DS4 numerical PASS only if assumptions close.
- **Stage 2 reactor source, thermal coupling and limits:** Demonstrate reactor-to-rotor gas transfer efficiency and electrical interfaces, heat rejection and reactor waste accounting. Declared 2226 power hardware is not experimentally qualified.
- **Stage 2 torch-idle, research-only:** Independently resolve stable low-power turndown, zero-exhaust confinement, source-to-tipjet heat efficiency, mast/duct/rotating-joint mass and thermal survivability. **No torch-idle gate credit**.
- **Stage 2 pod-detached endurance:** D-pod is passive hours OUTPUT with no specified minimum target; select a mission requirement before inventing a pass/fail gate.
- **Stage 3 Titan rotor and plasma:** Validate atmospheric density above lower levels, cryogenic rotor/material performance, real handover and methane/nitrogen plume. Reaching the 60 km numerical model boundary is **not** a mission qualification.
- **Stage 3 light-world propulsion:** 1.2–1.5 T/W thrust is sized by formula, not flight-proven; qualify engine mass, sustained actuation and ground plume. Moon, Mars and **Mercury polar settlement** use chemical H₂/O₂ as BASELINE, electric water as RESEARCH ALTERNATIVE.
- **Stage 3 chemical energy:** ONLINE ~5.0–6.2 kWh/kg stoichiometric water covers *electrolysis electricity only*. Oxygen/hydrogen liquefaction, chilling, storage, fill plumbing and production plant remain OPEN. See Stage 3 source JSON provenance.
- **Stage 3 Mercury dayside:** Separate non-gating thermal sensitivity; dayside thermal-kit mass and site conditions OPEN. No dayside kit in default polar sortie.
- **Earth:** Spaceport access is the shuttle v1 research boundary; no Earth surface-entry gate was constructed or implied by Stages 1–3.

**Closure principle:** gather or demonstrate the specifically missing physics/geometry/mission requirement and rerun only the affected gates. No automatic promotion to canon or Wayfarer hardware authority.
