## Non-negotiable docking test

- **Same position != same ordinary velocity.** At illustrative equatorial Earth 200 km circular orbit, Wayfarer moves ~7.79 km/s inertially; a locally co-rotating craft at the same altitude moves ~0.48 km/s. Nominal mismatch ~7.31 km/s. For a 48.5 t craft, `(1/2)m Δv² ≈ 1.30 TJ` in that chosen local frame, before potential-energy changes or losses. This is a diagnostic kinetic-energy difference, not a drive-cost formula or global energy budget.
- **Weave shift** moves the field centre without leaving the hull with matching ordinary velocity (R1). The R3 local Loom hop is NOT granted; R7's natural arrival rule is chosen, not derived. Neither ordinary Weave nor the canonical *interstellar* Loom mode provides a free terminal velocity match.
- **Docking also requires** low relative translation and rotation, compatible nose/hatch/bay alignment, field shutdown or a tested overlap condition, and an actual mechanical restraint/transfer interface. Existing 12.8 × 3.8 m geometry and 0.8 m nose interface do not prove docking capture or latching. No cradle is assumed. Do not use the 15 m-radius active shuttle field inside Wayfarer's occupied hull without a field-interference result.
- Any mode must respect complete energy/linear and angular momentum exchange, thermal consequences and no free restored cycles. This is not relaxed by identifying an energy ledger or a Sun-frame bookkeeping convention.

## Ranking and recommendation

**Preferred practical fallback:** A, self-return, is the *only current reference-design route* with a complete attempted Earth orbit ascent card, though it retains torch, remass, heat and large hardware mass. It is not certified.

**Preferred non-torch, no-new-physics solution for inhabited worlds:** C *where real infrastructure already exists*; B is a possible ship-supported option but requires a second capable spacecraft and a capture system. Do not build that spacecraft just because we can describe it.

**Preferred Builder-specific research candidate:** D, narrowly scoped to whether the hull can establish a mutually compatible **complete relative state** with Wayfarer, as a controllable relational operation. A purely positional Weave dock (after conventional matching) is already consistent with existing equations and provides no magical recovery. Any velocity-changing version remains a permission proposal requiring a conservation-complete mechanism or stated fictional closure, NOT a derived result. No R3 local Loom hop or R7 natural-arrival rule is granted by this proposal.

**Small-body specialty:** E is worth retaining but cannot substitute for Earth-class recovery.
