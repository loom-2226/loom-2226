**Premise (DERIVED in this lane).** On the Weave field the craft carries its own pocket of air, so hull shape plays no aerodynamic role in field flight. A smooth capsule is the natural pure-field shape. Shape only matters for conventional flight, entry and stowage.

**Capsule geometry and fit.**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | **12.8 m × 3.8 m** capsule (cylinder with hemispherical ends) |
| Bay fit | The diameter is capped by the 3.8 m exit width (rolling does not help a round body). Standard 12.8 m length, so no docking collar is needed |
| Volume | ~131 m³ hull, **~113 m³ interior**: about twice the shuttle's |
| Field | Max radius **6.4 m** (shuttle: 8.55 m), the cleanest carriage. Differential ≤ 0.08%, about 0.05 g at a 71 g jink |
| Water | Floats on its own; no floats needed |
| Mass (rough, ASSUMPTION) | **~44 t dry**: same module and flywheel; no wings or fans; small reactor to charge the flywheel; all-round entry heat protection |

**Gives up:**
- wings and fans, so **no conventional fallback** (field failure means the torch, or ballistic flight plus flotation);
- lifting entry: L/D about 0.3 at best, little crossrange.

**So it is a Weave manoeuvring test bed or demonstrator, not a replacement for the shuttle.** It could ride in the bay instead of the shuttle on a test campaign. It looks the part.
