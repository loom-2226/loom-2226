## Part 1: energy from relational topography, with visible conservation intact

**As posed, the two requirements are mutually exclusive.**

- **Visible local conservation** means ∇·T_visible = 0 everywhere. Then any energy reaching the hull flows in **through spacetime**, from a physical partner or sector: the R1, R2 and R3 routes of the source closure. That is ordinary joule accounting.
- **A direct feed from the relational domain** puts a source term into the visible equations: ∇·T_visible = J_relational ≠ 0 wherever the field acts. That is exactly what Canon II §7.3 (∇·T = 0) forbids for physical sectors.
- **The precise form of "energy is emergent":** treat the relational configuration as a non-dynamical background in the visible action. Then ∇·T_visible = −(∂L/∂background)·∂(background), and energy is pumped in by the background's time dependence, like a driven oscillator. This is option (ii) above.
- **Whether that avoids free-work cycles** needs the relational action S_R, which is unspecified.

**Stop.** This is a foundational question for the Research Lab (S_R, §7.3), not a shuttle screen.

## Part 2: what existing physics already allows

The reactor in the previous screens came from booking the field's work **in Earth's frame**, i.e. from an Earth-fixed partner. A partner of effectively infinite mass absorbs momentum but no energy **in its own rest frame**. So the energy bill depends on which body the partner is:

| Partner | Onboard energy for the ascent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Earth, fixed (previous screens) | 1.56 TJ | Reactor plus waste heat |
| Earth's spin | 0.17 TJ available (11% of the need) | Insufficient alone |
| **The Sun (canon §7.1b's partner for the Metric)** | **0** | Heliocentric speed is unchanged: her velocity is redirected, as in a gravity assist |

**The rule the Sun partner imposes:** she must reach orbital speed with her new velocity pointing about **98.5° from Earth's orbital motion**. Her change of velocity must have a −1.08 km/s component along Earth's orbital direction. For an equatorial orbit that puts insertion **near the dawn or dusk terminator**. Rendezvous then means meeting Wayfarer at that point in its orbit.

**Where the energy really comes from:**
- During the ascent: heliocentric bookkeeping. There is **no reactor and no source waste heat**. Whatever the field itself dissipates is unknown and not booked.
- If she later dumps that momentum into Earth (aerobraking), the 1.5 TJ is ultimately drawn from **Earth's orbital energy**. That is a finite physical reservoir, so this is **not** a free-work cycle.

**Canon status:** this is the same law canon already uses for the Metric: the Sun as partner (§7.1b), with arrival velocity depending on heading (ENERGY-B, CCR-2026-0003). For the prototype it is **option B**, which kT did not choose at step 4; the current CCR uses option A, partner UNKNOWN.
