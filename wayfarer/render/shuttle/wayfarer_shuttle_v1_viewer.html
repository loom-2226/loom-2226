Wayfarer Shuttle

An uncrewed truck shuttle carrying a three-crew pressure pod that is also the rover. On Venus and Titan a detachable kit adds a 12 m tip-jet rotor and a house reactor. On airless and thin-air worlds it lands on chemical belly thrusters.
