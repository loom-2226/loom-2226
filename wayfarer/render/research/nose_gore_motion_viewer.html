Read this before deciding

This is a real-coordinate, constant-thickness rigid-panel swept-motion screen, not an engineered hinge. A zero collision count means none of the sampled panel material points intersects the modelled pressure hull, bow sensor solid or node-face spheres; the sample is not a proof of clearance. Adjacent petal self-collision, hinge bracket, seals, stops and actuation are not yet qualified.

The petal surface is shown as a thin 3D sheet. Collision testing uses 0.196 m effective armour thickness at three depth samples. The original crown's zero-angle test registers a small nominal pressure overlap because the simplified normal-layer construction differs from the source shell thickness geometry. The angular designs do not register this at zero.
