Read the limitations

A physically shared laminate is not necessarily redundant. The two-dome switch doubles full-capable material layers but does NOT prove independent failure survival: sealing, venting, gap pressurization and dynamic load transfer are absent.

The thermal value is a purposely conservative full-restraint stress scale. It is not a prediction of actual Wayfarer temperatures or stresses, and cannot be freely added to pressure stress as a certified worst-case.

Fracture threshold uses ideal surface flaw K = 1.12 × stress × √(πa), not an acceptance criterion. The full 7m material object and boundary ring have not been qualified. This demonstration cannot conclude that a seamless pressure dome is safe.
