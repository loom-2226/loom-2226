Pressure glazing and held 37-piece outer panel pattern

Blue/teal outer rings represent the 37 independently replaceable protective panels. Red lines show the inner pressure-frame layout. These are projections, not a coincident physical support grid: the layers sit at different axial depths, and parallax matters.

What this does not certify

The inner pressure layer must remain load bearing. Narrowing or relocating its mullions changes pane spans, pressure loads, crack paths and redundancy. Here it is a geometric visibility test only. The outer 37 panels carry no cabin pressure.
