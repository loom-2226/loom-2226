What changed in v0.5.7

  • Rev 7: the skin (kT's four decisions, Sol agreed). Strata over the whole habitat armour, bare at the bow where the dust hits; tunable-emissivity flanks with two modes, normal (light, shedding heat) and cold running (low-e, holding heat: about 17 h before the strakes must open). Shading only: no new shielding is implied. Strata's look is a placeholder.
  • Rev 7: 208 metric node faces, 13 rings x 16, flush on the hull. Placement is on HOLD (PR #184): 20 sit under the stowed strakes, 12 on the bay doors, 80 on the torch chain. Each face must carry the armour's g/cm2: as open holes they would drop the berth screen to 14.8.
  • Rev 7: external equipment. Retractable sensor turrets, flush RF arrays, laser terminals, star trackers, service ports, vents, crawler docks and grip points, grapple fixtures and EVA rails (amber). Most are located inside existing ledger lines; 1.4 t of new hardware from the reserve. Drone bays on HOLD.
  • Four longerons, sized. Five members (3 planks, 2 sills; 36.9 t at 65 MPa against 400) become four 0.40 m CFRP tubes in an X, matching canon. 21.2 t goes back to the engineering reserve. Tap Structure, then switch versions.
  • Joints counted. 8 titanium end fittings, a node-mount and hatch allowance (location open: the longeron lines sit under the stowed strakes), 4 bay-corner doublers: 3.3 t.
  • Berth viewports. One 0.35 m viewport at the outer end of every berth (10). Acrylic plugs carry the armour's and the ring's g/cm², so the berths stay storm-shelter grade with the shutter open. Tap Habitat decks.
  • Crown dome replaces the 8 nose windows. 25 m² of ceramic glazing inside r 2.8 m on an Osteon frame (8 roots forking into 16 edge-on blades round a clear 2.3 m centre pane); 16 tambour gores close it flush and stow in the brow ring. Pilots see 75% of the forward sky (was 22%). Use Dome open / closed; in Command deck try View from: Pilot or Standing.
  • Rev 4: the band's load path closed (after Sol's review): 14 x 4 cm titanium edge rings all the way round, a mid transom in the 1.6 m lounge, bolted mullion roots, and plate-sized 2226-ceramic panes (16 mm, 18 mm in the lounge). Band 5.9 t. New G5 checks.
  • Viewports everywhere (rev 3). A panoramic band round the L2 common deck (19 m², 1.6 m tall in the lounge) on Osteon titanium mullions; berth viewports up to 0.5 m; medical 2, storm shelter 2 (the plug continues through the water jacket), suit room 1, workshop 2. Every opening below the crown has an acrylic shield plug and a Strata shutter. ~48 m² of glazing in all. Tap Dome + shutters open.
  • Concepts, not in the build: external spines and visor sketches, Osteon longeron joints.
