## Single 2226 material extrapolation

**Name:** Adaptive Optogel Interlayer (AOI-2226), a *plausible fictional material family*, not a discovered chemical formula or technology-timeline authority.

One transparent, stable, optically matched polymer/ionogel composite containing:
1. A percolating **reversible crosslink network**, which can cycle between a mobile/viscoelastic gel and a stiffer glassy/solid-like gel (no complete dissolution or lost material). *Stiff* does not mean brittle pane, rigid hydraulic block or pressure-bearing outer shell.
2. A **rate-dependent dissipative network**: at fast shear or concentrated impacts, the interlayer transiently stiffens and dissipates energy **passively**. It cannot wait for computers to detect a microsecond rupture. Impact resistance is a research **target** pending actual high-rate tests, NOT proof the redundant dome survives.
3. Independently switchable **optical absorbers/electrochromic chromophores** embedded at optically small scales or at contiguous transparent interfacial coatings: tune visible brightness/glare and near-IR thermal load separately, with UV suppression and high clarity. This optical control is independent of mechanical state. Solar/thermal protection needs a real radiative/thermal balance; changing absorptivity may create heat in the stack.

This composite is more physically coherent than literal mass-scale liquid-to-crystal freezing. Both operational modes are **soft-matter states of the SAME closed inventory**. Reversible bonds tune relaxation and storage modulus; the nominal 2226 "solid" is a high-modulus, ductile glassy-gel regime, not a perfectly rigid structural wall.

## Minimal behaviour model for LOOM's 2226 experience

Two **independent** controllable dimensions, not a switch-specific spacecraft subsystem:

**Mechanical state `gel` or `locked`**
- `gel`: transparent, shape-following, dissipative viscoelastic medium; mobile on longer timescales, potentially strong high-rate shear response. NOT negligible bulk modulus.
- `locked`: transparent, reversible crosslinked **solid-like** damping layer with increased storage/shear stiffness, retaining ductility. Intended for special maneuvers, inspection/maintenance and active fragment capture experiments. It remains **non-pressure-bearing** and must not bond both pressure shells into one common structural failure body.
- Trigger: selective light/electric/thermal actuation of reversible links (the proposed 2226 control technology). **No claimed switching speed**. Passive millisecond impact response is a separate material response and cannot depend on deliberate mode switching.
- Failure/maintenance: soften back to gel when commanded; high-rate self-healing is *not* automatic pane repair.

**Optical state `clear` / `glare` / `dark`** (independent of gel/locked):
- `clear`: maintain low haze and refractive-index match to pressure glazing; UV rejection may persist.
- `glare`: visible attenuation in selected region or direction; near-IR attenuation may be regulated separately.
- `dark`: heavy visible attenuation while maintaining as much spectral control as possible; NOT an impenetrable radiation shield.
- Operations: a Pilot can choose **clear + gel**, **clear + locked**, **glare + locked**, **dark + gel**, etc. Night and high-contrast navigation use either true clear state or instrumentation, not mandatory electrochromic opacity.

**No extra controllers, data schema, HUD mode machinery, fluid physics engine, or new technology-timeline entry required** to represent this as authored ship behaviour.

## Existing mass, pressure, thermal and failure invariants

- Previous physical cavity volume ~3.023m³. At near-water density the continuous gel is **~3.0t** additional mass, not free. The previous two independent pressure domes consume nominal ~7.05t of already *research-level* transparent structural glazing mass. Gel-plus-pressure-glazing **~10.1t before support hardware**; prior ~14.28t exterior protection allocation remains treated as existing exterior mass equivalent, NOT double-booked as new.
- A filled gel cavity is **not an evacuated cavity**. At nominal 50kPa cavity pressure, inner dome sustains ~51.3kPa differential and outer dome ~50kPa; if an outer or inner pane fails, the surviving separate pressure shell must be capable of the FULL 101.3kPa case. The 50kPa setpoint is *illustrative*, not a designed pressure management mechanism.
- If the chosen AOI bulk properties resemble silicone gel assumptions in the earlier screen, **+20K unconstrained thermal swelling can be ~56 litres**. Completely trapping a nearly incompressible fill in an inflexible cavity could create *megapascal-scale* hydraulic pressure. The **minimum required physical accommodation** is a passive thermovolumetric compliance capacity concealed in the perimeter, sized for the specific eventually selected material; **at least ~56L for the old unverified +20K silicone expansion assumption**, PLUS margin. No actual compensation system is designed or approved. The material may eventually be formulated for much smaller expansion, but no measured AOI value exists.
- Under 4g component across the full 7m, water-density fluid has a pressure-gradient scale `rho a L ≈ 274kPa`. Main-axial g across 0.12m produces ~4.7kPa. The true stiffness, shear support at the rim and maneuver orientation matter; do not assume uniform 50kPa is possible under every acceleration vector, or that locked-gel mode evades inertia.
- **Critical interface:** the AOI must not weld, adhesively bond, or crack-bridge the two pressure shells into a mechanically single common-failure assembly. If it does, the backup concept collapses. Non-destructive optically matched **slip/contact interfaces** are a design *condition*, not a qualified hardware detail.
- The prior 21.3kg/0.25m² detached-fragment case in an evacuated gap gave an illustrative ~1.38kJ collision. A filled optogel could reduce displacement, **or instead transmit a destructive fast pressure pulse**. We have NO measured high-rate stress or attenuation law for AOI-2226 and therefore no legitimate fragment impact transmission number, backup survival probability, or failover safety claim. DO NOT invent one.
- Spectral control is not a substitute for radiation shielding, optical-grade transmission testing at high off-normal incidence, or a genuine cabin solar/thermal budget.

## Concluded user-facing concept and disposition

**ARCHIVAL DESIGN CHOICE — ACCEPTED AS NON-CANON WORLDBUILDING RESEARCH:**

> Wayfarer has a seven-metre, almost uninterrupted panoramic bow protected by 37 separately replaceable outer transparent shield panels. Behind those are two independent, seamless pressure domes. Their interspace contains a 2226-developed adaptive optogel: a reversible soft-to-solid-like damping network with independently switchable clear, glare and dark optical states. This makes the canopy feel like a living, controllable material rather than a pile of mechanical shutters. The gel is *not* the pressure boundary, and it does not guarantee backup survival after one dome shatters.

**Decision:** For narrative/design purposes this is the **final candidate** and no more speculative feature accretion is necessary. For scientific/physical status it remains **unqualified** until (a) independent high-rate impulse/fracture response, (b) perimeter thermovolumetric compliance, and (c) failure of one shell without entraining the other are evidenced. Those are **explicit hard limitations**, not an invitation to build a qualification program. The previous evacuated gap stays the comparison reference; adopt AOI only as a 2226 hypothetical materials extrapolation, not demonstrated current technology or retroactive canon.

**STOP.** Retain the 37 outer panels; preserve the experience; stop designing more subassemblies.
