Ideal kinetic power per 25kN unit is ~24.5MW **before** vaporisation, heating and system losses. Tank withdrawal, pressurisation, feed, thruster ramp, cryo interaction, plumes, operating duty, power/heat and mass reserve impacts remain HOLD; do not admit this performance to Rev7 without an actual engineering design.
