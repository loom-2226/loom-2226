## Structure

- **Longerons:** four CFRP tubes, 0.40 m across with an 84.2 mm wall, in an X from x 14 to 34.3. The upper pair edge the bay slot.
  - At 5 g x 1.5, the axial stress plus bending from the thrust-line offset and a 0.5° thrust error is 248 MPa, against a 250 MPa design stress.
  - Buckling margin 22; first bending mode 4.8 Hz; with one longeron lost, the rest carry 39 MPa at 1 g.
  - Joints: titanium end fittings and bay-corner doublers, 3.5 t.
- **Twist** is carried by the tank's closed cell (torsion mode 3.8 Hz).
- **Node mounts** sit on the ring frames, off the strake lines. Placing them against PR #184 is Sol's task.

## Propulsion

- **Torch:** pulsed. Each helium-3-rich, spin-polarised D-He3 pellet is wrapped in a shell of remass that becomes the exhaust. The burn sits 6 m aft of the reactor face.
- **Torch card:** inherited working thrust/flow card, `src/wayfarer_torch_mode_cards.py`, which gives mass flow and exhaust velocity per mode. Jet power is held to 11.36 TW, the power the hot fins and strakes are sized for. Crew doctrine caps sustained thrust at 4 g; 5 g is the structural rating.

| Mode | Exhaust velocity (km/s) | Card thrust (MN) | This ship (MN) | Burn time (not fixed here) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ECON | 3,000 | 3.41 | 3.41 | source-derived |
| CRUISE | 2,000 | 11.36 | 11.36 | source-derived |
| EXPEDITE | 1,000 | 22.72 | 22.72 | source-derived |
| FAST | 700 | 34.08 | 32.46 | source-derived |
| HARD | 450 | 56.81 | 50.49, 4 g cap | source-derived |
| LIMIT | 300 | 85.21 | 75.74, 4 g cap | source-derived |

- **Steering:** the magnetic nozzle steers the thrust line by up to 1°. This is a declared assumption, accepted by kT. It keeps the thrust line through the centre of mass with trim plus an assumed 0.5° thrust error (0.85° needed).
- **Torch heat:** 1.40 GW reaches hot structure and is carried by the hot fins, whose capacity is 1.49 GW. The magnet coils take 0.48 MW, carried by the strakes at 4 m. Light on the reactor's aft face is 5.0 MW/m², against a 10 MW/m² limit. With one fin lost, the torch runs at 88%.
- **Thrust line:** source offset **(y+0.03,z−0.26) m**; maximum trim **0.339702°** in S7 (unchanged **0.5°** limit).
- **RCS:** eight clusters of 25 kN thrusters, four at the bow (x 3) and four aft (x 44).
  - Each cluster has three attitude thrusters (one inward, two tangential) and one axial thruster. The axial thrusters are new in rev 6: the bow ones push aft, the aft ones push forward, and each is canted 10° outward.
  - A 180° pitch or yaw slew takes 35 s (G3). Under main's closed-loop controller, every axis settles within 62 s in every state, and still settles with any one cluster out.
  - Axial translation: about 98 kN each way. Main's test velocities need 15 kN.
- **Metric:** the canon card. Ramps take 10-30 s, the cold load is at most 136 kW (cryoplant 150 kW), and the strakes reject the heat at 2 m. The torch and high Metric are never run together.
- **Loom:** sequence: Metric collapse, NRE relaxation, Loom formation. A direct jump from mid-field Metric is not crew-rated.
  - Locked configuration: shuttle docked, docking collar free, strakes and hot fins stowed, crown gores closed.
  - Arrival remass and velocity matching are mission-solver results; this spec does not assign a fixed post-arrival 295 km/s budget.

## Glazing (about 48 m²)

- **Crown dome over L1 (25 m²).**
  - Flaw-controlled ceramic panes on an Osteon titanium frame: 8 roots forking into 16 blades round a clear 2.3 m centre pane.
  - 16 tambour gores close flush and restore the armour. The gores are open only at low speed or with the bow out of the dust flow. They are closed in storms, radiation belts, torch burns and Loom.
  - The pilots see 99.9% clear across 60° and about 75% of the forward sky.
- **L2 viewport band (19.4 m²)**, round 330° of the common deck. It is 1.6 m tall in the lounge.
  - 23 Osteon titanium mullions between 14 x 4 cm titanium edge rings, with a lounge transom.
  - The rings attach to the shell by flexure fingers.
  - Ceramic plate panes, 16 mm in the general band and 18 mm in the lounge.
- **22 round viewports:**
  - 10 berth viewports, 0.5 m;
  - 4 on the L1 shoulder;
  - 2 medical, 2 storm shelter, 1 suit room, 2 workshop, 1 vestibule.
- **Shielding:** every opening below the crown has an acrylic shield plug matching the armour's g/cm². Berths and shelter keep their screens with the shutters open.
  - Minimum berth screen: 40.38 g/cm² (need 40).
  - Every opening also has a flush Strata shutter.

## Skin (rev 7)

- **Criterion:** low signature (Canon I §9.5, §8.2-8.3), an operational constraint, not invisibility.
- **Habitat armour, x 0-14:** faced in Strata laminate (Builder-derived, MHL-22). No new shielding performance is implied; density, properties and look are open. The viewer's look is a placeholder.
- **Bow, x 0-2.6:** bare Strata, the erosion face.
- **Flanks, x 2.6-34.3:** tunable-emissivity layer (proposed Frontier item F5-TE-01).
  - Normal: emissivity 0.85, solar absorptivity 0.15 (light).
  - Cold running: emissivity 0.10. Heat is held, not removed: about 17 h before 50 t of protected water has warmed 15 K, then the strakes must open or the flanks switch back.
- **Seams (kT ACCEPTED 2026-11-09):** visible hull panel seams at the Option C bow-to-cylinder shoulder joint (x2.35 m), habitat deck frame planes x3.0/5.6/8.2/10.8 m, and service/midship frame planes x14,16,18,20,22,24,26,28,30,32,34 m; longitudinal seam azimuths 40/140/220/320° on strake/longeron lines plus 0/180/270° mid splits. Midship top quarter follows the existing bay-door edges instead of an angular grid. The previous continuous 22.5° **skin seam** grid is superseded, but the distinct 13×16 Metric node-face arrangement (placement HOLD) and 16 crown gore segmentation are unchanged. Shading only; no physical skin cuts or material credit.
- **Metric node faces:** 208 (13 rings x 16), 0.35 m, flush. Each must carry at least the armour's g/cm²; as open bores the berth screen would drop to 14.8 g/cm². Placement HOLD (PR #184): 76 clear, 20 under the stowed strakes, 12 on the bay doors, 80 on the torch chain, 16 facing the dust flow.
