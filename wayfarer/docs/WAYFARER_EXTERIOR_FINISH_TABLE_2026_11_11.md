## kT approval gate

**Approval is needed for the visible palette, surface textures, glTF PBR mapping and glow behaviour.** The source fixes functional material categories, not all visible surface colours. In particular, Strata's current look is explicitly a placeholder. Do not promote any OPEN row by inferring physical colour from the legacy renderer.

Other bounded point requiring explicit acceptance: a 30 mm-deep *physical* seam is impossible on a 20 mm shell without perforation. The staged approach makes a 6 mm blind display cut on the two-centimetre shells and reserves **30 mm apparent depth** for normal/roughness treatment after PBR approval; the thick armour can take the 30 mm drawing groove. No wall safety, loss of shield, tank performance or pressure credit comes from that cosmetic representation.

**Current staged scope:** seam placement, torch surface grooves, selected maintenance outlines, RCS source-vector directions + conservative direction-based zero-net-force torque screen, and shuttle display-skin lines / separate TPS identity. **Not done:** adopted material definitions, PBR export, six-state GLB regeneration, D9 release, canon tag or pointer movement.
