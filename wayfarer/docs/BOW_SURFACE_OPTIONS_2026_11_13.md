ALL bodies. ALL sponsors. 2026-2226.

**Scope:** the bare Strata shoulder between the crown tiles (r 3.5) and the hull
joint (x 2.35, r 4.5). The crown tiles and gores keep their existing approved lines.
The 16 ring-0 node faces stay as they are.

## Claude's recommendation: 1 Clean

The bow is the erosion face, which the skin decision of 26 Oct already describes as
"bare Strata: the erosion face meets the dust flow". A seam facing the flow is where
erosion starts, so leaving the shoulder unbroken is the physically honest choice. It
also gives the dark bow a clear visual break from the tiled pale hull.

Option 2 is the visual alternative, and its lines run along the flow. Option 3
puts a joint across the flow, so it would need a replaceable-plate requirement to
justify it.
