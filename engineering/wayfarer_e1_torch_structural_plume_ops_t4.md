# Wayfarer E1 Torch T4 — Structural / Plume / Operational Integration

**Status:** ENGINEERING INTERFACE / NON-CANON / NON-COMPONENT-CERTIFICATION

T4 binds the already-earned torch performance, thermal/shield/thrust-frame requirement envelope and physical-plume clearance requirement to the E1 vehicle interface. It does not certify unrealized structural, magnetic-nozzle, shielding, RCS, radiator or plume hardware.

## Earned vehicle-facing envelope

The six qualified torch cards are inherited without replacement. Known initial axial thrust ranges from 3,408,301.2075 N in ECON through 85,207,530.1875 N in LIMIT. LIMIT is therefore the governing **known quasi-static axial load** before any unearned structural design factor, dynamic amplification, nozzle side load, fatigue spectrum or local reinforcement factor is applied.

The load-path interface remains the four-main-longeron concept converging into the aft thrust frame and single axial magnetic nozzle. Candidate aft stations remain candidate only: shadow shield x=38–43 m, reactor/thrust frame x=43–50 m, magnetic nozzle x=50–57 m. These coordinates are packaging sensitivity, not certified component geometry.

## Structural holds

T4 does not invent structural design factor, dynamic amplification, side-load fraction, fatigue spectrum, local stress solution, reinforcement design, material allowables or magnet/nozzle structural loads. E1 knows that these are required for component certification and that the known axial envelope must be reacted; it does not pretend they are solved.

## Physical plume and external clearance

No plume half-angle, plume length or clearance margin is selected. The existing physical-plume envelope remains authoritative for the requirement topology: clearance is mode-dependent; RCS and torch plume interaction must be reviewed; radiator deployed and stowed states must clear; plume interception feeds the deposition/thermal budget; detachment and divergence must be physically bounded before component geometry freeze.

A conical plume may be evaluated only as an explicit sensitivity construction. It is not a plasma model and cannot become authority merely because it fits the vehicle.

## RCS / thermal integration

The five existing RCS installation holds are carried unchanged, including physical plume/clearance, working-fluid/thermal load, MIB/feed dynamics and life, vectoring dynamics/life, and mount/load-path/local-structure closure.

Shield, magnet and thermal lifetime; torch deposition partition; transient rejection duty; and physical radiator geometry/torch clearance remain component-certification holds.

## Operating exclusions

Torch operation and high-metric thermal/field operation remain mutually exclusive. Protected water is not normal torch remass. Candidate packaging cannot freeze component geometry. External hardware cannot freeze before physical plume clearance. Transition preconditions remain open pending feed, field, thermal and attitude dynamics; safe shutdown/fault isolation and RCS/torch mutual plume interaction remain open.

## T4 result

T4 closes the E1-facing **integration requirement envelope** while carrying unresolved technology into T5. It does not close detailed thrust-frame design, plume physics, RCS installation, shield/magnet lifetime or thermal hardware certification.
