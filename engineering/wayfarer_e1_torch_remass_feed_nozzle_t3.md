# Wayfarer E1 Torch — Remass / Feed / Nozzle Interface T3

**Status:** WORKING ENGINEERING INTERFACE / NON-CANON / NON-HARDWARE-CERTIFICATION

T3 consolidates the already-earned E1 remass, feed-rate and nozzle requirement work. It does not select a working-fluid species, feed architecture or nozzle efficiency.

## Earned E1 requirements

- normal torch remass inventory: 250,000 kg;
- protected water reserve: 50,000 kg and unavailable to normal torch operation;
- exact mass-flow/exhaust-velocity pairs remain those of the six earned mode cards;
- the resulting feed envelope spans approximately 1.1361004025 kg/s to 284.025100625 kg/s, a 250:1 turndown ratio;
- effective exhaust velocity spans 300 km/s to 3,000 km/s.

These requirements are imported from the shared exact mode-card authority rather than restated as an independent physical truth.

## Working-fluid boundary

Working-fluid identity remains OPEN. Prior research/trade work identifies light hydrogenic or helium-class remass as a useful topology analogue, but that is research direction only and is not a species selection. Water, steam-derived fluids, noble gases and metallic/high-Z fluids likewise remain unselected candidate families.

No storage density, phase, pressure, temperature, tank utilization, tank mass or volume is promoted without an explicit species/state assumption.

## Feed boundary

E1 requires a feed path capable of the earned 1.136-to-284.025 kg/s envelope, but does not require E1 to certify the valve/pump/injector implementation. Open technology holds include response and mode-transition time, pressure/two-phase stability, thermal conditioning and ionization, dynamic range and cycle life, fault isolation and safe shutdown, tankage, and centre-of-mass migration.

## Nozzle boundary

The magnetic-nozzle interface must preserve the earned card exhaust velocity and thrust requirements without pretending that nozzle efficiency or plasma behavior has been solved. Nozzle efficiency, species-dependent energy transfer, magnetization/gyroradius, plasma detachment, divergence, wall/coil interception, erosion, contamination and lifetime remain explicit holds.

A supplied candidate efficiency or feed parameter is sensitivity input only. It does not certify hardware or promote canon.

## E1 closure rule

T3 is closed for E1 when the earned inventory and mode-card feed requirements are represented exactly and every unresolved species/feed/nozzle physical quantity remains explicit. Component certification is downstream.

This preserves the firewall:

**PROTECTED 50 t WATER RESERVE IS NOT NORMAL TORCH REMASS.**

T4 consumes this bounded interface for structural, plume and operational integration.
