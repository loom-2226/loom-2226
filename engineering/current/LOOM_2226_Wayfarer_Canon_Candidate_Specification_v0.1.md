## 2. Vehicle identity and role

### CURRENT CANON / ESTABLISHED BASELINE

Wayfarer is a mature 2226 courier-type spacecraft built around three distinct mobility regimes:

- torch propulsion for mass movement, local maneuver and major in-system propulsion;
- metric operation for high-value / urgency-dominant long-baseline Solar travel;
- Loom translation for sparse interstellar/frontier movement.

The mothership is a vacuum spacecraft. It is not intended to perform routine atmospheric entry or planetary landing. Planetary descent/ascent is handled by a dedicated launch/shuttle carried aboard.

Normal crew: **6**.

Payload target: approximately **50 t**.

## 4. Structural grammar

### CURRENT CANON

Wayfarer uses:

- exactly **four major working-fluid/remass tanks**;
- exactly **four principal axial longerons**;
- exactly **four major radiator assemblies** arranged in quadrature;
- exactly **one primary axial fusion-torch / magnetic-nozzle system**.

### CANDIDATE

The preferred cross-sectional grammar is:

- four tanks centered approximately at 45°, 135°, 225° and 315° around the thrust axis;
- four principal longerons on the cardinal axes at 0°, 90°, 180° and 270°;
- central technical/service core on the thrust axis.

The purpose is not aesthetic symmetry but alternating structural and fluid-service sectors around a central engineering axis.

Current trial dimensions:

- tank external diameter approximately **3.0 m**;
- tank-center radius approximately **2.7 m**;
- longeron radial station approximately **2.45 m**;
- central technical-core diameter approximately **1.4 m**.

These dimensions remain candidates rather than canon locks.

## 16. Exterior form and visual engineering constraints

### CANDIDATE PRINCIPLES

Wayfarer should read as a purpose-built hard-SF vacuum courier rather than a naval ship or fighter.

Preferred design language:

- blunt forward armored crew body;
- off-white ceramic/composite external armor;
- small meaningful windows distributed by inhabited deck function;
- dense industrial midship structural/thermal systems;
- visible fourfold structural grammar where appropriate;
- semi-recessed integrated launch bay;
- one dominant aft torch/nozzle;
- dark refractory radiator surfaces;
- relational hardware that looks like instrumentation/cryogenics/field engineering.

Explicitly avoid:

- atmospheric wings on the mothership;
- mothership landing gear;
- artificial-gravity ring;
- bridge canopy;
- decorative fins;
- multi-engine fighter clustering;
- ship-of-the-line/naval silhouette assumptions;
- ungrounded weapons fit.

## 17. Recommended items to promote now or soon

The following appear mature enough for serious canon review:

1. Broad axial packaging regions (habitat/service/tanks-plant/thermal/shield/reactor/nozzle), while preserving internal freedom.
2. Alternating four-tank / four-longeron cross-sectional grammar.
3. Pressure-vessel versus external armor distinction.
4. Integrated bay on +Z and standard docking side on -Z as a stable ship coordinate convention.
5. Broad launch envelope and role, though not detailed launch aerodynamics.
6. Relational plant centered near the mass center rather than isolated fore/aft.
7. Four radiator roots structurally tied to the four-longeron grammar, while keeping physical panel geometry open.
8. One aft thrust-frame convergence into one axial nozzle.
9. Shared configuration-state vocabulary.
10. Explicit statement that metric radiator equivalent area is not physical panel area.
11. Explicit statement that configuration changes can invalidate/retrigger field/trajectory certification.

## 19. Review standard before promotion

Before moving any CANDIDATE item into governing canon, require at least:

- no contradiction with CANON II v2.4 or later governing baseline;
- mass compatibility;
- structural plausibility;
- launch-bay and docking clearance plausibility;
- radiator deployment/interference plausibility where relevant;
- compatibility with the geometry authority and runtime configuration model;
- no accidental inheritance from legacy/archive values that conflict with the current baseline.

The deterministic Pixel/Blender geometry system should be used as the proving ground, not as automatic canon authority.
