# E1 Geometric Admissibility Relevance Audit v0.1

## Parent problem

Determine where Wayfarer can safely transition from metric displacement back to ordinary-space flight near Neptune without mistaking an operational seam, conventional orbital boundary, or merely measurable environmental quantity for a fundamental physics boundary.

## Canon basis

Foundational Canon v1.5 §6.11 states that translation viability depends on the geometry of the entire domain and endpoint state, including curvature, curvature gradients/tidal shear, stress-energy/matter density, electromagnetic conditions, causal structure, domain size, Loom coherence, lattice coherence, and uncertainty.

The canon names these families. It does **not** assign them equal weight, define a numeric score, or provide a threshold law.

## Audit rule

Engineering priority is based on what can advance the parent problem without inventing a coupling law or measurement model.

### Next engineering

- local curvature at the earned Neptune collapse endpoint;
- local curvature gradients / tidal shear at that endpoint.

These terms are directly geometric and can be approached from already-qualified ordinary gravitational inputs without first inventing new exotic-physics coupling semantics.

### After local geometry

- stress-energy / local matter density;
- causal structure.

These remain relevant, but should follow the first local-geometry calculation so the work stays ordered around the parent question.

### Hold until coupling is earned

- electromagnetic conditions.

Historical Voyager magnetic/plasma data can be ingested correctly, but more source data does not establish how electromagnetic conditions affect metric collapse. Data availability is not physics relevance.

### Physics binding required / physics blocked

- operational domain-size binding;
- Loom coherence;
- lattice coherence.

These may ultimately be central, but no qualified observable or endpoint rule currently exists. Engineering must not fabricate one.

### Continuous decision gate

- uncertainty.

Uncertainty is not another environmental force. It must travel with each qualified driver and govern whether an endpoint decision is safe to use.

## Context-only quantities

Hill radius, Laplace sphere of influence, Neptune GM, and Neptune mean radius remain useful ordinary-spacetime context. None has direct Geometric Admissibility boundary authority. GM and radius are inputs to local geometry, not thresholds themselves.
