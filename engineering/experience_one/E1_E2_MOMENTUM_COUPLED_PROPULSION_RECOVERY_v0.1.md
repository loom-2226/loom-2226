## Electrostatic-asymmetrical-drive ancestry

Kevin's recovered design recollection points specifically toward the family of **electrostatic / asymmetrical-capacitor propulsion** as a useful historical and architectural inspiration.

NASA/MSFC and associated researchers tested asymmetrical capacitor thrusters in the early 2000s. Those experiments are legitimate external research ancestry, but they do **not** establish a vacuum spacecraft drive:

- measured thrust was consistent with charged-ion momentum transfer through a surrounding gas;
- published testing reported no measured force in vacuum / hard vacuum;
- some proposed space-application configurations explicitly supplied gas around the capacitor.

Therefore E1 must not claim that NASA demonstrated reactionless or vacuum asymmetrical-capacitor propulsion.

The useful inheritance is narrower: high-voltage asymmetric field geometry is a candidate architectural clue for a future momentum-coupled system, subject to an explicit momentum partner and full conservation closure.
