# Wayfarer E1 — Metric Canon, Vehicle Interface and GLB Integration Specification v0.1

**Class:** ENGINEERING / PROPOSED E1 SPECIFICATION; NOT GOVERNING CANON UNTIL PROMOTED. **Dependency:** governing Canon II v2.4, schematic v2.4a, frozen Frontier and E1 torch/RCS interfaces. **Disposition proposed:** E1 metric interface closed with physics-validation and component-realization holds. This document does not certify a physical drive, approve environmental coefficients, or amend machine constants.

## 1. Canon promotion boundary

Promote by reference, not by rewriting physics: unified ~88 t Metric/Loom plant; 10.0 kg reusable Mc-299m, 100 x 100 g active tiles, 208 distributed boundary/metric nodes, 2 GJ reversible shared field bank, 150 kW-class 20 K cryoplant, 900 K reject interface. Metric law beta_machine = chi_M J_M/N_eff, measured chi_M reference 1.00, lot acceptance 0.95–1.05 and fitted sigma <=0.01 remain governing physics. Certified clean-environment cards: NORMAL beta .268, 10 s ramp, 20 kW cold, 3.5 MW cryo electrical, 106 m2 equivalent; FAST .437, 12 s, 38.1 kW, 6.7 MW, 202 m2; EXPEDITE .519, 15 s, 63.5 kW, 11.2 MW, 336 m2; HARD .595, 30 s, 136 kW, 23.9 MW, 719 m2. Saturation ~.678c uncertified; FTL BLACK. 719 m2 is an equivalent area, not installed panel geometry. No change to 57 m x 9 m vehicle envelope, four major radiators, frozen torch or RCS hardpoints. Historical 32 kg/64-cell/90 t Loom + 60 t metric/PJ bank design is retired; never blend it with this interface.

## 2. E1 load and mass boundary

The 88 t is the unified plant, not a second additive metric package. The 10 kg Mc inventory is contained in the 100 tiles, not an additional 10 kg. Mass allocations must reconcile to the frozen vehicle dry/wet ledger; installed node/plant/cryoplant/PMAD/radiator structural masses may not be independently added without identifying the parent mass bucket and avoiding double count. A schematic envelope is not a component mass certification. The 2 GJ bank is a reversible formation/control/abort buffer, not propulsion fuel, free work, or a momentum source. Reserve fraction, state-of-charge thresholds, discharge profile and recharge duty are not certified by a bare 2 GJ capacity: keep them unresolved until derived from mission load-time profiles.

## 3. Power and thermal ledger

For each clean card, preserve the canon delivered cryogenic electrical requirement. Scenario support calculation: P_upstream = P_delivered/(eta_conversion eta_PMAD), P_distribution_heat = P_upstream-P_delivered. Show both separately and pin Frontier producer version/hash. Do not add distribution heat blindly to canon 900 K equivalent radiator area: source location, heat-rejection interface, existing canonical inclusion and duty/overlap must be established to avoid double counting. Report calculated distribution-area equivalent as a *conditional additional support requirement*, not installed geometry. P_corr(environment), node switching losses, wake heat, standby loads, source output and total radiator requirement remain UNKNOWN until calibrated. Zero is not a permissible substitute for unknown. 150 kW-class plant is not an assertion that all operating modes draw 150 kW cold. Four radiator assemblies remain fixed; physical area, view factor, mass, stow/deploy and interference are unresolved.

## 4. Operating state contract

Represent at least TORCH_ACTIVE, TORCH_SHUTDOWN, RELATIONAL_READY, METRIC_RAMP, METRIC_CRUISE, METRIC_COLLAPSE, NRE_RELAX_REPHASE, LOOM_FORMATION and HOLD/DERATE/REORIENT/ABORT. Transitions are requests gated by authority, not automatic success. High metric and major torch are mutually exclusive thermal/field states. METRIC_COLLAPSE -> NRE_RELAX_REPHASE -> LOOM_FORMATION is the permitted conceptual sequence; direct mid-field Metric-to-Loom is not crew-rated. Metric acquisition requires current applicable signed/non-revoked environment certification, correct lot/ship/mode/ramp applicability, calibrated U^M record, node topology/array health, coupled thermal/cryogenic and conservation channels and no hard veto. Missing/stale/uncertain certification => HOLD, DERATE/REORIENT or COLLAPSE, never proceed by default. Bank abort availability is a condition to establish, not inferred from capacity. No E1 state machine may claim these unresolved inputs PASS. Torch and RCS frozen interfaces remain untouched.

## 5. Conservation, navigation and physics holds

Metric U^M_gamma is path-dependent ordinary-state propagation, distinct from Loom T_AB; arrival ordinary-state mismatch remains explicit and is paid by torch, tug, infrastructure or other exchange. Wake is not an independent reactionless thruster. Local total stress-energy conservation and finite ordinary/relational/bank/radiation/environment ledgers remain governing. Full finite time-dependent 4D RSET benchmark, environmental correction power/thermal calibration, U^M calibration, wake/finite ledger, and topology/failure calibration remain authorial physics-validation holds; no immediate sub-c adiabatic hard kill found does not equal completed RSET proof. In-world 2226 crew-rated operation is canon and is not revoked by these open authorial gates.

## 6. GLB representation contract (non-governing candidate packaging)

Keep binary GLB outside Git. The GLB deliverable must carry a text manifest/inspection report with generator version, source model hash, governing spec commit SHA, output GLB SHA-256, coordinate frame and units, semantic hierarchy, counts, bounds, mass-bucket references, authority tags, and validation results. A rendered screenshot is not validation.

Required inspectable hierarchy: `WAYFARER/METRIC_RELATIONAL_PLANT` with identifiable `PLANT_ENVELOPE`, `ACTIVE_TILES` (100 instances, tile inventory total 10 kg), `BOUNDARY_METRIC_NODES` (208 indexed instances), `SHARED_FIELD_BANK`, `CRYOPLANT_20K`, `PMAD_INTERFACE`, `THERMAL_MANIFOLD`, `SERVICE_ROUTING_CANDIDATE` and `STATE_VISUALIZATION`. A common relational plant must not be duplicated as separate Metric and Loom machinery. The 100 and 208 instance counts are semantic requirements, not permission to choose authoritative exact positions. Each instance and assembly carries `authority=CANON_QUANTITY_CANDIDATE_PLACEMENT` where appropriate; exact physical positions, node topology, cable/pipe routing, dimensions and local structural reinforcement remain non-governing until certified. A placeholder node must not imply its visual size or spacing is canonical. No gratuitous emission/glow implies propulsion physics.

Coordinate contract: metres, +X aft from bow (x=0 to 57), vehicle axial centerline, canonical nominal body diameter 9 m; identify any known non-body appendage or bay blister separately rather than silently enforcing a 9 m global bounding sphere. Preserve four radiator assemblies and all frozen RCS hardpoints; inspect AFT x44.5 m r4.5 m and launch extraction +Z wedge. Show metric node/radiator/root/torch shield and aft RCS clearances as measured geometry, or record `UNRESOLVED` with collision/clearance hold. Never claim PASS without full swept/deployed/stowed checks. The candidate plant x18–34 m envelope is not canon; use it as a clearly labelled starting packaging hypothesis, with four tank sectors, structural longerons and launch bay accounted for.

Display states must make TORCH_ACTIVE, METRIC_ACTIVE and TRANSITION/HOLD distinguishable without implying simultaneous incompatible operation. State display is visualization only and has no execution authority.

## 7. Acceptance and freeze gates

**Specification gate:** source/version provenance; exact canon values and retired-value rejection; 88 t/10 kg no-double-count assertions; Frontier power accounting with unknown correction and no radiator double count; transition guards and failure defaults; frozen torch/RCS invariants; physics-vs-hardware holds separated; tests and GitHub Gate PASS; exact-head Pixel receipt before merge if required by governed qualification. Only then may E1 interface freeze be asserted.

**GLB gate, after spec freeze:** locate actual current source generator and GLB; inspect source/manifest rather than guessing; generate non-Git binary; verify 100 tile and 208 node semantic counts, hierarchy, units, axis, hull/bay bounds, four radiators, frozen RCS points, plant mass-bucket reference, visual operating states, and interference report. Geometry with unresolved clearances is candidate visualization, not final certified installation. Freeze GLB only after actual artifact hash, viewer inspection and validation receipt. If source GLB or generator is unavailable, record BLOCKED_SOURCE_ARTIFACT rather than fabricating completion.

**Reopen rule:** only a demonstrated contradiction or changed frozen interface reopens E1; better future Frontier support technology alone does not.
