## Governing finding

For E1, `domain_size` is **not** a Neptune-centered exclusion radius.

The qualified binding rule is:

`CERTIFIED_TRANSLATION_DOMAIN_IS_VESSEL_CONFIGURATION_BOUND_NOT_CELESTIAL_EXCLUSION_RADIUS`

Therefore the spatial region that eventually must satisfy Compound GA is the governed translation-domain geometry associated with the committed vessel configuration, not a familiar celestial-mechanics radius around the destination body.
