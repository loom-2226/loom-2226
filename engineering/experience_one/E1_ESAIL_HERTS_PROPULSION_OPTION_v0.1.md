## HERTS retained only as future option

NASA HERTS/electric-sail concepts exchange momentum with solar-wind plasma through long charged tethers and can operate without carried propellant for the propulsion interaction. That makes them scientifically useful as a control/example of **no carried reaction mass with an explicit external momentum partner**.

They are not the E2 mechanism and shall not be used as a substitute for resolving E2.

No HERTS tether geometry, deployment state, steering cone, power system, mass model, Navigator mode, HUD mode, or Wayfarer packaging requirement is active for E1.
