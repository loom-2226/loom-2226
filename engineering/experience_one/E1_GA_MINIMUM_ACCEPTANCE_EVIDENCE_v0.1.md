**Class:** ENGINEERING QUALIFICATION SUPPORT  
**Date:** 14 September 2026  
**Scope:** E1 Neptune only  
**Status:** CANDIDATE / NON-RUNTIME-POLICY

## Purpose

Define the minimum additional evidence required to move the qualified Compound Geometric Admissibility decision rule from `INDETERMINATE_NOT_CERTIFIABLE` toward a certifiable E1 Neptune outcome without inventing universal thresholds, weights, or cross-axis compensation.

## Governing compression

Five axes still require additional acceptance evidence, but they do not require five independent new physics programs. The minimum evidence can be organized into two governed packages while preserving separate axis dispositions.

### Package A — endpoint/domain physical compatibility

Supports:
- local geometry;
- causal structure;
- domain size.

Must identify the committed vessel configuration, provide the translation-domain geometry or governed certification envelope, carry the qualified local-geometry result across that domain, carry a governed causal-compatibility result or explicitly qualified equivalent invariant, and preserve uncertainty/provenance.

This package does **not** merge the three axes and cannot convert an unresolved axis to satisfied merely because the other two are satisfied.

### Package B — route/vessel coherence

Supports:
- Loom coherence;
- lattice coherence.

Must identify the direction-specific route solution and committed vessel configuration, preserve route evidence/destination state/momentum mapping, route burden and uncertainty, formation/integrity state, hardware state, thermal/bank margins, and certification disposition.

The route and lattice axes remain distinct. A good route cannot compensate for an invalid vessel formation state, and excellent formation cannot create relational access that is not supported by route evidence.

## Already satisfied axis

Local stress-energy remains `SATISFIED_NON_MATERIAL` under its qualified standard-GR materiality screen. No additional acceptance evidence is required for that axis at this stage.

## Authority boundary

This qualification defines the evidence contract only. It does not:
- certify the current endpoint;
- bind Navigator/runtime policy;
- move the endpoint;
- define a scalar GA score;
- define weights;
- promote RF research to engineering authority;
- allow package-level shortcuts around axis-level evidence.
